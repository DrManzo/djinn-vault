## Planning suggestions

- Treat convention and festival weeks as opportunities for carry gear, organizers, repair kits, display stands, and table accessories rather than pure decorations.
- Treat religious and cultural observances respectfully by focusing on hosting, storage, candle safety, meal service, reading stands, and floral/altar support pieces.
- Treat cannabis-related weeks as chances to explore storage, smell-control accessories, tray systems, jar risers, humidity-pack holders, and event carry kits.
- Reuse mechanical cores across themes: tray bodies, clip systems, hook rails, organizer grids, stand bases, and insert systems can all be re-skinned week by week.
- Strong recurring product families for the whole year: candle systems, desk organizers, pet accessories, gaming stands, con utility kits, table-service tools, and modular display risers.
