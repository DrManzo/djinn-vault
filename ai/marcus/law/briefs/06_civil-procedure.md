## Cover
- Jurisdiction types: subject matter (federal question, diversity), personal (minimum contacts), venue
- Pleading standards: Twombly/Iqbal plausibility standard — why this matters for complaints
- Pre-trial motions: 12(b)(6) motion to dismiss, motion for summary judgment (Rule 56)
- Discovery: interrogatories, depositions, requests for production, Rule 26 initial disclosures
- Joinder: compulsory/permissive counterclaims, cross-claims, third-party claims, class actions (Rule 23)
- Erie doctrine: when federal courts apply state substantive law, federal procedural law
- Trial: jury selection (voir dire), burden of proof (preponderance vs clear and convincing)
- Appeals: final judgment rule, interlocutory appeals, standard of review
- Res judicata and collateral estoppel: claim and issue preclusion
