## Cover
- Sole proprietorship: zero setup cost, full personal liability, self-employment tax on all profit
- General partnership: default when two people do business together, joint and several liability
- LLC: most flexible, pass-through tax, limited liability — when it beats a corporation
- S-Corporation: salary + distribution split to reduce SE tax, restrictions (100 shareholders, US citizens only, one class of stock)
- C-Corporation: double taxation, but needed for VC funding, stock options, IPO path
- Nonprofit (501c3): mission-driven, no income tax, restrictions on profit distribution
- Decision matrix: solo freelancer → LLC; small business with partners → LLC or S-corp; raising venture capital → C-corp; California-specific considerations ($800 CA LLC tax vs CA corp tax)
- Tax comparison table: sole prop vs LLC vs S-corp vs C-corp — effective tax rates at various income levels
