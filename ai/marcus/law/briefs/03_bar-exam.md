## Cover
- UBE vs state-specific bars: which states use UBE (40+), portability of UBE scores between states
- MBE: 200 questions, 7 subjects (Civil Procedure, Constitutional Law, Contracts, Criminal Law, Evidence, Real Property, Torts)
- MEE (Multistate Essay Exam): 6 essays, 30 min each, additional subjects
- MPT: 2 tasks, lawyering skills (memo, brief, letter from given materials)
- California bar: not UBE, separate essays + performance tests + California-specific subjects
- Pass rates by state (CA ~40%, national average ~58%)
- Timeline: graduation → 2-3 months intensive prep → exam (Feb or July)
- Top prep courses: BARBRI, Themis, Kaplan — cost comparison, what's included
- First-time vs repeat takers: strategies differ significantly
- Character and fitness: criminal history, financial issues, mental health disclosures
