- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_se_legal_representation_in_the_United_States — self-representation

## Cover
- Situations where you absolutely need a lawyer: criminal charges, custody/divorce, serious injury, business disputes over $10k, IRS audit, immigration
- Situations where you can DIY with good research: small claims (<$10k), simple contracts, LLC formation, minor traffic tickets
- How to find a lawyer: ABA referral service, state bar referral, Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell, personal referrals
- How to evaluate a lawyer: specialization, bar standing (check state bar website), fee structure, initial consultation
- Fee structures: hourly ($200–$600/hr), flat fee (simple matters), contingency (personal injury, 33%), retainer
- Free and low-cost options: legal aid organizations (income-based), law school clinics, limited scope representation, unbundled legal services
- Attorney-client privilege: what's protected, what isn't, joint clients
- When to fire your lawyer: unresponsive, conflicts of interest, billing disputes — how to do it cleanly
- Self-representation (pro se): small claims court strategy, form courts (landlord-tenant, traffic), when it's viable
