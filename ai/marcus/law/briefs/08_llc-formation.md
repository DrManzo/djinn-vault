## Cover
- What an LLC is: limited liability protection + pass-through taxation — why it's the most popular structure
- Formation steps: choose state, name search, file Articles of Organization, get EIN, operating agreement
- Operating agreement: what it must include (member roles, profit distribution, dissolution process)
- Single-member vs multi-member: different tax treatment, self-employment tax implications
- Tax options: default (disregarded entity/partnership), S-corp election (IRS Form 2553), C-corp election
- California specifics: $800/year minimum franchise tax regardless of income, CA LLC fee schedule by gross receipts
- Registered agent requirement: who qualifies, services (Northwest, ZenBusiness, etc.)
- Cost breakdown: state filing fees ($50–$500 by state), registered agent (~$50–$300/yr), legal fees
- Common mistakes: not funding the LLC properly, commingling personal/business funds (veil piercing risk), missing annual filings
