## Cover
- Core CBT model: thoughts → emotions → behaviors — the triangle, how they reinforce each other
- All 15 cognitive distortions: all-or-nothing thinking, overgeneralization, mental filter, disqualifying the positive, jumping to conclusions (mind reading + fortune telling), magnification/minimization, emotional reasoning, should statements, labeling, personalization — each with concrete example
- ABC model: Activating event → Beliefs → Consequences — how to use it
- Thought records: situation, automatic thought, emotion, evidence for/against, balanced thought
- Behavioral activation: scheduling pleasant activities, activity-mood monitoring for depression
- Exposure therapy: gradual exposure hierarchy for anxiety/phobia
- Evidence base: CBT has more clinical trial support than any other therapy modality — specific conditions where it's most effective (depression, anxiety, OCD, PTSD, eating disorders)
- Self-directed CBT: can you do it without a therapist? Apps (Woebot, Moodfit), workbooks (Mind Over Mood)
