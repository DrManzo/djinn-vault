## Cover
- Goleman's 5-component model: self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills — each defined with behavioral indicators
- Self-awareness: recognizing your emotions in real time, emotional triggers, impact on others
- Self-regulation: impulse control, managing disruptive emotions, adaptability, trustworthiness
- Motivation: achievement drive beyond money/status, optimism in the face of failure, organizational commitment
- Empathy: understanding others' emotions (not the same as sympathy), organizational awareness, service orientation
- Social skills: influence without authority, conflict management, building bonds, teamwork
- EQ vs IQ: EQ accounts for 67% of leadership effectiveness in one meta-analysis, IQ is a threshold variable
- Measuring EQ: Mayer-Salovey-Caruso model (ability-based), Bar-On model (mixed), limitations of self-report
- Developing EQ: feedback, mindfulness practice, journaling, therapy, mentoring — what actually moves the needle
