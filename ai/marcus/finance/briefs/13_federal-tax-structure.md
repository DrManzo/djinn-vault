## Cover
- 2025/2026 federal tax brackets: exact rates and income ranges for single, married filing jointly, head of household
- How marginal rates work: only income IN each bracket is taxed at that rate — the misconception explained with example
- Gross income vs AGI vs taxable income: the three income figures and how they relate
- Standard deduction 2025: $14,600 single, $29,200 MFJ (get current figures)
- When to itemize: mortgage interest, state/local taxes (SALT $10k cap), charitable deductions, medical expenses (>7.5% AGI)
- Above-the-line deductions (adjustments to income): student loan interest, HSA contributions, self-employed health insurance, IRA contributions — these reduce AGI regardless of standard vs itemized
- MAGI (Modified Adjusted Gross Income): used for IRA eligibility, ACA subsidies, Medicare surcharges
- W-2 vs 1099 vs K-1: different income types and their tax treatment
- Effective vs marginal tax rate: how to calculate your actual total tax rate
