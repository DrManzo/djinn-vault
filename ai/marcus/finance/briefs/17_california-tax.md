## Cover
- CA tax brackets 2025: 1% → 13.3% (10 brackets). Get exact thresholds from FTB. Note: CA has the highest top marginal rate in the US
- CA does NOT conform to all federal deductions — detail key differences (no SALT cap at state level, no standard deduction parity, no QBI deduction)
- CA SDI (State Disability Insurance): 1.1% of wages 2025, no wage cap, employer withholds
- CA mental health services tax: 1% surtax on income over $1 million
- CA estimated payments: required if you expect to owe >$500 (single) or >$1,000 (joint), due same schedule as federal
- CA self-employment: same federal SE income but CA taxes the net profit at CA rates on top; no SE tax deduction at state level
- CA vs federal retirement contribution differences: 401k/IRA contributions reduce federal but NOT CA taxable income in same way — detail carefully
- Residency rules: CA aggressively pursues domicile claims — safe harbor requires 546+ days outside state over 2-year period
- Key CA credits: Renter's Credit ($60/$120), Child/Dependent Care, Senior Head of Household, Young Child Tax Credit

⚠️ Educational only. Not tax advice. CA tax law changes frequently — verify with FTB.
