## Cover
- What an option is: right (not obligation) to buy/sell 100 shares at strike price before expiration
- Calls vs puts: call = right to buy (bullish), put = right to sell (bearish)
- Key terms: strike price, expiration date, premium, in/out/at-the-money
- Time decay (theta): options lose value daily as expiration approaches — why selling options has an edge
- The Greeks:
  - Delta: how much option price changes per $1 stock move (0-1 for calls)
  - Theta: daily time decay (options seller's friend)
  - Vega: sensitivity to volatility — why options get expensive before earnings
  - Gamma: rate of delta change
- Covered calls: own 100 shares, sell a call against them. Income strategy, reduces cost basis. Max profit = strike - current price + premium
- Cash-secured puts: sell a put, keep cash to buy if assigned. Income strategy or way to buy stock at discount
- Approval levels: brokers require approval based on experience/net worth. Level 1 (covered calls) → Level 4 (naked options)
- Risk profile: buying options = defined risk (premium paid), unlimited upside. Selling naked options = unlimited risk

⚠️ Educational only. Not investment advice. Options trading involves significant risk of loss. Verify broker approval requirements.
