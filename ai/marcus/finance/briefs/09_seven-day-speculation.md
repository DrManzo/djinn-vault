## Cover
- What a catalyst is: earnings, Fed meetings, CPI/PPI releases, product launches, FDA approvals — each moves specific sectors
- Pre-market analysis: how to check overnight futures, pre-market movers, gap-up/down setups
- Options flow as a leading indicator: what unusualwhales.com and similar services track, what large unusual options activity signals
- Sector rotation signals: defensive sectors (utilities, consumer staples) lead in downturns, growth (tech, discretionary) lead in expansions
- Position sizing for speculation: never risk more than 1-2% of portfolio per speculative trade
- Stop-loss discipline: set it before you enter, don't move it down, 10-15% below entry for stocks
- Risk/reward minimum: only enter if potential reward is at least 2x the risk (1:2 R/R minimum, 1:3 preferred)
- Pattern recognition: earnings play setups, IV crush after earnings, gap fill probabilities
- The psychological traps: revenge trading, FOMO entries, averaging down on losers

⚠️ Educational only. Speculation involves substantial risk of loss. Most short-term traders lose money. Not investment advice.
