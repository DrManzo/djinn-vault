## Cover
- FICO score components: Payment history 35% (most important), Amounts owed 30% (utilization), Length of history 15%, Credit mix 10%, New credit 10% — what actually moves each
- Score ranges: 300–579 Poor, 580–669 Fair, 670–739 Good, 740–799 Very Good, 800–850 Exceptional
- Credit utilization: keep under 30%, ideally under 10% for best scores. Per-card utilization matters too
- Building credit from zero: secured credit card, credit-builder loan, becoming authorized user, student cards
- Repairing bad credit: dispute errors (free, go directly to bureaus), negotiate pay-for-delete, goodwill letter for late payments, time — negative items fall off after 7 years (bankruptcy 10 years)
- Credit cards as tools: full balance pay-off monthly eliminates interest, rewards maximization, authorized user strategy to build someone else's credit
- Hard vs soft inquiries: hard (loan application) drops score 5-10 points temporarily; soft (checking your own) has no effect
- FICO vs VantageScore: both used, FICO is primary for lending decisions
