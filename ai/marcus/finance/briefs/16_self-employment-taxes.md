## Cover
- Self-employment tax rate: 15.3% on net self-employment income (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare) — covers both employee and employer share. Above $160,200, Social Security portion stops
- Deduction for half of SE tax: you get to deduct 50% of SE tax from gross income (above-the-line)
- Quarterly estimated payments (Form 1040-ES): due April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15. Safe harbor: pay 100% of prior year tax (110% if income >$150k) or 90% of current year estimate
- Schedule C deductions: home office (regular and exclusive use, simplified method = $5/sq ft up to 300 sq ft), vehicle (actual expenses vs standard mileage rate — get current IRS rate), equipment (Section 179 immediate expensing), software and subscriptions, professional development, health insurance premium (100% deductible above the line)
- QBI deduction (Section 199A): 20% deduction on qualified business income for pass-through entities. Phase-out above $182,050 single for service businesses. Not available in C-corps
- Self-employed retirement accounts: SEP-IRA, Solo 401k, SIMPLE IRA — contribution limits and calculation method
- Record-keeping: retain all receipts, mileage log, home office measurements
