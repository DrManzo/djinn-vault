# standing freezer door seal

A standing freezer door seal is the rubber gasket around the door that keeps cold air in and moisture out. If it is dirty, warped, torn, or no longer magnetic, the freezer can frost up, run longer, and waste energy.

What to check

Clean the gasket and the freezer frame first, since dirt or ice can stop a good seal.
facebook

Close the door on a thin piece of paper or a dollar bill; if it pulls out easily at multiple spots, the seal may be weak.

Look for cracks, flattening, gaps, or a door that no longer sits flush.

If the gasket is just stiff or slightly misshapen, warming it with hot water or a hair dryer can sometimes help it conform again.
facebook

Repair options

If the gasket is intact but not sealing well, cleaning and reshaping may solve it.
facebook

If it is torn, brittle, or permanently deformed, replacement is the proper fix.

Why it matters

A bad seal lets warm air in and cold air out, which can cause frost buildup and make the freezer work harder. That can also lead to food temperature problems and higher electricity use.
