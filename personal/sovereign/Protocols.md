# Protocols

Five modules. Each states: Purpose, Hard rules, the single Daily action tracked (binary, one per domain — no sub-checklists), Weekly anchor, and Failure recovery. This is the page you actually open daily.

## Body

**Purpose:** Physical standard as proof of command, not a self-improvement side project. Runs on a minimum-viable standard, not an ideal plan — vague aspiration to "become more physical" is too easy to romanticize and too easy to skip.

**Hard rules:**
- Training days and windows are fixed in advance, set with the trainer/nutritionist — never "whenever there's time."
- If it's the training window, the first ten minutes start without negotiation. If the full session is genuinely impossible, the minimum-viable version happens immediately instead — never zero.
- Programming (sets, reps, nutrition targets) is the trainer/nutritionist's call, not something to freelance given the weight-cycling and metabolic history already on record. Sovereign tracks adherence, not prescription.
- Weight and health entries go in `personal/health.md` — don't duplicate a tracking system here.

**Daily action (binary):** Completed today's assigned body proof — full session, minimum-viable session, or scheduled recovery/mobility day. Yes/no.

**Weekly anchor:** The training schedule itself — primary training days, one full rest day, one mobility/recovery day, set during the weekly review.

**Failure recovery:** A missed day doesn't get made up by doubling the next one. It gets one line in the evening command mark: what blocked it, what the if-then plan is next time it's blocked the same way.

## Work & Selective Output

**Purpose:** Protect against two failure modes at once — overproduction for its own sake, and "selectivity" as an excuse for producing nothing. The pivot away from volume toward taste, generosity, and exclusivity does not suspend the requirement to actually make things.

**Hard rules:**
- Every workday gets exactly one decisive move, chosen from four mutually exclusive options: **Make** (one meaningful creation block), **Refine** (improve one existing asset), **Publish** (release one selected artifact), **Position** (one deliberate act that advances placement, reach, or collaboration).
- Every week ends with at least one visible or released output of your own — endorsing or promoting someone else's work never substitutes for this. A week that produces only curation and no output of your own failed this module, regardless of how much taste was on display.
- Define the week's highest-leverage move in advance during the weekly review, then each day executes toward it or explicitly logs why it degraded to a smaller version.

**Daily action (binary):** Made today's decisive move — yes/no, and which of the four.

**Weekly anchor:** One named decisive output or advancement target for the week.

**Failure recovery:** Three days without a Make/Publish action in a row is the trigger to name the obstacle explicitly in the evening mark, not just note the miss.

## Social Field

**Purpose:** Direct correction to edge-dwelling — watching and testing without full engagement, observed consistently across relationships, investing, and professional communication style. This module has a split rule, because the failure mode isn't the same in every context.

**Hard rules — the split:**
- **External / strategic contexts** (negotiation, reputation-building, business positioning): measured disclosure, selective revelation, composure. This is where restraint-based frameworks (48 Laws, Gracián, Machiavelli) actually apply — see [[Canon]] for the explicit "external strategy only" tags.
- **Close relationships:** the opposite default — mandatory unforced self-disclosure. A preference, an inner state, an authored opinion, an invitation — not merely a reactive or calibrated response. Strategic opacity does not belong in intimacy or trusted bonds; using it there is edge-dwelling wearing a strategy costume.
- The standard to hold yourself to: a relationship where you exist with little to no translation is the target state, not the exception to explain away.

**Daily action (binary):** Either "initiated one direct social move" or "made one unforced personal disclosure in a close interaction" — pick whichever the day's actual context calls for.

**Weekly anchor:** One relational emphasis for the week — initiate more, or disclose more directly — named explicitly during the weekly review.

**Failure recovery:** If a week's log is all reactive (responding, calibrating, managing others' worlds) and nothing authored or initiated — that's edge-dwelling recurring. Name it directly in the review; don't reframe it as a quiet week.

## Reputation & Taste

**Purpose:** Formalize the move toward selective visibility, generosity, and signal control — without letting curation alone count as progress indefinitely.

**Hard rules:**
- Never post or endorse from agitation.
- Never endorse what you would not privately defend.
- Curation and signal-setting support authored output; they do not replace it (see Work & Selective Output's weekly floor).
- Keep language in any public-facing material abstract and behavioral (signal quality, standard consistency) rather than narrated personal specifics.

**Daily action (binary):** One deliberate reputation move — endorse, refine (your own public surface), publish, or connect.

**Weekly anchor:** One endorsement of someone else's work, one refinement of your own public surface, one act of selective presence.

**Failure recovery:** If curation/endorsement activity is high but authored output is flat for two weeks running, that's the "prestige fantasy" failure mode — the fix is a Work module action, not a Reputation one.

## Command

**Purpose:** The executive layer that keeps the other four alive with minimal friction. Matches the low-friction, single-action-per-day design already validated elsewhere in this system (one-tap logging, no multi-step tracking) rather than a heavier multi-item daily architecture.

**Daily rhythm — three short moments, no long journaling:**
- **Morning:** name today's one action in each domain. A few words, not a paragraph.
- **During the day:** execute, or degrade gracefully to the minimum-viable version rather than skipping outright.
- **Evening:** mark each domain complete or incomplete. For anything incomplete, write one line: what blocked it, what the if-then plan is next time.

**Weekly rhythm — one review block, five questions:**
1. What repeated?
2. What drifted?
3. What produced visible proof?
4. What became theater (insight without a resulting rule or action)?
5. What single rule changes next week?

End the review by setting: the week's Body schedule, one Work target, one Social emphasis, one Reputation target.

**How you actually process — operating principle, not noise:**
Talking through a problem out loud, in motion, addressed to someone (even imagined) is a native processing mode, not a distraction from "real" thinking or a sign something's wrong. Treat it as a tool to schedule deliberately — a walk, a paced voice-note — rather than something that only happens uninvited late at night. The same mechanism that makes rehearsal and self-translation exhausting in front of other people is also the mechanism that does the best thinking when it isn't being performed for an audience. Use it on purpose instead of waiting for it to arrive uninvited.

**Standing floor:** recovery and fellowship commitments are not part of Command's audit scope. They're the foundation this whole system stands on, not one more tracked item.
