# Sovereign — genesis planning thread

## Round 1: initial integration-framework request

Prompt focus: integration, power, discipline, self-possession — grounded in real psychological/strategic frameworks, not just Marcus's own prior files. Established context: anxiety shows up as mental rehearsal, self-translation, performance management, attachment-triggered overcorrection; self-erasure in relationships via over-investing in others' worlds while under-sharing his own; a pivot from production-heavy 3D printing toward reputation via taste, generosity, exclusivity, and promotion of other creators; redirected energy toward gym/physical discipline. Requested framework built on 48 Laws of Power, The Prince, Stoicism, and practical behavioral systems — implementation over therapy language.

Response established: five-module structure (Body, Work, Social Field, Reputation, Command), a daily protocol (morning claim / afternoon execution / evening audit), three power-literature rules (conceal strain through composure not dishonesty, court attention through selectivity, guard independence before mutuality is demonstrated), one Prince principle (stability through consistency of conduct, not popularity), one Stoic discipline (command over action/perception/response, not over others' interpretation), and a 30-day non-negotiables floor.

## Round 3: structural home recommendation

Recommended a vault-based MOC hub rather than leaving the material as a chat artifact: one Home note, a Canon note, doctrine/protocol notes, and an execution layer, following PARA/MOC conventions. Original proposal was ten pages (Home, Canon, Protocols, Weekly Review, Daily Command, Body, Power, Reputation, Work, Integration) — collapsed during build to three (Home.md, Canon.md, Protocols.md) to match how the rest of this vault actually gets built incrementally rather than all at once. Also proposed the doctrine's three founding paragraphs (core purpose, behavior rule, maintenance loop) — refined and folded into `Home.md`.

## Round 4: full research-grounded plan (post-rename to Sovereign, post-scope-rule)

Following the rename to **Sovereign** (to avoid collision with "Marcus" as this vault's Perplexity research-agent identity) and the scope rule (everything about Javier's own conduct is fair game; nothing about a specific partner, relationship, or explicit content — public repo), Marcus delivered a full research-grounded revision:

- **Home doctrine:** SOVEREIGN as a structure for self-command, not self-stylization — explicitly reducing translation between inner life and outward conduct through ordered action, embodied discipline, and selective expression. "Reflection" narrowed to brief calibration in service of the next action (per implementation-intention research), not open-ended review.
- **Body:** minimum-viable-standard design over an ideal plan — "if it's the training window, begin the first ten minutes without negotiation; if the full session is impossible, complete the minimum session immediately." Single tracked action per day (body proof completed y/n), identity language only follows the action, never precedes it.
- **Work & Selective Output:** protects against both overproduction and ornamental curation — one decisive work move per workday (make / refine / publish / position), one visible or public-facing output per week minimum, grounded in implementation intentions plus ACT-style values-based committed action.
- **Social Field — the strongest correction to the original plan:** split rule. External/strategic contexts keep measured disclosure and composure (Greene/Machiavelli-style concealment scoped here only). Close relationships get the opposite default — mandatory unforced self-disclosure — grounded in self-disclosure/closeness research (reciprocity and appropriate depth as the actual mechanism of relational progress, not restraint).
- **Reputation & Taste:** repeated, legible signals over time rather than mood-driven output; never post from agitation, never endorse what wouldn't be privately defended; curation supports but never replaces authored output.
- **Command:** one implementation-intention-style commitment per domain per day, one brief weekly review — three short daily moments (morning claim, execute-or-degrade, evening binary mark) rather than an open-ended audit, since implementation-intention research shows specificity and cueing outperform elaborate tracking for adherence.
- **What didn't hold up in the original plan, per Marcus's own audit:** "reflection" was too open-ended and could become elegant rumination; the reputation pivot risked underproduction if selectivity was read as permission to produce nothing; the original power framing didn't sufficiently separate strategic opacity from close-relationship conduct (corrected via the Social Field split-rule).
- **Revised acquisition buy order:** Atomic Habits, Mastery, Discipline Is Destiny, The Laws of Human Nature, Attached, Man's Search for Meaning, The War of Art, The Art of Seduction (moved last — flagged as most likely to bleed strategic/concealment framing into the Social Field module if introduced before the split-rule is stable).
