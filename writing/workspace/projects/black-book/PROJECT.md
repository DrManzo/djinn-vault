# Black Book

**Type:** Personal psychology work — nighttime reflections, active imagination, dream analysis
**Framework:** Jungian — shadow work, active imagination, individuation, inner persona development

---

## What It Is

The Black Book is Javier's personal psychology journal. Not a book for publication in the traditional sense — a body of inner work using Jungian methods. Nighttime reflections, active imagination sessions, free association, and dream analysis. The recurring cast of inner figures — the Child, the Ancient, the Warrior, Faust — evolve across entries.

---

## Key Themes

- Shadow work and integration
- Inner personas: Child, Ancient, Warrior, Faust
- Alcohol and self-destruction vs. clarity
- Identity exploration and vulnerability
- Fiction as psychological mirror (Malazan, Belgariad as emotional proxies)
- Individuation and the burden of self
- Sobriety and accountability

## Inner Figures

| Figure | Role |
