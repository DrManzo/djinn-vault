## Geography & Regions

**Aethoria** is a Victorian-inspired fantasy realm with four primary regions:

### Ironhaven (The Grand Metropolis)
- Sprawling Victorian city — the central hub of Aethoria
- Districts: **Upper Terraces** (wealthy), **Smokestack Quarter** (industrial), **Undercity** (shadowy, hidden)
- Surrounded by a massive wall — controls both physical access and the flow of Essence (political tool)
- Climate: temperate, with industrial smog and magical influences affecting weather
- The Undercity beneath Ironhaven: shadowy, damp — unique ecosystems and magical phenomena; rules of surface do not fully apply

### The Misty Moorlands
- Wild, Essence-rich lands surrounding Ironhaven
- Home to strange creatures and unpredictable magical phenomena
- Supplies rare materials for Essence catalysts
- Climate: misty, temperate, frequent fog, unique magical storms driven by high Essence concentration
- Flora and fauna influenced by magical energies

### The Sundered Isles
- Archipelago known for powerful Adepts and ancient magical traditions
- Landscapes: beaches, forests, possible volcanic regions
- Long history of conflict with Aethoria — the Feyrift War left deep scars
- Home to pirate nations and marauder clans that threaten Aethorian maritime trade
- Cultural and magical practices differ significantly from mainland Aethoria (elemental magic, ancient technologies)
- Climate: tropical to temperate microclimates created by isolation and magical energies
- May hold ancient secrets about Essence and the Void

### The Rustlands
- Vast industrialized northern region constantly shrouded in smog
- Dominated by Rust Barons who push technological advancement over traditional magical practices
- Climate: harsh, polluted, limited natural vegetation
- Relies on steam-powered technology rather than Essence
- Environmental degradation creates visual and thematic contrast with the Moorlands

## Political Structure

### The Crown
- Nominally rules Aethoria but lacks true power
- Current monarch: young and easily manipulated, serving as a figurehead
- Provides legitimacy and tradition — the public face of governance

### The Assembly of Lords
- Composed of noble houses controlling different aspects of society
- Ostensibly advises the Crown but each pursues its own agenda
- **House of Steele** — industrial magnates, significant sway over industrial sectors
- **House of Blackthorn** — old nobility and traditionalists, maintain grip on historical and cultural aspects
- **House of Silverbrook** — new money progressives, pushing for advancement and modernization

### The Shadow Council *(true power)*
- Clandestine organization of powerful individuals — truly controls Aethoria from behind the scenes
- Master Thorne is a member
- Secretly controls the Essence Academy, using it to maintain monopoly over magical knowledge
- Any political event may have a Shadow Council layer beneath the surface appearance

### The Guild of Artisans
- Controls much of the economy and technological advancements
- Includes Essence-infused technology and steam-powered machinery
- Economic and technological control gives it significant political influence

## Society & Class

### Class Tiers

| Class | Characteristics |
|-------|----------------|
| Upper Class | Wealth, power, influence; high-end education; exclusive social clubs; political access; tend to have stronger magical abilities |
| Middle Class | Struggles to maintain decent lifestyle; white-collar focus; educated enough to question but not powerful enough to change |
| Working Class | Long hours, blue-collar; limited education; financial insecurity; limited social mobility |
| Lower Class | Harsh poverty; no education access; relies on welfare; barred from quality healthcare and legal services |
| Underworld | Operates beneath visible class structure; alternative economies and power networks |

**Key dynamic:** Magic and social power concentrate in the same hands — upper classes tend toward stronger magical abilities, lower classes have raw talent but no training access. Self-reinforcing cycle.

**Entertainment by class:** Upper class attends exclusive events. Working class prefers sports and local parks. Unique customs per class reinforce social divisions.

## Economy

### Guilds
- **Guild of Artisans** — sub-guilds for blacksmithing, carpentry, glassmaking; apprentice → journeyman → master system
- **Merchants Guild** — trade routes with Sundered Isles and Rustlands; sets market regulations; negotiates trade agreements
- **Essence Workers Guild** — sub-guilds for healing, enchanting, elemental control; licensing at different expertise levels

### Financial Institutions
- **Royal Bank of Ironhaven** — central bank; regulates currency; loans for business ventures; secure storage for Essence-infused artifacts
- **Sundered Isles Trading Company** — monopoly on exotic materials (rare metals, magical plants); maritime security against piracy

### Industries
- **Essence-infused technology** — research centers, manufacturing of enchanted weapons and magical tools, regulated
- **Steam-powered machinery** — advanced steam engines, clockwork mechanisms, manufacturing/transportation/agriculture
- **Exotic materials trade** — sourced from Sundered Isles and Misty Moorlands

**Central tension:** Traditional Essence industries vs. steam-powered industries — mirrors the broader society conflict between tradition and progress.

## Magic System — Essence Manipulation

### Core Principle
Essence is a natural, invisible, omnipresent energy that flows in currents through the world. Magic users shape it through **willpower, concentration, and mental imagery** — spell effectiveness directly tied to the clarity and vividness of the caster's mental image.

### Catalysts
Personal objects (jewelry, tattoos, trinkets) that focus the user's will and amplify their Essence connection. Replace traditional wands or staffs. Each practitioner's catalyst reflects their personality and approach.

### Practitioner Classes

| Class | Description |
|-------|------------|
| Adept | Rare — strong natural affinity, complex casting, can influence minds |
| Channeler | Common — functional ability, simpler spells |
| Sensitive | Can sense Essence but limited manipulation |
| Ascendant | Transcended mortality through Essence mastery |
| God | Ultimate tier — divine Essence wielders |

**Training:** Apprenticeships or self-discovery — no formal schools (Academy is the exception, controlled by Shadow Council). Keeps magic somewhat rare and mysterious.

### Limitations
- Mental stamina, distance, concentration, imagination — complex spells drain the caster more
- **Distance:** Tied to visual range. Maximum ~3 miles for minimal effects. Optical aids (telescopes, enchanted lenses) can extend effective range.
- Casting beyond visual range: significantly more energy, rapid exhaustion, diminished effects

### The Essence Drought
A decline in Essence flow — potentially linked to overuse of steam technology. As steam expands, Essence becomes harder to access. Creates a ticking clock: traditionalists losing power, gods potentially desperate for worshippers, regions becoming isolated as magical communication fails.

### Technology Integration
- **Essence-powered airships** — luxury travel for the wealthy
- **Steam-Essence hybrid technologies** — cutting edge of Aethorian innovation
- **Essence Exchange** — Essence-infused items traded as commodities
- **Magical telegraph** — near-instantaneous communication across the realm, vulnerable to Essence disruptions
- **Steam-powered underground trains** — mass transit in urban areas

## Education & Healthcare

### Essence Academy
- Prestigious institution training magic users in Essence manipulation
- **Secretly controlled by the Shadow Council** — ensures magical power concentrates among loyalists
- Hidden curriculum: advanced techniques, forbidden knowledge, or political indoctrination graduates don't know they received
- Coveted and feared institution

### Healthcare
- Blends magical healing with steam-powered medical devices
- **Guild of Healers** — oversees training and certification; bridges magic and technology; enforces ethics
- Upper class: full access to both magical and technological care
- Lower class: relies on traditional methods
- Steam-powered devices: surgical tools, diagnostic machines, prosthetic limbs
- Ethical questions around limits of magical intervention — strict regulations in place

## Transportation & Communication

### Travel (by class)
- **Wealthy:** Essence-powered airships — fast, luxurious, exclusive
- **Common:** Steam-powered underground trains — efficient, crowded, industrial
- **Elite/Military:** Magical portals — instant travel between major cities; restricted; require tokens or magical activation; limitations and risks (unpredictable opening times)

### Communication
- **Magical telegraph** — Essence-based, near-instantaneous, long-distance; vulnerable to Essence Drought disruptions
- **Traditional:** Letters and messengers — still in use alongside telegraph

## International Relations

### Aethoria vs The Sundered Isles
- Long history of conflict — the **Feyrift War** left deep animosity
- **The Sundering** — catastrophic event that ravaged the Isles; legacy of destruction and displacement
- Pirate nations and marauder clans threaten Aethorian maritime trade
- Cultural and magical differences create ongoing misunderstandings
- An **expansionist empire** threatens the Isles — potential common enemy with Aethoria, or new source of conflict
- Ancient Isles secrets may relate to Essence or the Void
- The Isles tension feeds the balance of power between Crown, Shadow Council, and Rust Barons
