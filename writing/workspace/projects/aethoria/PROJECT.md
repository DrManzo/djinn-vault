# Aethoria

**Genre:** Dark Victorian fantasy — political intrigue, magic vs technology, class warfare
**Tone:** Influences: Malazan (epic scope, active gods), Mistborn (rule-based magic), First Law (cynical realism, dark humor)

---

## Premise

Aethoria is a Victorian-inspired fantasy world where Essence magic and steam-powered technology coexist and compete. True power belongs not to the Crown — which is a young, easily manipulated figurehead — but to the Shadow Council, a secret organization operating from the darkness. Into this world steps Corvus Shadowblade, an assassin apprentice to the Machiavellian Master Thorne, who is slowly uncovering the truth about his mentor's grand scheme.
