# Confederates in Research

## Summary
This note examines the use of confederates in psychological research, drawing on studies involving placebo alcohol administration. It covers how confederates enhance experimental credibility, the ethical considerations of deception, and the four validities framework applied to studies using confederate-based methodologies.

## Key Points
- Confederates are trained individuals who act as participants or social influences within experimental settings to standardize social context
- In placebo alcohol studies, same-sex confederates make scripted statements to reinforce participants' belief that real alcohol is being consumed
- The use of confederates minimizes variability in social influence and ensures consistent social context across experimental groups
- Bernstein, Wood, and Colby (2017) achieved an 89.1% deception rate using a confederate combined with multiple sensory cues
- Sensory cues included real liquor bottles, rum and tequila extracts for taste, tequila-rimmed glasses for smell, and confederate behavior
- Deception was assessed using a semi-structured funnel debrief with responses rated on a 9-point scale
- Deceived participants were significantly more likely to estimate having a positive blood alcohol content (95.9% vs. 13.3% for non-deceived)
- Borderline deception cases were resolved through experimenter consensus to increase classification reliability
- Construct validity should be prioritized in studies using confederates to ensure the manipulation truly measures what it intends
- Internal validity is strengthened by standardized procedures and controlled environments
- External validity is limited when samples consist predominantly of college students from a single university
- Statistical validity is supported by appropriate analyses (chi-square tests, correlations) showing large, significant effects
- The lack of a real alcohol comparison group limits the ability to directly compare placebo and actual alcohol effects

## Details
The use of confederates in research raises important ethical considerations around deception. Researchers must ensure that deception is justified by the scientific value of the study, that no non-deceptive alternative exists, and that participants are fully debriefed afterward.

The four validities framework provides a systematic approach to evaluating studies that use confederates. Construct validity asks whether the confederate manipulation actually created the intended psychological state. Internal validity examines whether confounding variables were controlled. External validity considers whether findings generalize beyond the specific experimental context. Statistical validity assesses whether the data analysis appropriately supports the conclusions.
