### Emerging Adulthood

**Living Arrangement 1: Living with College Roommates**

- **Emotional:** Living with roommates can provide emotional support and reduce feelings of loneliness, which is crucial during the transition to adulthood. This arrangement fosters social connections and enhances emotional resilience among young adults (Mortimer, Kim, & Swartz, 2010).
 
- **Social:** Sharing living space encourages social interaction, collaboration, and the development of interpersonal skills. It also helps young adults build a network of friends that can be beneficial for future career opportunities (Mortimer et al., 2010).

- **Physical:** Roommates may influence each other's health behaviors positively, such as exercising together or cooking healthy meals. However, they may also contribute to unhealthy habits, like late-night eating or irregular sleep patterns (Mortimer et al., 2010).

- **Financial:** Sharing rent and utilities can alleviate financial strain, allowing young adults to allocate resources to education or savings. However, reliance on roommates may hinder the development of financial independence (Mortimer et al., 2010).
