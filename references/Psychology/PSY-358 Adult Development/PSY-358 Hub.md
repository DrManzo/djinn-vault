# PSY-358 Adult Development and Aging - Course Hub

## Summary
Central hub for all PSY-358 Adult Development and Aging course notes. This course explores the biological, psychological, sociocultural, and life-cycle forces that shape adult development and aging. Topics include demographics, physical changes, cognitive development, relationships, career transitions, retirement, mental health, and end-of-life issues.

## Key Points
1. Adult development is influenced by multiple interacting forces across the lifespan
2. Aging involves both gains and losses across biological, cognitive, and social domains
3. Successful aging is multidimensional and includes physical, mental, and social well-being
4. Cultural and societal factors significantly impact the aging experience
5. Evidence-based interventions can promote healthy aging and life satisfaction
