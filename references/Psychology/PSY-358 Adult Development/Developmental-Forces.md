Biological:

Definition: Biological forces are physiological developments and changes that lead to maturity and affect growth and behavior.

Examples:

1. Genetic factors influencing traits and predispositions
2. Hormonal changes during puberty triggering physical development
3. Brain maturation enabling more complex cognitive abilities

Psychological:

Definition: Psychological forces are mental, emotional, and behavioral developments that shape an individual's thoughts, feelings, and actions.

Examples:

1. Formation of self-identity and personality during adolescence
2. Development of attachment styles in early childhood
3. Acquisition of language and communication skills

Sociocultural:

Definition: Sociocultural forces are values, ideas, and beliefs from one's social environment and culture that influence development and behavior.

Examples:

1. Cultural norms and expectations shaping gender roles
2. Influence of peer groups on adolescent attitudes and behaviors
