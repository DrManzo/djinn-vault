# PSY-402 Cognitive Psychology - Course Hub

## Overview
This hub organizes structured notes from Perplexity AI chat exports into 12 core topics for PSY-402 Cognitive Psychology at Grand Canyon University. Each note follows a consistent format with summary, key points, details, APA references, and cross-links.

## Notes Index

### [[Neurological Structures And Cognitive Processes]]
The biological foundation of cognition—from neurons and glial cells to action potentials, synaptic transmission, brain lobes, and nervous system organization.

### [[Multisensory Processing Abnormalities]]
How the brain integrates information across sensory channels, and what abnormalities in conditions like autism and schizophrenia reveal about normal sensory processing and cross-modal plasticity.

### [[Dorsal vs Ventral Visual Processing Streams]]
The two major visual pathways: the ventral "what" stream for object recognition and the dorsal "where/how" stream for spatial processing and visually-guided action, with clinical double dissociations.

### [[Neurological Networks And Attention Networks]]
Three interacting attention networks—dorsal (goal-directed), ventral (stimulus-driven), and subcortical (foundational)—and their dynamic coordination for sustained focus and reorienting.

### [[Memory Types]]
Four distinct memory systems: sensory memory, short-term/working memory, nondeclarative (implicit) memory, and declarative (explicit) memory, with clinical implications for neurodegenerative conditions.

### [[Brain Mechanisms In Memory]]
How memory is stored as distributed cortical traces indexed by the hippocampus, the reconstructive nature of retrieval, reconsolidation processes, and the left-hemisphere interpreter's role in false memories.

### [[Metacognitive Regulation And Memory]]
The executive management of memory through planning, monitoring, and controlling cognitive activities; includes judgments of learning, retrieval practice, and overcoming illusions of competence.

### [[Cognitive Mapping]]
The brain's ability to create internal mental representations of relationships, extending from spatial navigation to organizing abstract knowledge, concept mapping, and strategic planning.

### [[Working Memory]]
The active processing workspace with limited capacity, including Baddeley's four-component model (central executive, phonological loop, visuospatial sketchpad, episodic buffer) and implications for learning.

### [[Intelligence And Cognitive Psychology]]
The relationship between intelligence and cognitive processes—working memory, attention, executive function—and practical applications for learning optimization and professional development.

### [[Self-Concept And Cognition]]
How self-concept operates as a cognitive structure shaping information processing, memory, decision-making, and behavior, including self-schemas, the self-reference effect, and cognitive biases.

### [[Source Gathering And Research Methods]]
Systematic approaches to locating, evaluating, and synthesizing scholarly sources in cognitive psychology, including research methodologies, source evaluation criteria, and evidence-based practices.
