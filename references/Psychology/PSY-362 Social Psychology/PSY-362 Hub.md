# PSY-362 Hub

## Course Overview
**PSY-362 Social Psychology** at Grand Canyon University examines how individuals think about, influence, and relate to one another. The course covers major topics including social cognition, attitudes and persuasion, social influence, group processes, prejudice, aggression, helping behavior, and intimate relationships.

**Textbook:** Heinzen, T., & Goodfriend, W. (2021). *Social psychology* (2nd ed.). SAGE Publications.

## Notes Index

### Social Influence and Group Dynamics
- [[Groupthink and Conformity]] - How groups shape individual decisions and when consensus becomes dangerous
- [[Group Membership and Social Identity]] - Deindividuation, social loafing, and social facilitation in group contexts
- [[Deindividuation Disinhibition and Anonymity]] - Loss of self-awareness in group settings and its consequences
- [[12 Angry Men Analysis]] - Film analysis of persuasion, conformity, prejudice, and aggression escalation

### Cognition and Bias
- [[Mental Shortcuts and Stereotypes]] - How cognitive categorization leads to stereotyping and prejudice
- [[Cognitive Processes and Prejudice]] - Unconscious cognitive mechanisms that produce bias without malice
- [[Cognitive Dissonance]] - Psychological discomfort from conflicting beliefs and how people resolve it

### Persuasion and Attitudes
- [[Persuasion Models and Ethics]] - Heuristic-systematic model, dual-process frameworks, and ethical persuasion
- [[Theoretical Frameworks for Relationships]] - Attachment theory, investment model, and Sternberg's triangular theory

### Prejudice and Discrimination
- [[Religion and Prejudice]] - Allport's paradox and how religion both creates and reduces prejudice
- [[Advocacy Plan Autism Spectrum Disorder]] - Advocacy strategies for combating stereotypes and discrimination

### Relationships and Attraction
- [[Attraction and Relationships]] - Universal patterns and personal factors in relationship formation
- [[Gender and Culture in Attraction]] - How gender and cultural differences shape attraction and dating
- [[Evolutionary Theory and Altruism]] - Evolutionary explanations for self-sacrificing behavior

### Applied Social Psychology
- [[Quiet Rage Stanford Prison Experiment]] - Situational forces, social roles, and institutional abuse
- [[Bystander Intervention]] - Why people fail to help in crowds and strategies to increase helping
- [[Media Violence and Aggression]] - Short-term and long-term effects of media violence exposure
