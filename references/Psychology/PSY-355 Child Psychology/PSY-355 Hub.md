# PSY-355 Child Psychology - Hub

## Notes
- [[Childbirth And Neonatal Health]] - Stages of childbirth, birth options, APGAR scoring, and infant caregiving adjustments
- [[Low Birth Weight Vs Preterm Infants]] - Comparison of LBW and preterm conditions, long-term consequences, and mitigation strategies
- [[Postpartum Period]] - Physical, emotional, and psychological adjustments after birth; postpartum depression and support systems
- [[Prenatal Development And Biological Beginnings]] - Conception, three stages of prenatal development, teratogens, and pregnancy options
- [[Infant Developmental Milestones]] - Physical, cognitive, and socioemotional milestones during the first year of life
- [[Developmental Toy Analysis]] - How stacking ring toys support fine motor, gross motor, cognitive, and social development

- [[Developmental Theories And Theorists]] - Erikson's industry vs. inferiority and Piaget's concrete operational stage applied to middle childhood
- [[Teenage Risk Factors]] - Risk factors for adolescent substance abuse across familial, peer, mental health, and biological domains
- [[Adolescent Protective Factors]] - Family support, school environments, peer relationships, and community resources that build resilience
- [[Study Guide And Sample Questions]] - Key definitions, developmental patterns, brain structures, and Piagetian concepts for exam preparation
- [[Research Methods In Psychology]] - Naturalistic observation and surveys: advantages, disadvantages, and ethical considerations
- [[Research Ethics]] - Ethical considerations for research with human participants, informed consent, IRB approval, and the Emilio scenario
- [[Parenting Challenges In Middle Childhood]] - Common challenges during ages 6-12, disabilities, support systems, and discipline approaches
- [[Families With Children With Disabilities]] - Experiences of families with children with ASD, Piaget's theory applied, and community/faith resources
- [[Child Art And Development]] - Rhoda Kellogg's five stages of artistic development and art as non-verbal communication
- [[Adhd Research Paper]] - Scholarly research on ADHD, theoretical frameworks, article analysis, and ethical considerations
