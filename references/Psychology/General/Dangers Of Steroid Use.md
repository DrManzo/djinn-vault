# Dangers Of Steroid Use

## Summary
This scholarly discussion response examines the health risks associated with anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS) use throughout an individual's lifespan, particularly when combined with exercise. It covers cardiovascular, reproductive, and psychological consequences across different age groups.

## Key Points
- Anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS) users face a threefold increase in mortality risk compared to non-users
- AAS use leads to a higher frequency of hospital admissions
- Steroid use affects various bodily systems and can lead to long-term complications
- Cardiovascular issues are a major risk of AAS use
- Liver damage is a significant health consequence of steroid use
- Psychological disturbances are associated with AAS use
- Reproductive health problems include erectile dysfunction and infertility
- Adolescents who engage in AAS use may experience stunted growth
- Adolescents face elevated risk of health problems later in life
- Intense exercise regimens combined with AAS amplify strain on the body
- Combined use increases likelihood of severe cardiovascular complications
- Overexertion injuries are more likely when using steroids
- Even minor side effects like acne and male pattern baldness can have significant psychological impacts
- Side effects can affect self-esteem and mental well-being throughout life
- The perceived benefits of enhanced performance and muscle growth are far outweighed by potential side effects
- The dangers of steroid use extend across the entire lifespan, from adolescence through old age
