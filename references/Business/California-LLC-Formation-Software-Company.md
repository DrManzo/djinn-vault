# California LLC Formation for Software Development Company

## Summary
A comprehensive guide to forming a Limited Liability Company (LLC) in California for a software development and technology company, covering all required steps, ongoing compliance requirements, costs, and tax implications for formation in 2025.

## Key Points
1. **Name selection**: Must be unique and distinguishable; must include "LLC," "L.L.C.," or "Limited Liability Company"; verify availability through California Secretary of State Business Entities Search
2. **Registered agent**: Required; must be 18+, have physical California street address (no PO boxes), available during business hours; can be self or professional service
3. **Articles of Organization (Form LLC-1)**: File with California Secretary of State; $70 filing fee; 3-5 business days processing online via BizFile
4. **Initial Statement of Information (Form LLC-12)**: File within 90 days of formation; $20 fee; includes principal address, member/manager names, business activity description; updated biennially thereafter
5. **Operating Agreement**: California law requires all LLCs to adopt one; not filed with state but must be maintained in company records; should cover ownership structure, management, capital contributions, profit/loss distribution, voting rights, member roles, dissolution procedures
6. **Employer Identification Number (EIN)**: Free from IRS; 15-minute online application; required for hiring employees, corporate tax election, business bank account
7. **Business licenses**: No statewide license for software companies; local city/county business tax certificate required ($50-$100); may need home occupation permit if working from home
8. **Annual franchise tax**: $800 minimum, due regardless of income; first year due 15th day of 4th month after formation; subsequent years due April 15; file Form 3522
9. **Additional LLC fee**: Graduated fee on California-source income exceeding $250,000 ($900 to $11,790); due by 15th day of 6th month of tax year
10. **Annual tax return**: Form 568 required every year; first Form 568 not due until the year after formation
11. **Total first-year costs**: Approximately $960-$1,600 (Articles of Organization $70, Statement of Information $20, franchise tax $800, local license $50-$100, optional registered agent $100-$300)
12. **2025 formation tax implications**: $800 franchise tax must be paid for 2025; Form 568 tax return not required until 2026; no additional fee unless gross receipts exceed $250,000

## Details

### Step-by-Step Formation Process
The formation process begins with name verification through the Secretary of State's database, followed by appointing a registered agent who will receive legal documents and tax notices. The Articles of Organization officially create the LLC and require business purpose, registered agent information, and management structure designation (member-managed or manager-managed).

The Operating Agreement, while not filed with the state, is legally required in California and provides the internal governance framework. A written agreement is strongly recommended for legal protection even though oral agreements are technically valid.

### Ongoing Compliance
California LLCs face among the highest compliance costs in the nation. The $800 annual franchise tax applies even with zero income, making budgeting essential. The biennial Statement of Information ($20) keeps state records current. Form 568 must be filed annually, with automatic 6-7 month extensions available.

### Software Company Advantages
Software development businesses benefit from not requiring special state-level professional licenses, ability to operate from home with appropriate local permits, and California's robust technology ecosystem. If selling software products, a seller's permit from the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration may be needed.
