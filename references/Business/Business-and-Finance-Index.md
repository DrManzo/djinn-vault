### Summary
The current business model of Typhons Forge LLC, including its subsidiaries like The Forge (online smoke shop), Terp Tribe (TT), and Asgard Acres (AA), is not inherently too complex to be perceived negatively by banks. Key factors in bank evaluations include the business plan's viability, financial projections, collateral, industry experience, and credit history.

### Key Points
- **Business Model Structure:** The current structure with multiple divisions/subsidiaries is manageable.
- **Bank Evaluation Factors:**
  - Business Plan Viability
  - Financial Projections and Cash Flow
  - Collateral and Assets
  - Industry Experience of Founders
  - Credit History

### Details
Typhons Forge LLC operates a complex business model with multiple divisions, including:
- **The Forge:** An online smoke shop.
- **Terp Tribe (TT):** Likely related to terpenes or cannabis products.
- **Asgard Acres (AA):** Possibly an agricultural venture.

To ensure the business is perceived positively by banks, focus on:
1. **Developing a Comprehensive Business Plan:** Clearly explain each division's role and potential impact.
2. **Solid Financial Projections:** Provide detailed financial forecasts to demonstrate growth and stability.
3. **Demonstrating Industry Knowledge:** Highlight your experience in relevant industries.
4. **Explaining Land Acquisition Strategy:** Articulate how the land acquisition fits into long-term business goals.
