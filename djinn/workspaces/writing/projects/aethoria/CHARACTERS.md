## Corvus Shadowblade — Protagonist

**Role:** Protagonist — Thorne's apprentice and unwitting pawn
**Age:** Young adult
**Occupation:** Assassin
**Essence Class:** Channeler, evolving toward Adept-level

### Physical
- Appearance: likely agile and unassuming (not yet specified)
- Carries: hidden blades and poisoned needles
- Attire: dark, practical clothing for stealth

### Personality
- Pragmatic
- Observant
- Adaptable
- Morally conflicted

### Motivations
- Uncover the truth about Thorne's plans
- Master Essence manipulation
- Find his place in the world

### Fears / Weaknesses
- Uncertainty about his role and loyalties
- Potential to be manipulated by Thorne

### Background
- Former noble turned assassin after a family tragedy (details unspecified)
- Became Thorne's apprentice

### Abilities
- Essence: growing skill — specializes in shadow and illusion magic
- Combat: exceptional with blades and poisons
- Stealth: master of disguise and infiltration
- Strategic: brilliant planner, quick thinker

### Character Arc
Channeler → Adept. Moral journey from assassin to potential hero or destroyer. Uncovering and confronting Thorne's true nature.

### Open Questions

4. How will growing Essence abilities change him?

## Master Thorne — Main Antagonist

**Role:** Main antagonist — mentor figure to Corvus, mastermind behind major political events
**Age:** Sixties
**Occupation:** Former royal assassin, current court advisor, secret Shadow Council member
**Essence Class:** Adept-level — highly skilled, able to influence minds subtly

### Physical
- Outwardly appears as a kindly, somewhat eccentric scholar
- Well-tailored attire, aristocratic style
- Capable of sudden, terrifying physical transformation when angered

### Personality
- Machiavellian
- Manipulative and cunning
- Patient and instructive as a mentor
- Capable of sudden bursts of cruelty and rage

### Motivations
- Long-term plans for reshaping Aethoria's power structure
- Hidden personal agenda possibly tied to the loss of Evelyn

### Fears / Weaknesses
- Vulnerability tied to lost love: **Evelyn** (kept as a hidden portrait in his study)
- His carefully constructed plans could unravel

### Background
- Former royal assassin who transitioned into court advisor
- Rose to become a Shadow Council member
- Secretly controls or influences the Essence Academy

### Abilities
- Essence: Adept — can influence minds subtly
- Combat: formidable, terrifying capacity for violence
- Political: expert manipulator of people and events
- Shapeshifting: can transform into a monstrous form when enraged (source unknown — open question)

### Character Arc
Gradual reveal of his true nature and plans. Potential tragic fall as his schemes come to light. Represents "the monster beneath the mask."

### Open Questions
1. What happened to Evelyn?
2. What is the ultimate goal of his grand scheme?
3. How did he transition from assassin to advisor?
4. What is the source of his shapeshifting ability?
5. What is his relationship to the Shadow Council's larger agenda?
