# Factions & Guilds — Dominion of Pyraxis

> These are organizations that exist independently of (or alongside) the Ten Houses. Some are house-adjacent, some are deliberately neutral, some are the actual power behind quiet doors. Characters in here may or may not have house affiliations — noted where known.

---

## The Fists

**Type:** Underworld / Paramilitary
**Alignment:** No formal chain of command; hired by those with reach
**First appeared:** Prologue — orchestrated the Burning of Sec-tra on behalf of unknown conspirators

The loose specialist crews that do what noble houses and the Fangs cannot be seen doing. They operate through specialist networks with no formal hierarchy, which makes them impossible to fully map and impossible to fully destroy. Individual cells may not know who hired them two steps up the chain.

**Key facts:**
- Told to burn Sec-tra in the Prologue; "forgot a couple of details" about leaving survivors — consistent with no formal command discipline
- Were promised they would help with reconstruction if understaffed — this is how they're handled: loose incentives, not orders
- Who can direct the Fists without being traceable is one of the real power questions in the empire

**Notable figures:**
- No named leaders — by design
- Their handler in the Prologue is unnamed; possibly connected to Morghaven or Faust's network

## The Fangs

**Type:** Imperial Intelligence
**Alignment:** Emperor Arctus — answers outside all cabinet accountability
**Structure:** Operatives → [classified chain] → Shadow Chancellor → Emperor

The state's eyes. Distinct from Morghaven's private network: the Fangs are the empire's institutional intelligence apparatus. They operate under the Shadow Chancellor, whose identity is state-classified.

**Key facts:**
- Javelin Solentine is Fang-trained — his combat/intelligence skills predate his role as Raxz's mentor
- Theo Altonian operates at Fang level as an imperial ambassador
- Verath Valerion technically reports to the Fangs but her primary loyalty is to her father Lord Gavren
- The Shadow Chancellor's identity is known only to Arctus and possibly one other person

**Notable figures:**
- **Shadow Chancellor** — unnamed by design; identity is the empire's most carefully kept secret
- **Javelin Solentine** — embedded operative inside the royal household
- **Theo Altonian** — foreign intelligence / ambassador track
- **Verath Valerion** — border network / overlapping loyalty

## The Merchant Syndicate

**Type:** Economic Guild
**Alignment:** Mercurial-adjacent, Vandris-adjacent; theoretically neutral
**Headquarters:** Mobile — offices in major trading cities; strongest presence in the southern ports

The empire's largest organized trade body. Not a house and not a government body — which means every house treats it as someone else's problem to manage, and the Syndicate exploits this deliberately. They finance the roads that Ironwell builds, insure the goods that Vandris ships, and process the taxes that Mercurial tracks. They are everywhere and officially part of nothing.

**Key facts:**
- Lady Kessa Ironwell's foundry network supplied the empire during the wars through Syndicate logistics — the supply chain Arctus owes her for ran through this body
- Master Verran Vandris (Theron's uncle) has southern operations contacts who overlap with Syndicate membership — the bad news he keeps summarizing optimistically passes through Syndicate intelligence first
- House Mercurial has the most formal relationship but deliberately keeps one degree of separation

**Notable figures:**
- **Guildmaster Torvyn Ashwick** 📋 — Head of the central trading council; name from registry (#38 / #229); a merchant's son three generations removed from any noble title, which is how he prefers it
- **Factor Kela Dravenmere** 📋 — Southern ports director; name from registry (#169 / #250); Verran Vandris's primary contact; the one sending the bad news

## The Arcanists' Circle

**Type:** Magical Practitioners' Guild
**Alignment:** Drakenoth-adjacent; jealously independent
**Headquarters:** The Circularium — a dedicated building in the capital

The formal body governing magical practice in the Dominion. Not the military magical branch (that's the Arcanists' Adjunct chain under Aldrix Drakenoth). The Circle handles licensing, training standards, magical incident investigation, and the political gray area of what magic can legally be used for.

**Drakenoth nominally seats the Circle's Archon position** but the Circle's membership includes practitioners from every house and no house — which creates the perpetual tension that the Drakenoth family thinks they run this and the Circle knows they don't.

**Key facts:**
- Raxz's public magical display at the gala (attire transformation without Marcella's consent) falls under Circle jurisdiction — whether anyone chooses to invoke that is a political question
- Javelin's limited magical abilities and his use of "trinkets/honest objects" places him in the Circle's ambiguous zone: not a licensed practitioner, not technically illegal
- Emerick's overactive magical core was likely Circle-assessed at some point; whether they have ongoing jurisdiction over his condition is unclear

**Notable figures:**
- **Archon Representative Zevrath Drakenoth** 🧠 — Nominal head; rarely engages directly
- **Circle Registrar Aldenn Garven** 📋 — Actual administrator; name from registry (#30 Garven adapted); tracks every licensed practitioner and every known incident
- **Investigator Corveth Strelwick** 📋 — Magical incident investigator; name from registry (#159 / #183); the one who would handle a complaint about Raxz's gala display — if anyone filed one

## The Ironwrights' Brotherhood

**Type:** Craftsmen's / Industrial Guild
**Alignment:** Ironwell-adjacent; represents workers not the house
**Headquarters:** The Forge District, capital; branches in every major city

The guild of builders, engineers, foundry workers, and infrastructure craftsmen. In theory it serves House Ironwell's interests. In practice it represents the workers whose labor the house profits from, and that tension is always one bad contract away from a work stoppage.

**Key facts:**
- Gorvin Ironwell (Master of Imperial Infrastructure) negotiates with the Brotherhood on Ironwell's behalf — he's the one who has to look these people in the eye, and they know it
- Ren Ironwell (Dravek's son, heir) is respected by Brotherhood members because he actually rides the roads and visits the sites rather than reviewing them from the capital
- Lady Kessa Ironwell's wartime supply operation ran largely on Brotherhood labor; the debt between them is unacknowledged but understood
- The Brotherhood has never formally organized against a house — but the memory of a strike in Year 1290 PR (45 years ago) that shut down palace construction for three months lives in institutional memory

**Notable figures:**
- **Guild Master Soldrak Keldford** 📋 — Brotherhood head; name from registry (#56 / #248); Gorvin Ironwell's counterpart in negotiations; fair but not soft
- **Chapter Captain Brak Greystone** 📋 — Capital chapter; name from registry (#82 / #235); organizes the daily work; the one the members actually listen to

## The Scholars' Compact

**Type:** Academic / Archival Institution
**Alignment:** Thessar-adjacent; considers itself above politics; is not
**Headquarters:** The Grand Archive, capital

The empire's academic body — libraries, research institutions, the school systems that train the civil service, and the record-keepers who maintain the empire's administrative memory. Thessar founded it; Thessar still chairs its governing council; but the Compact's membership is technically open to any qualified scholar regardless of house.

**Key facts:**
- Aldenn Thessar (Chief Imperial Historian, age 30) uses the Compact's access and credibility as cover for what is functionally investigative reporting
- Lady Evrath Thessar (Grand Archivist) maintains the Compact's public-facing neutrality; privately the Compact's files are Thessar's most valuable asset
- Iseth Thessar's legal consultancy practice draws on Compact archives — she bills the houses for access to their own empire's records
- The Compact has a standing policy of "no political alignment" that every political actor treats as a standing fiction

**Notable figures:**
- **Grand Archivist Evrath Thessar** 🧠 — Nominal head; the Compact IS her
- **Deputy Archivist Iskren Dornath** 📋 — Day-to-day management; name from registry (#28 / #178); the one who actually knows where everything is filed
- **Aldenn Thessar** 🧠 — Chief Imperial Historian; the Compact's most dangerous member

## The Senate Secretariat

**Type:** Civil Institution
**Alignment:** The Convocatum as a body — not any individual house
**Headquarters:** The Convocatum chambers

The permanent staff of the Ten Houses' legislative body. They predate individual house memberships and serve the institution itself. They know the rules better than the nobles who vote, which means every house eventually comes to them for procedural guidance.

**Key facts:**
- When House Kalvennor executed their coordinated gala exit, it was the Secretariat that verified protocol had not been technically breached — a ruling that confirmed the move's devastating subtlety
- Prime Minister Aldric Vernath served in the Secretariat before his elevation — the connection is publicly known and considered an asset
- The Secretariat maintains a complete record of every vote, every session, every procedural ruling since Year 1 PR

**Notable figures:**
- **Chief Clerk Elrath Selwick** 📋 — Head of the Secretariat; name from registry (#25 / #179); has served four prime ministers and outlasted all of them
- **Procedural Adjudicator Halvek Thorvane** 📋 — The rules expert; name from registry (#27 / #180); the person Casven Kalvennor consulted before the gala exit

## The Heralds' Corps

**Type:** Official Communication / Ceremony
**Alignment:** Imperial household — Solentine-adjacent through Brax
**Headquarters:** The Hall of Records, capital

The empire's official messengers, ceremony conductors, and formal announcers. The Herald who announced Raxz and Marcella's entry into the Grand Hall is a member. The voice that carries through the hall with magical enhancement is Heralds' technique.

**Key facts:**
- Every formal announcement, every noble introduction, every summons served officially passes through the Heralds' Corps
- Brax (Lord High Steward) has operational authority over the Heralds — one of his less-visible levers of power
- The Corps trains the magical voice enhancement that carries announcements; this places them in the Circle's gray zone
- A Herald can choose to announce someone slightly late, in the wrong order, or without their full title — these are the smallest available political weapons in the empire

**Notable figures:**
- **Herald Primarch Corvath Halvorn** 📋 — Head of the Corps; name from registry (#18 / #184); has announced more political disasters than any other person in the capital and maintains perfect neutrality about all of them

## The Cartographers' Guild

**Type:** Technical / Intelligence-adjacent Guild
**Alignment:** Valerion and Vandris primarily; theoretically neutral
**Headquarters:** Saltmere — a port city on the southern trade route

The guild that maps roads, borders, trade routes, and territorial claims. They have far more intelligence value than anyone credits. A current map of border changes, trade route disruptions, or territorial encroachments is military intelligence — the Cartographers just sell it as geography.

**Key facts:**
- Valerion patronizes them for border surveys; Vandris uses them for trade route documentation
- The Fangs maintain an informal relationship with the Guild's lead cartographer — maps are knowledge, and knowledge is the Fangs' currency
- Verath Valerion's border intelligence network and the Cartographers' field surveyors sometimes operate in the same areas and sometimes trip over each other
- House Drakenoth has tried to acquire the Guild's archive of magical anomaly maps; the Guild has refused three times

**Notable figures:**
- **Guild Master Marek Coldbrook** 📋 — Head cartographer; name from registry (#19 / #231); has personally mapped more of the empire's border than anyone alive; knows things about the southern routes Verran Vandris doesn't
- **Field Surveyor Grav Ashford** 📋 — Most active surveyor in the current roster; name from registry (#76 / #244); the one who brings back maps from places Valerion doesn't know are in trouble yet

## The Alchemists' Union

**Type:** Specialist Practitioners' Guild
**Alignment:** Drakenoth-adjacent; jealously independent
**Headquarters:** The Distillation Quarter, capital

Handles potions, compounds, magical residues, and the things that will-based magic can't cover cheaply or reliably at scale. The Arcanists' Circle handles licensed magic; the Alchemists handle the physical infrastructure of magical chemistry. They are different enough that each body insists it doesn't need the other, and both are lying.

**Key facts:**
- Javelin's poisons, darts, and trinkets likely come through Union-adjacent suppliers — Fang-trained operatives maintain these connections
- Emerick Mercurial's overactive magical core was probably assessed by a Union alchemist at some point — the compounds he uses to self-medicate are not standard court medicine
- Drakenoth tries to regulate the Union through the Circle; the Union's position is that alchemy is chemistry, not magic, and outside the Circle's jurisdiction; this argument has been ongoing for 200 years

**Notable figures:**
- **Union Guildmaster Dorvyn Esthorn** 📋 — Head of the Union; name from registry (#24 / #190); the 200-year argument's current champion; has won every jurisdictional ruling so far
- **Senior Compounder Thel Cinderwick** 📋 — Lead specialist in magical-core chemistry; name from registry (#172 / #237); the person Emerick's physician consults, quietly

## The Keeper's Faith

**Type:** Religious Institution
**Alignment:** Officially neutral; practically every faction wants to control it
**Headquarters:** The House of Passing, capital; sanctuaries throughout the empire

The empire's organized death-religion — keepers of memory, caretakers of the dead, ritual practitioners for the Valdross (autumn) season's observances. They claim no political alignment, which makes them a prize. Whoever controls the Keeper's Faith controls the narratives told about the dead — including dead emperors, dead battles, and dead alliances.

**Key facts:**
- Empress Valdra's funeral was conducted by the Faith; they hold her burial records, which are considered sacred and sealed
- The Faith's autumn observances are the one event all Ten Houses attend without political maneuvering — officially
- Lady Evrath Thessar (Grand Archivist) and the Faith's High Keeper maintain a tense collegial relationship; they both keep records and both consider the other's records incomplete
- Arctus donated the land for three Faith sanctuaries in Year 1325 PR, which many interpreted as either genuine piety or a calculated relationship investment; no one knows which

**Notable figures:**
- **High Keeper Lorvaine Velthorn** 📋 — Head of the Faith; name from registry (#112 / #176); the only person in the capital who speaks to every house with equal authority and owes nothing to any of them; this makes her either very powerful or very endangered
- **Memory Keeper Issa Duskford** 📋 — Keeper of imperial burial records; name from registry (#175 / #228); the one who knows what Valdra's records actually say

## The Wanderers

**Type:** Independent Network / Intelligence-adjacent
**Alignment:** Unknown — possibly Morghaven; possibly genuinely independent
**Headquarters:** None permanent; the Wanderers don't have one

Traveling merchants, entertainers, couriers, and the occasional vagrant scholar who moves through the empire without permanent residence. They are how rumors actually travel between cities — faster than official post, harder to trace, and invisible to formal intelligence networks because they don't look like a network.

**Key facts:**
- Every intelligence body in the empire suspects the Wanderers are someone else's asset; none has proven it
- Morghaven's Vraeven (the house's true operational center) maintains a handler relationship with at least one Wanderer cell; whether this means Morghaven controls the Wanderers or just uses part of it is unknown
- The Fists and the Wanderers overlap in membership — the same person may carry trade goods through one city and perform a Fists contract in the next
- The Prologue's third figure (cat-eyed youth who arrived through a spatial rip) may have Wanderer origins or connections

**Notable figures:**
- **The name "The Wanderers" is what outsiders call them** — internally they use no collective name
- **Tov Blackfen** 📋 — Known Wanderer contact; name from registry (#88 / #234); operates in the capital during Valdross season; Vraeven Morghaven's most reliable source for market-level intelligence
- **Sora Ravensford** 📋 — Southern route operative; name from registry (#168 / #238); may know what's happening on the Vandris trade routes before Verran does

## The Reclaimers

**Type:** Religious / Recovery Faction
**Alignment:** Border communities; Valerion-adjacent; officially under no house
**Headquarters:** Distributed across the frontier territories

Born in the aftermath of the wars 15 years ago. The Reclaimers work in border territories to restore abandoned settlements, recover lost archives, and — in their own framing — "return to the empire what the empire lost." They are part humanitarian, part religious, part political argument: their existence implies the wars left things broken that the government has not fixed.

**Key facts:**
- Lord Gavren Valerion has a complex relationship with the Reclaimers — they operate in his territory, they do work his house cannot resource, and their presence is a constant reminder that the border needs more attention than the capital gives it
- The Scholars' Compact has an ongoing project to recover and archive whatever the Reclaimers find; the collaboration is politically useful for both bodies
- The Keeper's Faith and the Reclaimers sometimes work the same sites — the Faith for the dead, the Reclaimers for the living; they have developed a rough division of labor
- Lady Kessa Ironwell has quietly funded Reclaimer reconstruction projects through Ironwell supply contracts — an investment in goodwill in territories that Ironwell eventually wants to build in

**Notable figures:**
- **Reclaimer-General Garven Stonedrift** 📋 — Operational head of the frontier recovery efforts; name from registry (#26 / #241); a former soldier who served under Kraeven Kavaren in the wars; chose recovery work over returning to the military
- **Archivist-in-Field Bryn Grimwell** 📋 — The Compact's embedded representative; name from registry (#170 / #245); Aldenn Thessar's primary field contact
