## The Government

### The Emperor

- **Title:** Emperor Arctus — "first among equals, first to battle, first sacrifice for the empire"
- **Power:** Not absolute — he maneuvers around court while keeping family and marriage in line
- **Senate check:** Supermajority (7/9 Houses) can override his veto
- **Philosophy:** Moral disengagement — systematically uses family and subordinates as tools for absolution
- **Reign:** 15 years as of 1335 PR
- **15 years ago:** Devastating wars and failed imperial pregnancies hollowed out the imperial family

### The Lord High Steward (Brax)

Brax is **not** Prime Minister. He is the Lord High Steward — a Great Officer distinction:

- Manages the Imperial Household, court protocols, schedule, and access to the Emperor
- Controls the flow of documents, summons, audiences, and informal negotiations between ministers
- On paper: oversees palace, staff, ceremonies, the Emperor's personal estates
- In practice: everything passes through his hands — he pulls strings without holding a portfolio
- High-noble tradition: an heir can be Lord High Steward but may not hold a ministerial seat
- The Stewardship is "officially apolitical, secretly where all the knives are kept"

## Magic

### Core Principle

Magic is **will-based** — users shape reality through focused will. Individual signatures exist. No pure-blood requirement, but breeding creates variance in ability.

**Foci:** Wands are used. Both verbal and non-verbal casting possible.

### Sensory Signatures (use consistently in prose)

Every act of magic has 2–3 sensory tells. Skill level determines how obvious or subtle:

| Sense | Tell |
|-------|------|
| Temperature | Change — heat or cold depending on type |
| Sound | Spatial rip (especially for blink/teleportation) |
| Visual | Distortion — beginner = obvious shimmer, expert = barely perceptible |

### Known Techniques

| Technique | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Blink | Short-range teleportation |
| Illusions | Reality distortion, sensory manipulation |
| Transformation | Altering appearance of self or objects |
| Mind influence | Subtle — Adept level required |
| Life/death boundary magic | Emperor's level — can prevent death, trap someone at the brink |

## Characters — Established Facts

### Raxz (Prince Regent)

- Age: 21
- Sexuality: Bisexual
- In love with Javelin (his mentor, an older man)
- Engaged in arranged courtship with Lady Marcella Kalvennor
- Personality: Politically naive, impulsive, resentful of court expectations, emotionally volatile
- Inciting incident: Used magic at the imperial gala to transform his and Lady Marcella's attire mid-dance without her consent — breach of etiquette and bodily autonomy
- Magic ability: Will-based, functional but not subtle (publicly visible incident confirms this)
- Currently: Summoned to Emperor Arctus's office to face consequences

### Emperor Arctus

- Ruler for 15 years
- Character philosophy: Four lessons on moral disengagement:
  1. "I don't dirty my own hands, not even for my own family" — delegation as power
  2. "That's why I have people" — power requires infrastructure
  3. "I dirty my hands elsewhere so that I can clean them on my family" — the transactional reveal
  4. "My sins are their blessings" — complete moral inversion
- Uses a maid as Raxz's recurring "whipping figure" — brought in whenever Arctus wants to drive home a lesson; she is punished in Raxz's place, creating Pavlovian conditioning
- Capable of magic at the life/death boundary — can cast a spell preventing someone from fully dying
- The impossible choice he creates for Raxz: kill the maid (be noble but become a killer) OR refuse (preserve innocence but leave someone to endless suffering)

### Javelin

- Raxz's combat and magic mentor
- Raxz's lover
- Caught between love for Raxz and duty to his role
- Under surveillance by Faust and Brax
- Legal son of Brax (Brax is his legal father)
- After the gala incident: spotted holding Princess Millie, showing hurt and judgment on his face
- Currently: at risk of Faust arranging a forced marriage for him

### Brax (Lord High Steward)

- Javelin's legal father
- Manages behind-the-scenes damage control after the gala
- Watches fallout with detached humor and professional concern
- Spotted Javelin holding Princess Millie — whispered "Javelin. I'm so sorry" from a distance (guilt and regret)
- Secretly summoned Lord Emerick (Finances) and Lord Bastyon (Military Affairs) for separate meetings
- Being manipulated by Faust
- The torches went out behind him as he left the Ministry — established visual motif: corruption, secrecy, the cost of serving the Dominion

### Faust

- Antagonist operating from the shadows
- Intercepts Brax in the palace gardens after the gala
- Aware of Javelin's emotional entanglement with Raxz
- Proposes to "arrange a wife" for Javelin — a control mechanism
- Manipulates both Brax and Javelin
- True goals and methods not yet fully revealed

### Lord Emerick (Minister of Treasury)

- Brilliant financial administrator
- Has an overactive magical core
- Self-medicates with alcohol
- Met secretly by Brax after the gala

### Lady Marcella Kalvennor

- Raxz's arranged courtship partner
- Magic was used on her without consent — her attire transformed mid-dance
- Participated in House Kalvennor's coordinated silent exit from the gala
- Character depth not yet fully established — needs development

### Princess Millie

- Imperial family member
- Was being held by Javelin after the gala
- Role in story not yet fully established

## Established Story Events (in order)

1. **The Burning of Sec-tra** (Prologue, Year 1 PR) — conspirators orchestrate the destruction of the capital to manipulate the Emperor. A youth arrives through spatial magic. Wider implications hinted.

2. **The Imperial Gala** (Chapter 1, 1335 PR) — Raxz transforms his and Lady Marcella's attire using magic without consent. House Kalvennor executes a coordinated silent exit. Emperor Arctus leaves his own gala immediately after.

3. **The Fallout** (Chapter 2) — Raxz summoned to Emperor's office. Javelin spotted holding Princess Millie, showing hurt. Brax begins damage control.

4. **Brax's POV** (Chapter 3) — Brax watches from a distance, manages logistics, contacts Emerick and Bastyon secretly. Faust intercepts in the gardens, proposes forcing Javelin into marriage. Torches go out behind Brax as he leaves. Chapter 3 deliberately delays the Raxz-Arctus confrontation to build tension.
