# Prologue — The Burning of Sec-tra

---

As the fires bellowed and the destruction of Sec-tra filled the night sky, the birthplace of the once-powerful Altonian Dynasty stood burning. For [**TBD — world name**] years, the people of [**TBD — people name**] had forged the first form of unified government from seven fractured kingdoms. Centuries of infighting had followed — the respected nobility and the common alike forming factions and alliances, only to turn on each other like the parasites they were. Neither the nobility of magical descent, nor those of martial prowess, nor those who relied on the money-lending trades had ever managed what was happening now. Many times had the heart of this land tasted the purge of fire, and yet the bones had always refused to give in — what came after always rose stronger. But while the world evolved, a quiet disruption in the flow of power had begun to appear, and many of the realms were starting to feel it.

A sudden thunder wave rolled over Gallows Hills. Two figures stood and watched the destruction unfold. Screams of terror and horror filled the night as flames enveloped the city and transfigured into various forms — running rampant through the streets, exploding whenever they reached a home or a business.

They watched with a detached sense of curiosity, as if this were nothing more than a simple test.

"Were you sure to tell the Fists not to go overboard and leave some of the citizenry alive..." — said one of the figures.

"Well, I... I may have forgotten a couple of details. But I'm sure they'll find a happy middle ground. I did tell them they would have to help with the reconstruction if we were understaffed — and I didn't mean admin level." — responded the other, still cloaked in darkness, only a single dancing light reflecting from the pommel of his sword visible.

---

A sudden chill passed through the meadow. Both figures reacted simultaneously — one pulled a wand from beneath his cap while the other did the same, a knife held in ready combat.

"The Emperor won't be happy. Now will he. You know how much he hates fighting..." — said a slim, younger-looking figure with dark black hair and almost cat-like eyes, stepping out of what appeared to be a tear in the fabric of the air itself. The figure dropped to one knee and spoke. "My apologies for the little joke — your mutual friend insisted on the jab." He said this with an impish grin. Then his tone shifted. "But. He is made. He is down there leading the forces, and he is winning despite the overwhelming numbers. If you ask me — if the plan was to make him more of a hero, you're doing a good job."

---

The three figures stood a few more minutes as the final barks of a dog died down in the distance. Without a word, the first of them raised his wand and slashed through the air. An audible rip was heard. A cold wind blew through, chilling the air for a moment. As the ripples at the edges swirled, he looked back and said: "Let's hope the Emperor doesn't find his grit too fast." He laughed as he stepped through the rip.

"He doesn't know who is really moving the field, does he?" said the younger man, facing the sunrise.

"No. And neither do I, really..." — said the remaining figure, rubbing the handle of his sword suggestively. "Whoever is behind this can only be after one of two things — either a seat at the table..." He stopped. He looked at the younger man. "...or they want the throne itself."
