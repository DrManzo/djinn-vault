# Characters — Dominion of Pyraxis

> Established profiles. Check here before writing any character's voice or scene.

## Raxz — Prince Regent (Protagonist)

**Age:** 21
**Role:** Prince Regent, heir to Emperor Arctus
**Sexuality:** Bisexual — in love with Javelin, in arranged courtship with Lady Marcella Kalvennor
**Magic:** Will-based — currently impulsive caster; techniques: transformation, illusion, blink

### Personality
- Impulsive and politically naive
- Resentful of court expectations
- Emotionally raw — feelings read on his face
- Capable of brilliant instinct but terrible judgment under pressure

### Background
- Grew up under Emperor Arctus — received his "four lessons" about moral disengagement through a systematic program of forced complicity
- A maid has been used as his unofficial proxy victim — every lesson Arctus delivers uses her as the punishment vessel
- In love with Javelin, his mentor and Brax's son, but the relationship is bounded by duty and surveillance

### Arc
Raxz's arc is about what the empire breeds. Every pressure the Dominion puts on him either destroys a piece of himself or forces him to destroy a piece of his principles. The central question: can he remain noble in a system designed to extract compliance?

### Key Scene — The Impossible Choice
Emperor Arctus delivers the fourth lesson: casts a spell preventing the maid from dying, then leaves Raxz to finish what was started. Kill her (become a killer, preserve her from suffering) or refuse (maintain innocence, abandon her to indefinite agony). Either way a part of Raxz dies.

## Javelin — Mentor (Supporting Lead)

**Age:** 21
**Role:** Raxz's combat and magic mentor; Brax's son
**Sexuality:** Bisexual — returns Raxz's feelings but holds strict boundaries

### Personality
- Maintains discipline even at personal cost
- Face shows hurt and judgment — poor at hiding emotion despite training
- Under constant surveillance by Faust
- Torn between love and duty

### Background
- Legally Brax's son
- Fang-level training (Theo-tier intelligence operative background)
- Warned Raxz about "golden cages" before the gala — Millie's silver hair triggers Raxz's memory of this warning

### Arc
Faust knows about Javelin's emotional entanglement with Raxz and has proposed to Brax that a wife be arranged for Javelin. This threat — of being married off as a political tool — mirrors what Raxz faces with Marcella.

## Brax — Lord High Steward (Supporting Lead)

**Role:** Lord High Steward of the Dominion — Great Officer, not a cabinet minister
**Legal relation:** Javelin's father

### What the role means
On paper: manages the Imperial Household, palace staff, ceremonies, court schedule, access to the Emperor, and the Emperor's personal estates.

In practice: every document, summons, audience, and informal negotiation passes through his hands — which means he pulls strings in the cabinet without holding a portfolio. The Stewardship is where all the knives are kept.

He can influence policy without being responsible for it. He can manipulate ministers without giving orders. He maintains plausible deniability while operating every string.

### Personality
- Cool, detached humor as a mask
- Professional concern underneath
- Guilt visible when he sees Javelin — whispered "Javelin. I'm so sorry" from a distance in Chapter 3
- Operates through clerks, whispers, and favors — trades on perception, access, and personal debts

### Background
- Watched Raxz grow up — guilt over his role in Raxz's upbringing
- Operates simultaneously under the Emperor's trust AND Faust's manipulation
- The fractured alliance between Arctus and Brax traces back to the Big Brain battle (see STORY-NOTES) — Arctus overriding Brax's nested barrier was the seed of lasting distrust

### Chapter 3 Arc
Spots Javelin holding Princess Millie at the gala — guilt. Uses a court clerk to secretly summon Lord Emerick (Finances) and Lord Bastyon (Military Affairs) for separate damage control meetings. Intercepted by Faust in the gardens. Meets Emerick — brilliant financial administrator with an overactive magical core who self-medicates with alcohol. Chapter ends with torches going out behind Brax as he leaves the Ministry.

## Emperor Arctus

**Role:** Emperor — "first among equals, first to battle, first sacrifice for the empire"
**Current year of reign:** 15th year (as of Year 1335 PR)

### Personality
- Stopped smiling 15 years ago after the wars and failed pregnancies
- Burdened, aging, pragmatic about the empire's survival
- Coldly pedagogical — treats moral disengagement as a skill to be taught
- Left his own gala immediately after Raxz's incident — signaling disapproval with restraint

### The Four Lessons (delivered over time to Raxz)
1. "I don't dirty my own hands, not even for my own family." — delegation as power and protection
2. "That's why I have people." — power requires infrastructure of complicity
3. "I dirty my hands elsewhere so that I can clean them on my family." — the transactional reveal
4. "My sins are their blessings." — complete moral inversion; family as a tool for self-absolution

The four lessons are delivered gradually, creating a boiling-frog effect where each piece is normalized before the next arrives. The final torture scene brings all four together in a forced moment of complicity.

### Background
- Fought in the devastating wars 15 years ago — hollowed out the imperial family
- Series of failed pregnancies hollowed out the succession
- Conducted the Big Brain battle alongside Brax — overrode Brax's nested barrier with a final destructive procedure, seeding lasting distrust

## Faust (The Advisor)

**Role:** Unknown — operates in the shadows, manipulates both Brax and Javelin
**Physical:** Diminutive — "somewhere between a dwarf and a midget"; the only person who can genuinely unsettle Javelin
**Signature:** Appears/vanishes with shimmer of air and sulfur smell (pop)

- Is family to Brax (literal or deep-bond — not yet specified which)
- Has held Brax under his control for 21 years (since Brax was approximately 9-10 years old)
- Trained Javelin in survival, knife work, and limited magic — under contract to Brax's father
- Claims Brax's father sent him out of love for Javelin
- Says Javelin "is one of us" — implying deeper allegiance or lineage
- Proposes arranging a wife for Javelin to sever his emotional bond with Raxz
- Connection to the fictional Faust inner figure (see Black Book) may be intentional psychological mirror
