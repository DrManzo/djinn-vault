## Research Queries — Run All of These

### Foundations
1. **Behavioral psychology** — classical conditioning (Pavlov), operant conditioning (Skinner), reinforcement schedules, behavior modification, applied behavioral analysis, habit formation loops
2. **Cognitive psychology** — mental models, cognitive biases (full taxonomy: confirmation bias, availability heuristic, dunning-kruger, etc.), memory systems, attention, problem-solving frameworks
3. **CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)** — core model (thoughts → feelings → behavior), cognitive distortions (all 15), ABC model, thought records, behavioral activation, evidence base

### Depth Psychology
4. **Freud — core framework** — id/ego/superego, conscious/unconscious/preconscious, psychosexual stages, defense mechanisms (repression, projection, rationalization, sublimation, denial, displacement), dream interpretation basics, transference/countertransference
5. **Jung — core framework** — collective unconscious, archetypes (Self, Shadow, Anima/Animus, Persona, Trickster, Hero), individuation process, psychological types (intro/extroversion, four functions), synchronicity, the transcendent function
6. **Shadow work** — what the shadow is (Jungian definition), how it forms, shadow projection, active imagination technique, shadow integration methods, common shadow patterns, shadow work journaling frameworks
7. **Jordan Peterson — key ideas** — Maps of Meaning (meaning vs chaos/order), 12 Rules for Life (each rule summarized), Big Five personality (OCEAN) and its applications, Jungian influence on Peterson, the Logos/Dragon archetype, responsibility as antidote to suffering

### Applied Psychology
8. **Addiction psychology** — neurological basis (dopamine reward pathway, nucleus accumbens), addiction cycle (trigger-craving-use-consequence), tolerance and withdrawal, behavioral addictions vs substance, recovery models (AA 12-step, SMART Recovery, harm reduction), relapse prevention
9. **Attachment theory** — Bowlby's model, four attachment styles (secure, anxious, avoidant, disorganized), how childhood attachment shapes adult relationships, earned security, attachment in therapy
10. **Social psychology essentials** — conformity (Asch), obedience (Milgram), group dynamics, in-group/out-group bias, bystander effect, social identity theory, persuasion principles (Cialdini's 6)
11. **Emotional intelligence** — Goleman's model (self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills), measuring EQ, practical development, EQ vs IQ in outcomes
12. **Trauma psychology** — types of trauma (acute, chronic, complex, developmental), PTSD symptoms and neurobiology, somatic responses, polyvagal theory (Porges), trauma-informed approaches, EMDR basics

### Self-Understanding Tools
13. **Personality frameworks comparison** — Big Five (OCEAN) vs MBTI vs Enneagram: validity, use cases, strengths/weaknesses of each, how to use them practically without over-identifying
14. **Cognitive distortions — applied guide** — all 15 distortions with real-world examples, how to identify them in self and others, reframing techniques for each
