## Cover
- Types of trauma: acute (single event), chronic (repeated), complex/developmental (childhood relational), intergenerational
- PTSD symptoms: intrusion (flashbacks, nightmares), avoidance, negative cognition/mood, hyperarousal — DSM-5 criteria
- C-PTSD (complex PTSD): what distinguishes it from PTSD, affect dysregulation, altered self-perception, relationship difficulties
- Neurobiology: hippocampus (memory consolidation impaired), amygdala (hyperactive threat detection), prefrontal cortex (goes offline under threat)
- Polyvagal theory (Porges): ventral vagal (safe/connected), sympathetic (fight/flight), dorsal vagal (freeze/shutdown) — the three states and how to move between them
- Somatic responses: trauma stored in the body — muscle tension, chronic pain, freeze/fawn responses
- EMDR: eye movement desensitization and reprocessing — how it works, evidence base, 8-phase protocol
- Trauma-informed care: understanding before diagnosing, safety, trustworthiness, peer support, collaboration, empowerment
- Recovery: it's not about forgetting — it's about integration, nervous system regulation, narrative meaning-making
