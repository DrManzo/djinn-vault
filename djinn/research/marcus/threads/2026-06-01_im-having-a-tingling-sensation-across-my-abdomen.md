# i’m having a tingling sensation across my abdomen

Tingling across the abdomen can come from irritated or compressed nerves in the abdominal wall, but it can also happen with anxiety, muscle strain, or less commonly a deeper medical problem. Some nerve-entrapment conditions can cause tingling, numbness, or burning in a localized area of the belly.

When to get urgent help

Get urgent care now if the tingling is happening with severe abdominal pain, fever, repeated vomiting, fainting, chest pain, trouble breathing, new weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, or a rash, especially on one side of the body. New or worsening numbness with these symptoms can signal something more serious.

What it might be

If the sensation is on the skin or in a strip across part of the abdomen, a nerve issue is more likely than a stomach problem. Nerve compression or entrapment can cause tingling, numbness, sharp pain, or increased sensitivity, and anxiety can also cause body tingling plus stomach symptoms.

What to do next

If this is mild and you otherwise feel okay, monitor it for a short time and note whether it changes with twisting, bending, sitting up, eating, stress, or touch. Because abdominal tingling is not something to ignore if it persists, a clinician should check it if it lasts more than a day or two, keeps recurring, or is paired with pain or other symptoms.
