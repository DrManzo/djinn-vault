Practical considerations

Recycling filament is possible but not perfect; extruders, shredders, and dryers add cost and complexity, and recycled filament can be lower or less consistent in quality than commercial stock. For this reason, many users either blend recycled material with virgin pellets or use recycling mainly for prototyping and non-structural parts.
