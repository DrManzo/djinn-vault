Adding Adhesives

While the Ender 3 V3 Plus comes with a textured PEI-like build surface that should work without additives, you may want to use adhesion helpers depending on your filament type:

Glue stick (like Elmer's) - Apply a thin, even layer for extra adhesion, especially useful for PLA and PETG

Additional Tips for New Filament

Since your friend gave you filament, ensure it's dry before printing, especially if it's been opened or stored without desiccant. Set your bed temperature appropriately: 50-60°C for PLA, 80-90°C for PETG. If you still experience adhesion issues after cleaning and leveling, enable a brim in your slicer software to increase the contact area with the bed.
