## 1. Budgeting Systems

Budgeting systems differ mainly by how much flexibility, precision, and behavioral structure they impose.[cite:283][cite:295] The best system depends on income stability, debt pressure, and whether you need simplicity or tight control.[cite:295][cite:296]

### Main systems
- **50/30/20**: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% saving/debt payoff. Best for stable income and quick rule-of-thumb planning.[cite:283]
- **Zero-based budgeting**: every dollar gets a job before the month begins. Best for variable income and tight control.[cite:283]
- **Envelope / category caps**: assign hard limits to spending buckets, often cash or digital equivalents. Best for overspending control.[cite:283]
- **Pay yourself first**: automate savings/investing before discretionary spending. Best for people who struggle to save consistently.[cite:283][cite:296]

### Practical fit for Javier
Because your income has been modest and sometimes variable, zero-based budgeting plus pay-yourself-first is likely the strongest operating model.[cite:295][cite:296] It gives tighter control while preserving your existing habit of moving money into savings and investing on purpose.[cite:284][cite:295]

## 2. Saving Strategies

Saving strategy is about liquidity, resilience, and automation rather than raw hoarding.[cite:283][cite:298] Emergency funds, sinking funds, and automated transfers serve different purposes and should not be mixed mentally.[cite:298][cite:304]

### Core ideas
- **Emergency fund**: usually 3 months of essential expenses for stable earners; 6 months for volatile income or self-employment.[cite:283][cite:290]
- **Sinking funds**: separate buckets for predictable large expenses like taxes, repairs, insurance, and equipment.[cite:283]
- **HYSA**: best for emergency liquidity; prioritize FDIC/NCUA safety and access speed over tiny rate differences.[cite:283]
- **CD laddering**: useful only for money that truly does not need to stay fully liquid.[cite:283]

### Practical fit for Javier
As a California self-employed builder growing Typhon’s Forge, a 6-month core emergency target plus separate tax and equipment sinking funds is safer than a bare minimum reserve.[cite:290][cite:285][cite:293]

## 3. Debt Management

Debt payoff strategy depends on whether the problem is math, behavior, or cash-flow fragility.[cite:283][cite:299] Avalanche minimizes interest by attacking the highest APR first, while snowball maximizes psychological momentum by clearing the smallest balances first.[cite:283]

### Key rules
- Use **avalanche** if you are disciplined and focused on cost minimization.[cite:283]
- Use **snowball** if behavior and motivation are the bigger problem.[cite:283]
- Compare investing vs payoff by after-tax return and guaranteed debt cost; high-interest debt usually wins.[cite:283]
- Protect credit by paying on time, even if making extra payoff elsewhere.[cite:283]

### Practical fit for Javier
Since you want debt-managed growth rather than reckless leverage, debt should be used only where business ROI is plausible and monthly payments remain easy under conservative income assumptions.[cite:285][cite:299][cite:302]

## 4. Credit Score Mechanics

Credit scores reflect repayment behavior, utilization, account age, credit mix, and new credit activity.[cite:283][cite:303] The widely used FICO weighting is approximately payment history 35%, utilization 30%, length 15%, mix 10%, and new credit 10%.[cite:283]

## 8. Options Basics

Options are contracts giving the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying security at a strike price before expiration.[cite:283] They are tools, not magic leverage, and broker approval levels plus strategy knowledge matter enormously.[cite:283]

### Basics
- **Call**: right to buy.[cite:283]
- **Put**: right to sell.[cite:283]
- **Premium**: price paid for the option.[cite:283]
- **Strike**: contract exercise price.[cite:283]
- **Expiration**: time limit.[cite:283]
- **Greeks**: delta, theta, vega help explain sensitivity to price, time decay, and volatility.[cite:283]

### Safer beginner structures
- Covered calls
- Cash-secured puts

### Compliance note
Options trading requires broker approval levels and may trigger additional suitability or risk disclosures under broker-dealer rules.[cite:283]

## 9. 7-Day Speculation Framework

Short-term speculation should be treated as a small, rules-based tactical process rather than investing.[cite:283] Professionals look for catalysts, define invalidation points in advance, size small, and refuse to confuse a trade with a long-term thesis.[cite:283]

### Core framework
- Identify catalyst: earnings, Fed, CPI, jobs, sector news.[cite:283]
- Check pre-market context and sector trend.[cite:283]
- Define entry, target, stop, and size before entering.[cite:283]
- Favor minimum risk/reward of 1:2 or 1:3.[cite:283]
- Risk only a small fraction of capital per trade.[cite:283]

## 13. Federal Income Tax Structure

Federal tax analysis starts with understanding gross income, adjusted gross income (AGI), taxable income, and the distinction between ordinary income and preferentially taxed income.[cite:283] The standard-vs-itemized decision, above-the-line deductions, and income type classification can materially change effective tax burden.[cite:283]

- **W-2 / 1099 / K-1** income each interacts differently with withholding, deductions, and filing complexity.[cite:283]

## 14. Investment Tax Rules

Tax drag can destroy returns if ignored.[cite:283] The most important ideas are holding period, wash-sale awareness, qualified dividends, and cost-basis control.[cite:283]

### Core rules
- **Short-term capital gains** are taxed like ordinary income.[cite:283]
- **Long-term capital gains** get preferential federal rates after the required holding period.[cite:283]
- **Wash sale rule** can disallow a loss if substantially identical securities are repurchased inside the 30-day window.[cite:283]
- **Qualified dividends** generally receive better tax treatment than ordinary dividends if holding requirements are met.[cite:283]

Tax-loss harvesting helps only if done with record discipline and without accidental wash sales.[cite:283]
