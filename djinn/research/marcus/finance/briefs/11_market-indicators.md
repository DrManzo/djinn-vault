## Cover
- VIX (CBOE Volatility Index): measures expected 30-day volatility of S&P 500, derived from options prices. Under 20 = calm, 20-30 = elevated, 30+ = fear, 40+ = extreme fear. Inverse relationship with market
- Yield curve: plot of Treasury yields across maturities. Normal (long rates > short rates), inverted (short > long — signals recession 6-18 months out). 10yr-2yr spread is the key metric
- Fed funds rate and market: when Fed raises rates, growth stocks fall hardest (discount rate effect), bonds fall, banks benefit. Watch FOMC meeting dates
- CPI (Consumer Price Index): month-over-month and year-over-year inflation. Hot CPI = more Fed hikes = bad for stocks. How to read the release
- PPI (Producer Price Index): leading indicator for CPI, measures wholesale inflation
- Earnings season calendar: 4x/year, concentrated in weeks after each quarter ends. Market most volatile
- Economic calendar: GDP release, jobs report (non-farm payrolls), retail sales, ISM manufacturing PMI, housing starts — each one's market impact
- Leading vs lagging indicators: leading (yield curve, stock prices, building permits) vs lagging (unemployment, CPI)
