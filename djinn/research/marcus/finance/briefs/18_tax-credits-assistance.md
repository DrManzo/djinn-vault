# Session 18 — Tax Credits and Assistance Programs

**Cover:**
- EITC (Earned Income Tax Credit): eligibility (income, filing status, children), 2025 credit amounts, refundable — many eligible people don't claim it

- Premium Tax Credit: ACA marketplace insurance, how to estimate and reconcile

- Low-income assistance programs: VITA (free tax prep), LIHEAP, SNAP eligibility, Medicaid/Medi-Cal, Section 8 — how to find and apply
