## Cover
- EITC (Earned Income Tax Credit): income limits by filing status and number of children, credit amounts, refundable (get money back even if no tax owed), most missed credit by eligible low-income filers
- Child Tax Credit: $2,000 per child under 17 (2025), partially refundable (Additional CTC up to $1,600), phase-out above $200k single/$400k joint
- Saver's Credit (Retirement Savings Contributions Credit): 10-50% credit on first $2,000 of retirement contributions, income limits ~$36,500 single (2025), non-refundable
- Premium Tax Credit: ACA marketplace health insurance subsidy, calculated on household income as % of FPL, reconciled on tax return
- CA EITC (CalEITC): separate from federal, lower income thresholds, also covers self-employment income — detail CA amounts
- LIHEAP: low-income home energy assistance, federal program administered by states, income limits, how to apply
- SNAP (food assistance): federal, income below 130% FPL, asset limits, apply at county DHS
- Medi-Cal: CA Medicaid, expanded under ACA, no asset test for adults under 65, income threshold 138% FPL
- Free tax prep: VITA (Volunteer Income Tax Assistance) sites for income <$67k, AARP Tax-Aide, IRS Free File

⚠️ Educational only. Program rules and income limits change annually — verify current figures.
