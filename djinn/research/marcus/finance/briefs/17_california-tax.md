# Session 17 — California State Tax Specifics

**Cover:**
- CA income tax brackets: all nine brackets (CA has the highest top rate in the US at 13.3%)
- CA capital gains: taxed as ordinary income — no preferential rate (unlike federal), impact on investment decisions
- CA SDI: State Disability Insurance, rate, wage base, what it covers
- CA FTB: Franchise Tax Board — filing requirements, CA source income rules for non-residents
- CA LLC fees: $800 minimum franchise tax (due even at zero profit), additional LLC fee schedule based on gross receipts
- CA Prop 19: property tax implications for inherited real estate
- CA estimated taxes: due dates differ slightly from federal, underpayment penalty
- CA vs federal differences: conformity issues, SALT deduction (CA allows full deduction on CA return), community property rules
- Moving in/out of CA: partial year residency, CA source income after leaving
- GitHub: CA tax calculators, FTB resources, CA vs federal comparison tools
