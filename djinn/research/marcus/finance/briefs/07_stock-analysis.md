## Cover
Fundamental analysis:
- P/E ratio: price per share ÷ earnings per share, industry comparison is key, high P/E = growth priced in, value trap risk
- EPS (earnings per share): diluted EPS, YoY growth rate matters more than absolute number
- Revenue growth: organic vs acquisition-driven, consistent double-digit growth signals
- Debt/equity ratio: leverage, when high debt is dangerous vs manageable
- Free cash flow: net income is an opinion, cash flow is a fact — why FCF is the truest measure of health
- Economic moat: Buffett's concept — competitive advantages that protect profits (brand, switching costs, network effects, low cost)

Technical analysis:
- Support/resistance levels: price floors and ceilings, how to draw them
- Moving averages: 50-day and 200-day MA, golden cross vs death cross
- RSI (Relative Strength Index): 0-100 scale, overbought (>70), oversold (<30)
- MACD: trend direction and momentum, signal line crossovers
- Volume: price moves on high volume = conviction, low volume = suspect

When to use each: fundamental = what to buy, technical = when to buy/sell
