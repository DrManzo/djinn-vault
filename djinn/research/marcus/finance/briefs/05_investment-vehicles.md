## Cover
- Stocks: ownership stake in a company, dividends + capital appreciation, voting rights, risk profile (individual stocks = high volatility)
- Bonds: loans to governments/corporations, fixed interest, lower risk than stocks, inverse relationship with interest rates, types (Treasury, corporate, municipal — munis often tax-free)
- ETFs: basket of securities traded like a stock, intraday pricing, typically low expense ratios, tax efficiency (in-kind creation/redemption)
- Index funds: tracks an index (S&P 500, total market), passive management, expense ratios typically 0.03-0.20%, Jack Bogle's thesis
- Mutual funds: actively managed (most), higher fees, end-of-day pricing, most 401k options are mutual funds
- REITs: real estate investment trusts, required to distribute 90% of income, dividend-heavy, publicly traded or private, tax treatment (REIT dividends taxed as ordinary income)
- Options: contracts (not covered here — see separate session)
- Comparing risk/return/liquidity: matrix showing each vehicle's risk level, typical returns, liquidity, tax treatment, minimum investment
