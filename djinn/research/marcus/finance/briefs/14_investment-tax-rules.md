## Cover
- Short-term vs long-term capital gains: < 1 year = ordinary income rates (up to 37%), ≥ 1 year = preferential rates (0%, 15%, or 20% depending on income)
- 2025 LTCG rates: 0% up to ~$47,025 (single), 15% up to ~$518,900, 20% above — get current thresholds
- Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT): 3.8% additional tax on investment income for high earners (>$200k single, $250k MFJ)
- Wash sale rule: cannot sell a security at a loss and repurchase the same or substantially identical security within 30 days before OR after the sale. Loss is disallowed. Applies across accounts including IRAs
- Tax-loss harvesting: deliberately selling losing positions to offset gains, replacing with similar (not identical) funds, improves after-tax returns
- Qualified dividends vs ordinary dividends: qualified dividends taxed at LTCG rates, ordinary at income rates. Requirements: held >60 days, from qualifying US/foreign companies
- Cost basis methods: FIFO (default), specific identification (best for tax optimization), average cost (common for mutual funds). Choose at purchase, changing has tax implications
- Dividend reinvestment: each reinvestment creates a new tax lot with its own basis and holding period

⚠️ Educational only. Not tax advice. Tax rules change — verify current IRS guidance.
