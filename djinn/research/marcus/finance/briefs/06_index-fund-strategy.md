## Cover
- Why index funds win: SPIVA data (80-90% of active managers underperform their benchmark over 10+ years)
- Expense ratios: why 1% vs 0.03% makes a catastrophic difference over 30 years (show the math)
- S&P 500 historical returns: ~10% nominal, ~7% inflation-adjusted, per year average
- The three-fund portfolio: US total market (VTI) + international (VXUS) + bonds (BND) — allocation by age and risk tolerance
- Vanguard vs Fidelity vs Schwab: expense ratio comparison for equivalent funds, minimums, account features
- Dollar-cost averaging: invest fixed amount on fixed schedule regardless of market, removes emotion, performs comparably to lump-sum long-term
- When NOT to DCA: if you have a lump sum, lump sum beats DCA ~66% of the time mathematically — but DCA is fine psychologically
- Target-date funds: one-fund solution, auto-rebalancing, good for set-and-forget investors (but check the expense ratio)
- Reinvesting dividends: DRIP (dividend reinvestment plan), compound growth effect
