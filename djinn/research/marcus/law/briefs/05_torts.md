## Cover
- Negligence: duty, breach, causation (actual + proximate), damages — the four-element test
- Reasonable person standard: objective test, what courts actually look for
- Intentional torts: battery, assault, false imprisonment, IIED, trespass to land/chattels, conversion
- Strict liability: abnormally dangerous activities, wild animals, products liability
- Products liability: manufacturing defect, design defect, failure to warn — who can be sued (manufacturer, distributor, retailer)
- Defamation: libel vs slander, public vs private figures, actual malice standard (NYT v Sullivan)
- Invasion of privacy: four types (appropriation, intrusion, false light, public disclosure)
- Defenses: contributory vs comparative negligence, assumption of risk, consent
- Damages: compensatory (special + general), punitive damages and their limits
- Vicarious liability: respondeat superior, employer liability for employee torts
