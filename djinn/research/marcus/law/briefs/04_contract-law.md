## Cover
- Formation: offer, acceptance, consideration — the essential three
- Mutual assent and the objective theory (what would a reasonable person understand?)
- Types of contracts: bilateral vs unilateral, express vs implied, void vs voidable
- Statute of Frauds: which contracts must be in writing (real estate, goods over $500, contracts >1 year)
- Defenses: duress, undue influence, misrepresentation, mistake (mutual vs unilateral), impossibility, frustration of purpose
- Performance: substantial performance doctrine, conditions precedent/subsequent
- Breach: material vs minor breach, anticipatory repudiation
- Remedies: expectation damages, consequential damages, liquidated damages, specific performance, rescission
- UCC vs common law: when each applies (goods vs services), key differences in offer/acceptance rules
