# Session 13 — When You Need a Lawyer vs DIY

Research the practical question of when to hire a lawyer.

**Cover:**
- Situations where DIY is viable: simple LLC formation, basic contracts, small claims court
- Situations where DIY is dangerous: litigation, criminal charges, IP disputes, complex contracts, real estate
- How to find and vet attorneys: State Bar directory, Avvo, Martindale, referrals, initial consultations
- Fee structures: hourly, flat fee, contingency, retainer — when each is appropriate
- Legal aid and low-cost options: legal aid societies, law school clinics, limited scope representation
- Alternative dispute resolution: mediation vs arbitration — costs, binding vs non-binding, when to use
- Online legal services: LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer, Clerky — what they're good for, where they fail
- Self-representation (pro se): rights, risks, how courts treat pro se litigants
