## Cover
- Corporate formation: articles of incorporation, bylaws, registered agent, state of formation (why Delaware)
- Corporate structure: shareholders, board of directors, officers — roles and authority
- Fiduciary duties: duty of care (business judgment rule), duty of loyalty (no self-dealing), duty of candor
- Piercing the corporate veil: when courts hold shareholders personally liable (undercapitalization, commingling)
- Securities law basics: publicly vs privately held, SEC registration requirements, Rule 10b-5 (fraud)
- Mergers and acquisitions: asset purchase vs stock purchase, due diligence, representations and warranties
- Shareholder rights: voting rights, derivative suits, appraisal rights
- Corporate governance: board composition, independent directors, audit committee, executive compensation
- Delaware vs other states: why 60%+ of Fortune 500 incorporate in Delaware (court of chancery, flexibility)
