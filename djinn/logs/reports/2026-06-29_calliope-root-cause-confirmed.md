## Summary

Extended diagnostic session that definitively identified the root cause of all Calliope key561 dropouts: specific XY toolpath positions generated by engraving/emboss geometry pull the nozzle_mcu cable to a stress point at the connector. A cable reseat by Javier restored proper slack. A simple solid Camood (no engraving, maker's mark only) printed to full completion at 106mm with no errors. ## Technical Decisions

**Reverted all workarounds → Why:** Fan cap, thermal soak, 3×3 mesh, and TRSYNC patch were applied to reduce dropout risk but none addressed the actual cause. Thermal soak specifically pre-stressed the connector at temperature before any motion, likely worsening the issue. Stock config confirmed working after cable reseat.

**Gyroid infill confirmed safe for simple solids on Calliope → Why:** The rattling during gyroid infill was toolhead vibration only — not cable stress. Once slack was restored via reseat, rattle disappeared and print completed. Gyroid's risk is only when combined with wide XY toolpaths from engraving geometry that sweep the cable to the stress position.

**Rectilinear/grid for engraved geometry on Calliope → Why:** Engraving paths generate inter-body travel moves and wide sweeping XY motion. Grid infill stays more conservative and was the infill pattern in the one print that previously completed (TTHQ engraved).

**Boolean union before slicing — law established → Why:** 66-body STL generated inter-body travel moves; 1-body merged STL did not. Validated across multiple print attempts. Exception: intentional separate bodies (moving parts, print-in-place) stay separate.

---

## Root Cause — BUG-014 Final Diagnosis

**key561 = cable connector stress at specific XY positions, not thermal, not EMI, not software.**

Sequence: engraving/emboss geometry → wide XY toolpath sweeps → cable pulled to connector stress point → contact loss → Klipper MCU timeout → key561 emergency stop.

key561 errors are **symptoms**, not causes. They do nothing to the hardware. The connector loses contact first; Klipper reacts with key561.

**Fix:** Cable reseat with proper slack routing + correct geometry (single body, no separate engraving shells).

---

## Calliope Print Rules (Validated 2026-06-29)

| Rule | Reason |
|------|--------|
| Single merged body before slicing | Separate shells → inter-body travel → cable stress |
| Gyroid OK for simple solids | Centered paths, cable stays in safe range |
| Rectilinear/grid for engraved/embossed geometry | Reduces wide XY sweeps near cable stress position |
| Exception: moving parts stay separate | Boolean union would fuse them |
| Creality Print on-device slicer only | OrcaSlicer gcode patterns also trigger stress |
