# Puffco Proxy Quad Uptake Recycler — Technical Specifications

## Product Description
The Puffco Proxy Quad Uptake Recycler is a modular, water-conditioned vapor pathway attachment designed to interface with the Puffco Proxy concentrate vaporizer core.[cite:10][cite:20][cite:23] The assembly uses a quad-uptake recycler geometry to circulate water between lower and upper chambers during inhalation, increasing vapor cooling, smoothing airflow, and reducing entrained splash relative to a simple single-pass bubbler path.[cite:5][cite:18][cite:24]

## Intended Function
This attachment serves as a passive post-heater fluidic component only and does not modify the Proxy’s electronics, heater, firmware, or temperature control logic.[cite:10][cite:20][cite:23] Its purpose is to condition aerosol generated by the Proxy through submerged diffusion, multi-channel uplift, phase separation, and gravity-assisted return flow.[cite:5][cite:18]

## Functional Sequence
1. The Proxy core generates vapor within its 3D chamber and routes the vapor stream into the recycler inlet path.[cite:10][cite:20][cite:23]
2. The inlet directs vapor into the lower water reservoir, where submerged diffusion promotes bubble formation and initial cooling.[cite:5][cite:18]
3. Inhalation creates a pressure differential that lifts water and vapor through four parallel uptake tubes into the upper chamber.[cite:5][cite:18][cite:24]
4. In the upper chamber, liquid separates from the vapor stream and drains back to the lower chamber through the return path.[cite:5][cite:18]
5. The conditioned vapor exits through the mouthpiece while the water column resets by gravity between draws.[cite:5][cite:18]

## Engineering Notes
The quad-uptake configuration distributes flow across multiple smaller channels rather than a single large riser, which can improve hydraulic stability and sustain visible recycling at usable inhalation rates.[cite:18][cite:24][cite:25] This geometry also tends to increase local flow velocity within each uptake while preserving an overall draw resistance suitable for hand-operated portable vaporizer use.[cite:5][cite:18][cite:25]

Because the part is printed rather than laboratory-formed borosilicate, dimensional control, wall continuity, and layer bonding are critical to safe operation.[cite:1][cite:2] The design should be treated as a low-temperature vapor accessory rather than a general-purpose heated glass substitute.[cite:1][cite:2][cite:22]

## Safety and Use Constraints
- Use only with the Puffco Proxy or a dimensionally compatible Proxy-format core.[cite:10][cite:20][cite:23]
- Do not expose the printed body to torch flame, stovetop heat, or boiling sanitization unless the selected material is specifically rated for that process.[cite:1][cite:2]
- Do not operate the attachment if cracks, leaks, delamination, or joint looseness are observed.[cite:1][cite:21]
- Fill only to a level that enables recycling without backflow into the Proxy receiver or mouthpiece.[cite:5][cite:18]
- Clean with material-compatible methods because aggressive solvents or excessive heat may degrade printed thermoplastics.[cite:1][cite:2]
