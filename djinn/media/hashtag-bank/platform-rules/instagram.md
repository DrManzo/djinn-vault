# Instagram Hashtag Rules

## Limits & Strategy
- Max: 30 hashtags per post (reels allow up to 30, but 15-20 performs best)
- Optimal mix per post: 2-3 broad + 4-5 mid + 3-4 micro + 1-2 brand
- Never repeat the same hashtag set across consecutive posts (shadowban risk)
- Put hashtags in caption, not comments (Instagram confirmed captions perform better)
- First 3 lines of caption appear before "more" — keep hashtags after a line break

## Known Shadowbanned Tags (avoid or rotate carefully)
#weed #marijuana #cannabis #hemp #420 #bong #dab #edibles #stonerlife
Note: shadowban status changes — check with a fresh account monthly

## Safe Cannabis-Adjacent Tags (lower risk)
#herb #plant #greenlife #elevate #plantlover #botanical #naturallifestyle #conscious #wellness

## Rotation Strategy
- Maintain 4-5 hashtag "sets" in the bank (rotated per post, never reused back-to-back)
- Each set: ~15 tags covering broad/mid/micro/brand proportions
- Swap 3-4 tags between sets to keep rotation fresh

## Reel-Specific Notes
- Reels get boosted by Instagram algorithm regardless of hashtag count
- Quality of content > hashtag volume for Reels
- Cover + hook text matters more than hashtags for Reel reach
