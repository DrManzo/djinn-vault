### Target Market Size and Demographics
- **Identify Key Demographic Segments**: Analyze age, income level, education, and other relevant factors.
- **Estimate Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: Calculate potential market size based on identified segments.

### Competitor Analysis
- **Evaluate Direct and Indirect Competitors**:
  - **Direct Competitors**: Companies offering similar products or services.
  - **Indirect Competitors**: Companies that serve the same customer base but offer different solutions.
  
- **Analyze Strengths, Weaknesses, and Market Share**:
  - Identify key strengths and weaknesses of competitors.
  - Assess their market share to understand competitive positioning.

### Industry Trends with Statistics
- **Review Current Industry Trends**:
  - Stay updated on emerging trends in the industry.
  - Use statistical data from reputable sources to validate insights.
