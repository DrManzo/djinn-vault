# Grounding Techniques for Stress and Anxiety

## Summary
This note provides a detailed guide on physical and mental grounding techniques to help manage stress and anxiety, emphasizing the importance of reconnecting with the present moment.

## Key Points
- 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Exercise
- Feet on the Floor
- The Categories Game

## Details
Grounding techniques are essential tools for managing stress and anxiety. These methods help individuals reconnect with their immediate environment, providing a sense of stability and control in challenging situations.

### Physical Grounding Techniques

**5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Exercise**
This exercise involves identifying:
- 5 things you can see
- 4 things you can touch
- 3 things you can hear
- 2 things you can smell
- 1 thing you can taste

By engaging multiple senses, this technique helps anchor you to the present moment and provides a sense of grounding.

**Feet on the Floor**
Simply place your feet firmly on the ground. Focus on the sensation of connection between your feet and the earth. This exercise reminds you of your physical presence in the world, offering a tangible sense of stability.

### Mental Grounding Techniques

**The Categories Game**
Choose a category (e.g., animals, countries, or movies) and list as many items as you can within that category. This technique redirects your thoughts and provides a helpful distraction, allowing you to focus on something other than your anxiety.
