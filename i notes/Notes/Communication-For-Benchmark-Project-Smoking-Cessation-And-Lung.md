# Communication for Benchmark Project: Smoking Cessation and Lung Cancer Prevention

## Summary
This note outlines the communication to be shared with classmates and professors regarding the chosen project topic, "Smoking Cessation and Lung Cancer Prevention," within a specific community. The goal is to provide a detailed overview of the project's purpose, key areas of focus, and how it aligns with broader health psychology principles.

## Key Points
- Project title: Smoking Cessation and Lung Cancer Prevention
- Purpose: Empower students in health psychology to evaluate real-world prevention plans.
- Focus: Analyze a program aimed at reducing lung cancer incidence through smoking cessation within a specific community.
- Areas of focus: Program design, implementation, outcome evaluation, challenges, and opportunities.

## Details
The purpose of this assignment is to empower undergraduate students in the field of health psychology with the knowledge and skills to critically evaluate and reflect upon real-world prevention plans designed to mitigate the incidence of cancer, chronic illness, and disease. By selecting and analyzing specific prevention plans that target lifestyle changes and risk factors within distinct communities, cultures, or subcultures, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the complex interplay between health psychology principles and culturally diverse populations.

### Project Overview
This project focuses on "Smoking Cessation and Lung Cancer Prevention: Examine a program focused on reducing lung cancer incidence by targeting smoking cessation within a specific community." The study will explore various aspects, including:

- **Program Design:** Analyzing the strategies employed to encourage smoking cessation.
- **Implementation:** Investigating methods used to reach and engage community members.
- **Outcome Evaluation:** Measuring the program's success in reducing smoking rates and lung cancer incidence.
- **Challenges and Opportunities:** Identifying obstacles faced during implementation and potential areas for improvement.

### Reflective Critique Paper
The reflective critique paper will be 1,000-1,500 words long. It will focus on a specific prevention plan that addresses lifestyle changes and risk factors while promoting wellness and decreasing illness rates within the chosen community. The paper must include current scholarly research supported by in-text citations.
