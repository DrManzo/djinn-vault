# Key Aspects of World Building for Aethoria

## Summary
This note compiles key elements for world building in a fantasy narrative set in the Victorian-inspired world of Aethoria, focusing on geography, climate, political structure, and magical innovations.

## Key Points
- **Geography and Climate**
  - The Grand Metropolis of Ironhaven: A sprawling city with distinct districts (Upper Terraces, Smokestack Quarter, Undercity) surrounded by a massive wall.
  - The Misty Moorlands: Wild, Essence-rich lands home to strange creatures and unpredictable magical phenomena.
  - The Sundered Isles: An archipelago known for powerful Adepts and ancient magical traditions.
  - The Rustlands: A vast, industrialized region constantly shrouded in smog.

- **Political Structure**
  - The Crown: Nominally rules Aethoria but lacks true power.
  - The Assembly of Lords: Composed of noble houses controlling different aspects of society.
  - The Shadow Council: A secret organization of powerful individuals, including Master Thorne, who truly control Aethoria.

- **The Guild of Artisans**: Controls much of the economy and technological advancements.

- **Magic and Technology**
  - Magic System: Essence Manipulation, intertwining with steam-powered innovations for unique abilities and challenges.
  - Key Characters:
    - Corvus: An assassin skilled in stealth and combat, evolving from a Channeler to an Adept-level magic user.
    - Thorne: A Machiavellian figure whose death drives much of the plot.
