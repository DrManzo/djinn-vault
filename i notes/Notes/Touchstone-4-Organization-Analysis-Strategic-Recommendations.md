# Touchstone 4: Organization Analysis & Strategic Recommendations

## Summary
This project involves creating an 11- to 14-slide presentation analyzing a public company, applying strategic frameworks like SWOT, PESTEL, and Porter's Five Forces, and providing recommendations on strategy and organizational design. The presentation will include speaker notes, ethical considerations, and references.

## Key Points
- **Choose a Public Company**: Select a company with readily available information.
- **Slides Overview**:
  - Title Slide
  - Executive Summary
  - Organization Background
  - SWOT Analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)
  - PESTEL Analysis
  - Porter's Five Forces Analysis
  - Generic Strategy Recommendations
  - Organization Design Recommendations
  - Ethical Considerations
  - References

## Details
The project will cover the following steps:
1. **Choosing a Company**: Select a public company with readily available information.
2. **Creating Slides**:
   - Title Slide: Include the name of the organization and your name.
   - Executive Summary: Provide an overview of the analysis.
   - Organization Background: Detail the history, mission, and current state of the organization.
   - SWOT Analysis: Analyze internal strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
   - PESTEL Analysis: Examine external factors (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal).
   - Porter's Five Forces Analysis: Assess industry competition, supplier power, buyer power, substitute products/services, and barriers to entry.
   - Generic Strategy Recommendations: Suggest strategies for cost leadership or differentiation based on the analysis.
   - Organization Design Recommendations: Propose changes to organizational structure and processes.
   - Ethical Considerations: Address any ethical implications of the recommendations.
3. **References**: Cite at least four reputable sources using APA format.
