# Best Cameras for Macro, Close-up, and Face Tracking

## Summary
This note provides a detailed list of cameras that excel in active optical zoom, macro photography, and face tracking.

## Key Points
- **Sony RX10 IV** — Best overall bridge camera with 25× optical zoom, excellent close-up performance, and real-time Eye AF.
- **Panasonic Lumix FZ300 / FZ330** — Best value bridge camera offering 24× optical zoom, dedicated macro mode, and face/Eye Detection AF.
- **OM System TG-7** — Rugged compact with industry-leading macro system, including Super Macro at 1 cm and Microscope Mode.
- **Canon PowerShot G7 X Mark III** — Best pocketable option for vlogging and general use, offering 4.2× optical zoom and good close-focus capability.

## Details
The Sony RX10 IV is recommended as the best overall camera due to its versatile capabilities in macro photography, face tracking, and all-in-one versatility. The Panasonic FZ300 offers a budget-friendly alternative with constant f/2.8 aperture and 1 cm macro focusing distance. For rugged use and extreme macro, the OM System TG-7 is ideal. Lastly, the Canon G7 X Mark III is perfect for compact travel photography.
