# Who is Iskaral Pust, the High Mage of Shadows in the Malazan Book of the Fallen Universe

## Summary
Iskaral Pust is a key character in the Malazan universe, serving as the High Priest of Shadow and Magus of High House Shadow. He is powerful due to his direct backing from Shadowthrone, mastery over multiple warrens, and strategic awareness.

## Key Points
- **Title**: Iskaral Pust is known as the High Priest of Shadow and Magus of High House Shadow.
- **Appearance**: He serves at the temple/monastery of Tesem in Seven Cities.
- **Power Sources**:
  - Direct backing from Shadowthrone, implying a close connection.
  - Mastery over multiple warrens: Rashan (assassination), Meanas (illusion/veils), and Thyr (light).
  - On-page magical feats such as healing serious wounds and traveling through the Warren of Shadow.
- **Strategic Awareness**: Despite his deranged internal monologues, he anticipates major players and positions them for Shadowthrone's plans.

## Details
Iskaral Pust is a complex character who plays a significant role in the Malazan universe. He serves as the High Priest of Shadow and Magus of High House Shadow, holding these titles across both Steven Erikson’s *Malazan Book of the Fallen* series and Ian C. Esslemont’s novels.

### Role Across Authors
- **Steven Erikson**: Pust appears prominently in *Deadhouse Gates*, *The Bonehunters*, and later volumes as a key (if chaotic) instrument of High House Shadow's schemes.
- **Ian C. Esslemont**: He is consistently treated as a powerful but deeply eccentric high servant of Shadow, maintaining his status and function.

### Power Analysis
1. **Direct Backing from Shadowthrone**:
   - Pust explicitly calls himself Shadowthrone’s “chosen mortal” and is acknowledged in-text as the Magus of High House Shadow.
   - Erikson has said that Pust functions as a stand-in for Shadowthrone's perspective, implying constant divine guidance.

2. **Mastery Over Multiple Warrens**:
   - He claims to be "Master of Rashan, Meanas, and Thyr," covering darkness/assassination (Rashan), illusion/veils (Meanas), and light (Thyr).
   - This breadth of tools makes him a formidable figure in combat, concealment, travel, and manipulation.

3. **On-Page Magical Feats**:
   - He heals serious wounds with alchemy and sorcery.
   - He travels through the Warren of Shadow and coordinates with demons like Azalan.
   - During key events, he shows strategic awareness and power that impresses even powerful entities such as Cotillion.

4. **Narrative Cues and Reactions**:
   - Characters like Cotillion acknowledge Pust's importance, noting his strategic value despite his madness.
   - The Hounds of Light choose not to engage him during critical moments, indicating his formidable nature.
