# The Dominion of Pyraxis — House Structure and Briefs

## Summary
This note details the structure and brief descriptions of the ten houses in the Dominion of Pyraxis, focusing on their roles, characteristics, and key members.

## Key Points
- **Soluteine**: Political machine; runs things quietly.
- **Kavaren**: Military house; produces generals and High Marshals.
- **Drakenoth**: Magical bloodline; oldest house with a formal, archaic presence.
- **Mercurial**: Economic house; tracks every copper in the empire.
- **Valerion**: Border defense; endurance and loyalty to the Dominion.
- **Thessar**: Knowledge house; archivists and scholars.
- **Ironwell**: Industrial house; infrastructure and manufacturing.
- **Morghaven**: Shadowed house; intelligence and influence.
- **Kalvennor**: Storm and rebellion.

## Details
The Dominion of Pyraxis is structured around ten houses, each with distinct roles and characteristics. Here's a detailed breakdown:

### 1. Solentine
- **Role**: Political machine.
- **Key Member**: Brax (Lord High Steward).
- **Description**: The house that runs things quietly. They manage power behind the scenes, ensuring wars happen on schedule and end as negotiated.

### 2. Kavaren
- **Role**: Military.
- **Key Members**: Not yet named.
- **Description**: Old blood, old glory; produces generals and High Marshals. Proud, direct, deeply traditional. Loyal to the Emperor in public but dangerous when loyalty fractures.

### 3. Drakenoth
- **Role**: Magical.
- **Key Members**: Not yet named.
- **Description**: Oldest house with a formal, archaic presence. Guards magical lineage as if it were a birthright. Treated with deference by other houses.

### 4. Mercurial
- **Role**: Economic.
- **Key Member**: Lord Emerick (Minister of Treasury).
- **Description**: Tracks every copper in the empire on three receipts and a half-burnt ledger. Named after mercury: fast, fluid, impossible to pin down.

### 5. Valerion
- **Role**: Border defense.
- **Key Members**: Not yet named.
- **Description**: Endurance; stationed at the edges of the empire for generations. Genuine and old loyalty to the Dominion but easily overlooked.

### 6. Thessar
- **Role**: Knowledge.
- **Key Members**: Not yet named.
- **Description**: Archivists, scholars, historians. Knows precedents and how many times the Emperor has overstepped. Useful allies when proving something or dangerous enemies when burying it.

### 7. Ironwell
- **Role**: Industrial.
- **Key Members**: Not yet named.
- **Description**: Infrastructure, manufacturing, supply chains. New money by noble standards; ambitious and aggressive in business.

### 8. Morghaven
- **Role**: Shadowed.
- **Key Members**: Not yet named.
- **Description**: Intelligence-adjacent family with a foot in both the state apparatus and nobility. Moves quietly and rarely speaks in full council sessions.

### 9. Kalvennor
- **Role**: Storm and rebellion.
- **Key Member**: Lady Marcella.
- **Description**: Known for their role in storms and rebellions, though more details are needed to flesh out this house fully.
