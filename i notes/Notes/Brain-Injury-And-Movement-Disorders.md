# Brain Injury and Movement Disorders

## Summary
The patient's difficulty in maintaining posture, walking, and coordinating movements suggests a cerebellar injury.

## Key Points
- Cerebellum involvement in movement and coordination.
- Difficulty in posture, gait, and motor coordination are common symptoms of cerebellar damage.

## Details
The cerebellum plays a crucial role in the coordination of voluntary movements, balance, and posture. Damage to this area can result in ataxia (loss of smoothness and accuracy of voluntary movements), dysmetria (inability to judge distances or ranges of movement accurately), and other motor symptoms such as tremors.

The patient's symptoms align with these characteristics, indicating a probable cerebellar injury.
