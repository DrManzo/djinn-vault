# TASK-053 Personal Layer Research

## Summary
This note synthesizes research findings for building a personal AI conciliary system, focusing on ADHD daily briefings, journaling/shadow work automation, Telegram habit tracking, academic support patterns, and personal AI adoption.

## Key Points
- **ADHD Daily Briefings**: Context signals like sobriety counter, sleep proxy, streak data, and 72-hour deadline window.
- **Journaling / Shadow Work**: Minimalist approach with one question per entry, no interpretation or summary.
- **Telegram Habit Tracking**: Using `python-telegram-bot`, `aiosqlite`, and `APScheduler` for minimal stack implementation.
- **Academic Support for Multi-Domain SDL**: Protocol designed to keep the metacognitive loop intact (Argument → Evidence → Draft with `/stuck` escape hatch → Revision).
- **Personal AI Adoption**: Systems that survive are in already-open apps, have a consistent personality, and initiate from data.

## Details
The research highlights the importance of context-aware daily briefings for ADHD users, focusing on delivering key signals effectively. For journaling, the study emphasizes the need to avoid AI interpreting or summarizing entries. Habit tracking via Telegram is shown to be effective with minimal tools, while academic support patterns are designed to enhance self-directed learning by supporting planning and monitoring.
