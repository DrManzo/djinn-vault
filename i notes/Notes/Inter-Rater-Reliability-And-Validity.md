# Inter-Rater Reliability and Validity

## Summary
This note covers the concepts of inter-rater reliability, content validity, and face validity. It explains how these are measured and their significance in psychological research.

## Key Points
- **Inter-Rater Reliability**: Measured using Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC).
- **Content Validity**: Involves systematic expert evaluations.
- **Face Validity**: More informal and subjective, based on participants' opinions.

## Details
### Inter-Rater Reliability
- **Definition**: Measures the agreement among raters for continuous data.
- **Measurement Tool**: Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC).
- **Calculation**: Necessary if you have four or more raters. Not required if research assistants are well trained or if intruder reliability is established.

### Content Validity and Face Validity
- **Content Validity**:
  - Involves systematic expert evaluations.
  - Ensures the test content accurately reflects the concept being measured.
  
- **Face Validity**:
  - More informal and subjective.
  - Based on participants' opinions about the measurement.
