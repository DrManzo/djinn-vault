# Dark Adult Fantasy Audiobook Recommendations

## Summary
This note provides recommendations for long, completed dark adult fantasy audiobooks suitable for an addiction.

## Key Points
- The First Law Trilogy by Joe Abercrombie
- The Black Company by Glen Cook
- The Broken Empire Trilogy by Mark Lawrence
- The Dark Tower Series by Stephen King
- The Broken Earth Trilogy by N.K. Jemisin

## Details
The recommendations are based on a variety of dark, adult fantasy series that offer long, immersive listening experiences with complex characters and intricate plots.

### Epic Dark Fantasy Series
1. **The First Law Trilogy by Joe Abercrombie**
   - Morally complex characters
   - Machiavellian politics
   - Violent conflicts
   - Exceptional narration by Steven Pacey

2. **The Black Company by Glen Cook**
   - Gritty, realistic take on military fantasy
   - Morally gray characters
   - Completed saga with multiple books
   - Excellent narration

3. **The Broken Empire Trilogy by Mark Lawrence**
   - Extremely dark and violent themes
   - A complex anti-hero protagonist
   - Completed trilogy
   - Narrated by James Clamp

4. **The Dark Tower Series by Stephen King**
   - Rich, complex world
   - Epic quest narrative
   - Narrated by George Guidall
   - Completed saga with a satisfying conclusion

5. **The Broken Earth Trilogy by N.K. Jemisin**
   - Unique world-building with geological and apocalyptic themes
   - Complex characters and social commentary
   - Narrated by Robin Miles
   - A completed trilogy, each book winning the Hugo Award

### Standalone Dark Fantasy
- "The Night Circus" by Erin Morgenstern, narrated by Jim Dale
- "The Bear and the Nightingale" by Katherine Arden, narrated by Kathleen Gati
- "The Poppy War" by R.F. Kuang (first in a trilogy, but can be enjoyed as a standalone)
