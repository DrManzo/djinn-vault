# Military Structure of Aethoria

## Summary
This note details the military structure and special units in the world of Aethoria, including high-ranking officers, special units, magical units, and other orders. It also includes an example battle to illustrate their roles.

## Key Points
- **Lord Marshal**: Highest military rank, appointed by the Crown.
- **Generals**: Command large divisions, responsible for strategic planning.
- **Admirals**: Command naval forces, oversee maritime defense and offensive operations.
- **Special Units**:
  - **The Shadowblade Corps**: Elite assassins and spies.
  - **The Ironclad Order**: Elite guards with Essence-enhanced armor.
  - **The Essence Cavalry**: Magic-enhanced mounted units for rapid deployment.
- **Magical Units**:
  - **Mages**: Trained in offensive and defensive magic, divided into specialties.
  - **Battle Mages**: Specialized mages trained in combat magic.
  - **Alchemists**: Experts in creating potions and elixirs for various purposes.
- **Other Orders**:
  - **The Arcane Engineers**: Create and maintain Essence-powered machinery.
  - **The Skyguard**: Elite group specializing in aerial combat and defense.
  - **The Terraverde**: Group using environmental magic for warfare.

## Details
### Military Structure of Aethoria

#### High-Ranking Officers
- **Lord Marshal**: The highest military rank, appointed by the Crown. Serves as the military advisor to the monarch.
- **Generals**: Command large divisions and are responsible for strategic planning and execution of military operations.
- **Admirals**: Command naval forces, overseeing maritime defense and offensive operations.

#### Special Units
- **The Shadowblade Corps**: An elite group of assassins and spies specializing in covert operations and espionage. Known for their stealth and ability to infiltrate enemy ranks.
- **The Ironclad Order**: Elite guards equipped with Essence-enhanced armor, nearly invulnerable in combat. Protect key locations and high-ranking officials.
- **The Essence Cavalry**: Magic-enhanced mounted units that use Essence to augment their speed and combat capabilities. Highly effective in rapid deployment and surprise attacks.

#### Magical Units
- **Mages**: Trained in the use of Essence for offensive and defensive purposes, divided into specialties such as elemental magic (fire, ice, lightning) and illusion magic.
- **Battle Mages**: Specialized mages trained in combat magic, using Essence to enhance their physical abilities and cast powerful spells on the battlefield.
- **Alchemists**: Experts in creating potions and elixirs for various purposes, including healing, enhancement, and combat. Often work closely with the military to develop new weapons and defenses.

#### Other Orders
- **The Arcane Engineers**: A group of mages and engineers who specialize in creating and maintaining Essence-powered machinery and devices.
- **The Skyguard**: An elite group specializing in aerial combat and defense, using Essence-powered airships and gliders.
- **The Terraverde**: A group specializing in environmental magic and warfare, using Essence to manipulate the terrain and weather for strategic advantages.

### Example Battle: The Siege of Ironhaven
During the Sundered Isles Conflict, the city of Ironhaven was besieged by a coalition of enemy forces. Defenders led by the Lord Marshal included units from the Ironclad Order, the Essence Cavalry, and various mage and alchemist groups.

- **Initial Phase**: Enemy forces launched a magical artillery barrage, countered by defensive spells cast by the city's mages.
- **Front Line Defense**: The Ironclad Order held the front lines, using their Essence-enhanced armor to withstand enemy attacks.
- **Surprise Attacks**: The Essence Cavalry launched surprise attacks on the enemy flanks, outmaneuvering them with their magic-enhanced speed.
- **Infiltration and Disruption**: The Shadowblade Corps infiltrated the enemy's ranks, taking out key commanders and disrupting command structures.
- **Support Roles**:
  - **Arcane Engineers**: Maintained city defenses using Essence-powered machinery to repair walls and fortifications.
  - **Skyguard**: Patrolled skies with Essence-powered airships, intercepting enemy aerial units.
  - **Terraverde**: Used environmental magic to create trenches and barriers, slowing down the enemy's advance. Provided potions and elixirs for soldiers.

The battle was a decisive victory for Ironhaven, showcasing the effectiveness of Aethoria's military structure and the importance of magical units in combat.
