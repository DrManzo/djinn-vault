# Djinn Publishings Research Suite

## Summary
This note compiles research on forming Djinn Publishings as a business entity, focusing on the legal structure and formation process.

## Key Points
- **Entity Choice:** An LLC is recommended over a sole proprietorship for liability protection.
- **LLC Structure:** Single-member LLC with California domicile, taxed as a sole proprietor (Schedule C).
- **Formation Checklist:**
  - Name search + reservation
  - File Articles of Organization
  - Draft Operating Agreement
  - Get EIN from IRS
  - File Statement of Information
  - Pay annual franchise tax

## Details
For Javier's situation, an LLC is the correct structure to form Djinn Publishings. This provides liability separation between personal and business assets, which is crucial when signing contracts or generating revenue.

### California LLC Formation Checklist
| Step | Action | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
1. **Name Search + Reservation**  
   - "Djinn Publishings LLC" via bizfile.sos.ca.gov  
   - *Cost: $0*
2. **File Articles of Organization (Form LLC-1)**  
   - *Cost: $70*
3. **Draft Operating Agreement**  
   - *Cost: $0–$2,000*
4. **Get EIN from IRS**  
   - Free
5. **File Statement of Information**  
   - *Cost: $20* (within 90 days)
6. **Pay Annual Franchise Tax**  
   - *Cost: $800/year*

### Total Formation Cost
- First year minimum cost: ~$890
