# Economic System: Guild System and Major Industries

## Summary
This note provides detailed ideas for building the economic system of Aethoria, focusing on guilds and major industries. It includes recommendations to add depth to each section.

## Key Points
- **Artisans Guild**: Specializations, apprenticeships, quality control.
- **Merchants Guild**: Trade routes, market regulations, trade agreements.
- **Essence Workers Guild**: Specializations, licensing, ethical guidelines.
- **Banking Houses**: Central banking, loans and investments, security services.
- **Major Industries**: Essence-infused technology, steam-powered machinery, exotic materials trade.

## Details
The economic system of Aethoria is structured around guilds and banking houses, with major industries driving the economy. Here are detailed ideas to add depth:

### Guild System

1. **Artisans Guild**
   - **Specializations**: Include sub-guilds for specific crafts (blacksmithing, carpentry, glassmaking).
   - **Apprenticeships**: Establish a rigorous apprenticeship program with clear progression from apprentice to journeyman to master craftsman.
   - **Quality Control**: Implement strict quality standards and regular inspections.

2. **Merchants Guild**
   - **Trade Routes**: Establish trade routes with other regions (Sundered Isles, Rustlands).
   - **Market Regulations**: Set up regulations to prevent monopolies and ensure fair competition.
   - **Trade Agreements**: Negotiate favorable terms for Aethorian merchants.

3. **Essence Workers Guild**
   - **Specializations**: Divide the guild into sub-guilds (healing, enchanting, elemental control).
   - **Licensing**: Require licenses for different levels of expertise.
   - **Ethical Guidelines**: Establish ethical guidelines to prevent misuse and ensure public safety.

### Banking Houses

1. **The Royal Bank of Ironhaven**
   - **Central Banking**: Function as a central bank, regulating currency and maintaining economic stability.
   - **Loans and Investments**: Offer loans for business ventures and investments in major industries.
   - **Security**: Provide secure storage for valuable items and documents.

2. **The Sundered Isles Trading Company**
   - **International Trade**: Facilitate trade between Aethoria and the Sundered Isles.
   - **Exotic Materials**: Specialize in trading rare metals and magical plants.
   - **Maritime Security**: Offer maritime security services to protect trade vessels.

### Major Industries

1. **Essence-Infused Technology**
   - **Research and Development**: Establish research centers for new technologies (efficient steam engines, advanced magical devices).
   - **Manufacturing**: Set up facilities to produce Essence-infused goods.
   - **Regulation**: Implement safety regulations to prevent misuse.

2. **Steam-Powered Machinery**
   - **Innovation**: Encourage innovation in steam-powered technology.
   - **Industrial Applications**: Apply machinery to various industries (manufacturing, transportation, agriculture).
   - **Environmental Impact**: Consider the environmental impact and implement measures to mitigate pollution.

3. **Exotic Materials Trade**
   - **Sourcing**: Establish reliable sources for exotic materials.
   - **Trade Agreements**: Negotiate favorable terms with other regions.
   - **Regulation**: Implement ethical and sustainable trade regulations.

## Recommendations for Integration into the Story

1. **Guild Politics**: Explore political dynamics within and between guilds, highlighting conflicts and alliances.
2. **Economic Tensions**: Introduce economic tensions between traditional Essence users and steam technology proponents.
3. **Character Involvement**: Involve characters in the economic system (merchants navigating trade agreements, Essence workers dealing with ethical dilemmas).
4. **World-Building**: Use the economic system to flesh out the world, detailing interactions between regions and factions.
