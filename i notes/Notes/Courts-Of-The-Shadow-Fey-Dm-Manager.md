# Courts of the Shadow Fey DM Manager

## Summary
This Python code is designed to help manage a Dungeons and Dragons (DnD) game with 10 level 5 to 6 characters, using mechanics from the "Courts of the Shadow Fey" PDF. It tracks party status, HP, AC, conditions, faction ties, and more.

## Key Points
- Supports a minimum of 5 and maximum of 10 characters.
- Tracks HP, AC, current conditions, faction, courtesan ties, and character-level notes.
- Implements the Status system from the PDF.
- Manages Factions: Ravens, Grey Ladies, Lords of Light, Akyishigal.
- Runs RP skill challenges with advantage/disadvantage.
- Handles Courtesan & Consort wooing.
- Implements Status gifting (Blood Oath) mechanic.
- Dice roller and combat management.

## Details
The tool is built directly from the "Courts of the Shadow Fey" PDF mechanics. It includes:
- **Party Tracking**: Supports 5 to 10 characters, tracking HP, AC, current conditions, faction ties, and more.
- **Status System**: Automatically calculates starting status based on character traits and actions.
- **Factions**: Detailed management for each faction with specific bonuses and penalties.
- **RP Skill Challenges**: Interactive skill challenges with custom DCs for various in-game activities.
- **Courtesan & Consort Wooing**: Full Table 32 implementation, including minimum Status checks and success/failure outcomes.
- **Status Gifting (Blood Oath)**: Permanent reduction of max HP to gift Status.
