# The Story So Far in Pyraxis

## Summary
The story follows the crumbling Dominion of Pyraxis, ruled by Emperor Arctus and his heir Prince Regent Raxz. Key events include a political gala where Raxz impulsively uses magic to transform their attire, leading to a public rebuke from House Kalvennor and a looming confrontation with Emperor Arctus.

## Key Points
- **World Setup**: The Dominion of Pyraxis is a post-war state held together by fragile alliances. Magic is will-based.
- **Characters**:
  - **Raxz (Prince Regent)**: Bisexual, in love with his mentor Javelin but engaged in an arranged courtship with Lady Marcella Kalvennor. Politically naive and resentful of court expectations.
  - **Javelin**: Raxz's mentor and lover, whose face shows hurt and judgment after the incident at the gala.
  - **House Kalvennor**: Proud and sensitive to slights; their walkout from the gala is a significant blow to the Dominion’s stability.
- **Plot Points**:
  - Raxz uses magic during the gala to transform their attire, causing a breach of etiquette and bodily autonomy.
  - The Kalvennors’ coordinated exit signals a public rebuke and damage to the crucial political alliance.
  - Emperor Arctus leaves the gala immediately afterward, signaling disapproval and impending consequences for Raxz.

## Details
The story is set in a post-war state called the Dominion of Pyraxis. It is ruled by Emperor Arctus with his heir Prince Regent Raxz and the Nine Great Houses. Magic is will-based, allowing users to shape reality through focused will. Techniques include blink (short-range teleportation), illusions, and transformation.

Raxz, at 21 years old, is bisexual and in a romantic relationship with Javelin, his combat and magic mentor. He is engaged in an arranged courtship with Lady Marcella Kalvennor but feels politically naive and resentful of the expectations placed on him by the court. At the imperial gala meant to secure a political alliance with House Kalvennor, Raxz impulsively uses magic to transform their attire into solemn imperial black, gold, and silver without her consent.

This act is seen as a breach of etiquette and bodily autonomy, leading to a coordinated exit from the gala by the Kalvennors. Emperor Arctus leaves immediately afterward, signaling disapproval and impending consequences for Raxz. The fallout includes a damaged political alliance, which could jeopardize the Dominion’s stability.

Javelin, who is present during the incident, shows hurt and judgment on his face. Brax, the High Steward of the Dominion and Javelin's legal father, watches these events with professional concern and internal guilt over his role in Raxz's upbringing.
