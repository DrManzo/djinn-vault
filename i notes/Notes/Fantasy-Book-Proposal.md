# Fantasy Book Proposal

## Summary
This note consolidates feedback and initial thoughts on a fantasy book proposal that integrates elements from the Malazan, Mistborn, and Harry Potter universes. The focus is on developing a magic system, empire structure, and character dynamics.

## Key Points
- **Magic System**: Influences include Malazan, Mistborn, and Harry Potter.
- **Empire Structure & Timeline**: 8-9 noble families already established but not all aligned with the Emperor.
- **Magic Mechanics**: Wands, verbal magic, high fantasy magic, and human abilities from breeding.

## Details
The proposal aims to create a rich, multi-layered universe where magic is woven into the fabric of society. The narrative will explore themes of political intrigue, social class, and conflict through the lens of an evolving empire. Key elements include:

- **Magic System**: 
  - Wands are used for casting spells.
  - Verbal magic exists but may not be as prevalent.
  - High fantasy magic is a significant aspect.
  - Human abilities from breeding add unique dimensions to characters.

- **Empire Structure & Timeline**:
  - Multiple lands form an empire with 8-9 noble families already in power.
  - These families are not uniformly aligned with the Emperor, adding complexity to political dynamics.
  - The story is set as a prologue or mid-series book, providing context for ongoing conflicts and character development.
