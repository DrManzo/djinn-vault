# Apps for iOS and Windows

## Summary
This note provides recommendations for apps that can help with various business and educational tasks on both iOS and Windows platforms, including paid options.

## Key Points
- Balance Sheets Explained & Income Statement Analysis (Finance)
- Four Stoic Virtues (Psychology)
- Stats in Psychology (GCU Class)
- Algorithm Tutorials (Computer Science)

## Details

### 1. **Balance Sheets Explained & Income Statement Analysis**
   - **Best Overall App: Financial Statement Reading** [iOS]
     - **Cost:** One-time purchase
     - **Features:** Interactive notes, investment decision checklists, structured learning path
     - **Availability:** iOS (check App Store)
   
   - **Windows Alternative: Quicken Classic** [Windows]
     - **Cost:** Annual subscription (~$100/year, but includes desktop software)
     - **Features:** Detailed account management, budgeting, investing
   
   - **Free Alternatives:**
     - NerdWallet
     - QuickBooks Online

### 2. **Four Stoic Virtues (Psychology)**
   - **Top Choice for One-Time Payment: FourVirtues** [iOS ONLY]
     - **Cost:** One-week free trial, then one-time payment
     - **Features:** Decision Mode, Reflect Mode, Learn section, Stoic Library, AI versions of Stoic authors
   
   - **Strong Paid Alternative: Stoic Nature** [iOS, $0-premium options]
     - **Why:** Combines Stoicism + psychology + modern science
     - **Features:** 50+ guided journals, Stoic quotes, morning/nighttime journals, blank journal mode
   
   - **General Stoicism (not specific to Four Virtues): The Stoic App** [iOS]
     - **Features:** 350+ quotes, classic texts (*Meditations*, *Enchiridion*), training tools

### 3. **Stats in Psychology (GCU Class)**
   - **Direct GCU Integration: SPSS Statistics** [Windows preferred, limited iOS]
     - **Why:** Your GCU class will use SPSS directly
     - **Features:** Full statistical analysis, required for psychology stats coursework
   
   - **Best Supplement: GCU's Visual Learner Statistics** [Web-based]
     - **Why:** Designed specifically for GCU psychology students
     - **Features:** Visual breakdowns of statistical concepts, tutorial videos

### 4. **Algorithm Tutorials (Computer Science)**
   - **Recommendation:** Focus on SPSS through your school first.
