# Equipment and Infrastructure Setup for Meanas Systems

## Summary
This note outlines the equipment and infrastructure setup needed to launch a one-man business, focusing on hardware requirements for both desktop and laptop computers, as well as additional components like cameras, microphones, and network connectivity.

## Key Points
- Desktop Tower: Ryzen 7 + RTX 4070 Ti Super, 64GB RAM, dual SSD setup.
- Laptop: i9 processor, RTX 5090, 64GB RAM.
- Video/Audio Equipment: Sony A6700 camera, Rode Wireless GO II microphone, Shure SM7B + interface.
- Connectivity: Cat6A wired ethernet and Wi-Fi.

## Details
The primary function of the business will be to hold licensing and major funding for various projects. The subsidiary Meanas Systems will focus on software development, testing, and technology engineering. A second subsidiary will provide financial backing for a community project around the fifth year.

For equipment, a desktop tower is essential as it serves as the main compute engine with sufficient power for mid-tier AI inference tasks. The laptop acts as a mobile workstation and backup node, ensuring consistent performance across devices. Additional components include high-quality video and audio equipment to support potential YouTube channel activities.

The total cost of hardware, including a 10% contingency buffer, is approximately $22,413.
