# Definitions for PSY-355-Q-T4-ExamStudyGuide

## Summary
This note provides key definitions from a study guide relevant to the course PSY-355, focusing on Piaget's theory and biological concepts.

## Key Points
- **Schemes**: Organized patterns of thought or behavior that help children make sense of their experiences.
- **Mitosis**: The process by which a cell divides to produce two identical daughter cells, crucial for growth and development.
- **Phenotype**: Observable characteristics resulting from the interaction of genotype and environment.

## Details
Piaget's theory emphasizes how schemes are fundamental in cognitive development. Schemes evolve through assimilation (adapting experiences to existing schemes) and accommodation (adjusting schemes based on new experiences). Mitosis is a key biological process that supports growth and development, ensuring genetic continuity within an organism. The phenotype reflects the visible traits of an individual, influenced by both genetic makeup and environmental factors.
