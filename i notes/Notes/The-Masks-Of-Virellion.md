# The Masks of Virellion

## Summary
A custom D&D campaign for levels 3–8, focusing on heavy role-play and mature themes like political corruption, blackmail, and moral ambiguity. Players are pulled into a web of conspiracies, negotiations, and social intrigue.

## Key Points
- **Campaign Name:** The Masks of Virellion
- **Setting:** City-state of Virellion with a Council of Masks
- **Core Tones:**
  - Mature themes (political corruption, blackmail, addiction)
  - Heavy role-play (negotiations, interrogations, social climbing)

## Details

### Major Factions
1. **The Council of Masks**
   - Each councilor has a secret real identity and a public "mask persona."
   - Players can investigate who is behind which mask.
   - Be hired as agents/fixers.
   - Opportunity to become a masked councilor.

2. **The Bleak Lanterns**
   - Underground group smuggling slaves and "maskless" people out of the city, but also selling secrets to the highest bidder.
   - Players must decide whether they are liberators, criminals, or both.

3. **House of Reflections**
   - High-end salon/temple that creates magical masks (and addictive illusion rituals).
   - Great place for intense 1-on-1 scenes and temptations for the party.

### Act Structure
#### Act I: The Vanishing Mask
- **Hook:** A patron or friend of the party is a minor noble whose sibling vanishes after receiving an invite to a private Council event.
- **RP Scenes:**
  - Interview servants, rivals, and former lovers.
  - Attend a masked party; everyone must adopt a fake persona.

#### Act II: Faces in the Gutter
- **PCs move into the slums, taverns, and safehouses.**
- **RP Scenes:**
  - Gain trust of a gang or community leader.
  - Negotiate with the Bleak Lanterns (they know about entire "shipments" of maskless people).
  - Decide whether to expose or assist a councilor who’s secretly financing disappearances.

#### Moral Dilemma:
- Stop the kidnappings at the cost of exposing a fragile underground refugee route, or tolerate some crimes to protect the many.
  
#### Act III: The Shattered Council
- **Discover a ritual that lets someone overwrite their identity with a mask persona permanently.**
- **RP Scenes:**
  - Public council session where the party presents evidence and is cross-examined.
