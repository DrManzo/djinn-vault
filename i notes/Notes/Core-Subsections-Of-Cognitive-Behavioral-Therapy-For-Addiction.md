# Core Subsections of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Addiction

## Summary
This note outlines the key subsections and treatment models within cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) specifically tailored for substance use disorders, including Relapse Prevention, Mindfulness-Based Relapse Prevention, Community Reinforcement Approach, Behavioral Couples Therapy, Guided Self-Change, Personality-Targeted Brief Interventions, Contingency Management, and Motivational Interviewing.

## Key Points
- **Relapse Prevention (RP)**: Focuses on functional analysis of triggers, coping skills training, and drug-refusal skills.
- **Mindfulness-Based Relapse Prevention (MBRP)**: Integrates mindfulness meditation to reduce craving intensity.
- **Community Reinforcement Approach (CRA)**: Targets environmental reinforcers through various modules.
- **Behavioral Couples Therapy (BCT)**: Addresses the bidirectional relationship between relationship functioning and substance use.
- **Guided Self-Change (GSC) / Brief Interventions**: Motivationally-based, brief treatment helping clients formulate change goals.
- **Personality-Targeted Brief Interventions**: Personalized CBT addressing specific personality risk factors.
- **Contingency Management (CM)**: Voucher/prize reinforcement for verified abstinence.
- **Motivational Interviewing (MI) / Motivational Enhancement**: Used to resolve ambivalence and enhance treatment engagement.

## Details
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) is a widely recognized approach in treating substance use disorders, offering various models that address different aspects of addiction. The core subsections include:

### 1. **Relapse Prevention (RP)**
Developed by Marlatt & Gordon (1985), RP conceptualizes relapse as a process involving high-risk situations and coping deficits. Key components include:
- Functional analysis of triggers
- Identification of intrapersonal/interpersonal high-risk situations
- Coping-skills training, drug-refusal skills
- Increasing non-substance activities

The Project MATCH adapted it to 14 sessions covering cognitive restructuring, problem solving, behavioral skills training.

### 2. **Mindfulness-Based Relapse Prevention (MBRP)**
An 8-session group extension of RP that integrates mindfulness meditation:
- Session structure: Automatic pilot awareness → Trigger exposure & mindful responding → SOBER method
- Mechanism: Reduces thought-avoidance and craving intensity through mindful awareness rather than suppression.

### 3. **Community Reinforcement Approach (CRA)**
A multimodal, modular intervention targeting environmental reinforcers with the following modules:
- Functional analysis
- Treatment planning (Happiness Scale, Goals of Counseling)
- Sobriety sampling (negotiated abstinence periods)
- Behavioral skills training (problem solving, assertiveness, refusal)
- Job skills
- Social/recreational counseling
- Relationship counseling
- Relapse prevention

### 4. **Behavioral Couples Therapy (BCT)**
Targets the bidirectional relationship between relationship functioning and substance use:
- Psychoeducation
- Contingency management within the relationship
- Scheduling mutually pleasurable non-drug activities
- Communication skills training

### 5. **Guided Self-Change (GSC) / Brief Interventions**
Motivationally-based, very brief treatment helping clients formulate and pursue a self-defined change goal.

### 6. **Personality-Targeted Brief Interventions**
Personalized CBT addressing specific personality risk factors such as impulsivity, hopelessness, anxiety sensitivity, sensation seeking.

### 7. **Contingency Management (CM)**
Though behavioral in origin, CM is often integrated within CBT protocols:
- Voucher/prize reinforcement for verified abstinence
- Variants: Standard CM, prize-based "fishbowl" CM, job-based reinforcement alternatives

### 8. **Motivational Interviewing (MI) / Motivational Enhancement**
Often used as a pretreatment or integrated component to resolve ambivalence and enhance treatment engagement.
