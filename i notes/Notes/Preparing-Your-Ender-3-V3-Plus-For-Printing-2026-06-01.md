# Preparing Your Ender 3 V3 Plus for Printing

## Summary
This note provides guidance on preparing your new Ender 3 V3 Plus and handling the filament provided by a friend.

## Key Points
- Clean and level the build plate.
- Use adhesives like glue sticks or hair spray if needed.
- Ensure the filament is dry before printing.
- Set appropriate bed temperatures based on filament type (PLA: 50-60°C, PETG: 80-90°C).

## Details
For your new Ender 3 V3 Plus, proper setup and filament handling are crucial for successful prints. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

### Bed Preparation Steps

1. **Clean the build plate thoroughly** - Use isopropyl alcohol (70% or higher) to remove any oils, dust, or residue from manufacturing.
2. **Level the bed** - Ensure uniform first layers and good adhesion by properly leveling the bed.
3. **Adjust Z-offset** - Make sure the nozzle is at the correct height for optimal first layer squish.

### Adding Adhesives

While the Ender 3 V3 Plus comes with a textured PEI-like build surface, you may need to use adhesives depending on your filament type:

- **Glue stick (like Elmer's)** - Apply a thin, even layer for extra adhesion, especially useful for PLA and PETG.
- **Hair spray** - Another popular option that can be sprayed in a thin coat.
- **No adhesive at all** - Many users get excellent results without any adhesive.

### Additional Tips

Since your friend provided filament, ensure it is dry before printing. Set the bed temperature appropriately: 50-60°C for PLA and 80-90°C for PETG. If you still experience adhesion issues after cleaning and leveling, enable a brim in your slicer software to increase contact area with the bed.
