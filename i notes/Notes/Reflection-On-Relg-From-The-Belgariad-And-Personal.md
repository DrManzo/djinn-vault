## Details
Relg’s journey in *The Belgariad* is marked by his deep devotion, which often manifests as withdrawal. This mirrors the experiences of many individuals who feel a tension between wanting connection and fearing it.

Relg’s character resonates because he embodies a universal paradox: deeply desiring connection, yet instinctively holding back. The passage highlights that Relg’s struggle is not just about asceticism but about the pain of wanting to belong while feeling fundamentally out of step with the world.
