# Accounting Cycle and Financial Ratios

## Summary
This note contains answers to questions related to the accounting cycle, financial ratios, and inventory methods.

## Key Points
- **Question 1**: The phase of the accounting cycle for transferring $12,000 in revenue and $8,000 in expenses into equity is Close.
- **Question 2**: The formula for the rate of return on sales is Net Income / Net Sales.
- **Question 3**: Using the provided financial information, the asset turnover ratio is 0.43.
- **Question 4**: The business best served by the periodic inventory method is a Construction equipment dealer.

## Details
### Question 1: Accounting Cycle Phase
Transferring $12,000 in revenue and $8,000 in expenses into equity would be performed during the Close phase of the accounting cycle. This involves closing entries to transfer revenues and expenses to retained earnings, thereby updating the equity section.

### Question 2: Rate of Return on Sales
The rate of return on sales is calculated using the formula:
\[ \text{Rate of Return on Sales} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Net Sales}} \]

This ratio measures how efficiently a company uses its sales to generate profits. It helps in assessing the profitability and efficiency of the business operations.

### Question 3: Asset Turnover Ratio
Given:
- Net Sales: $379,000
- Total Assets: $875,500

The asset turnover ratio is calculated as follows:
\[ \text{Asset Turnover Ratio} = \frac{\text{Net Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}} = \frac{379,000}{875,500} \approx 0.432895 \]

Rounding to two decimal places gives an asset turnover ratio of **0.43**.

### Question 4: Inventory Method
The business best served by the periodic inventory method is a Construction equipment dealer. This method is suitable for businesses with lower inventory turnover, fewer SKUs, and simpler supply chains. It involves counting inventory at specific intervals rather than continuously tracking it.
