# FBI Consultant Presentation: Stanford Prison Experiment Analysis

## Summary
This note summarizes an 8-10 slide PowerPoint presentation analyzing the Stanford Prison Experiment from the perspective of an FBI consultant specializing in obedience, group behavior, and conformity. The presentation covers key aspects such as implicit and explicit expectations, deindividuation, cognitive dissonance, ethical concerns, and practical implications for law enforcement.

## Details
The Stanford Prison Experiment, conducted by Philip Zimbardo, demonstrated how situational forces can transform ordinary people into abusive authority figures. Within days, randomly assigned college students adopted extreme behaviors—guards became increasingly cruel while prisoners showed signs of severe psychological distress. Key findings include:
- **Social Roles** in shaping behavior beyond individual personality traits.
- **Institutional Environments** corrupting good people.
- **Group Dynamics** perpetuating abusive behaviors.
