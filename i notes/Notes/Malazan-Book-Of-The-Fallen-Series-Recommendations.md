# Malazan Book of the Fallen Series Recommendations

## Summary
This note provides recommendations for continuing the Malazan Book of the Fallen series and similar fantasy series with extensive worldbuilding.

## Key Points
- Next book after "Memories of Ice": House of Chains
- Complete list of main series: Gardens of the Moon, Deadhouse Gates, Memories of Ice, House of Chains, Midnight Tides, The Bonehunters, Reaper's Gale, Toll the Hounds, Dust of Dreams, The Crippled God

## Details
The Malazan Book of the Fallen series is known for its complex worldbuilding and intricate plot lines. Here are some recommendations for similar fantasy series:

### Next in the Series
1. **House of Chains** - Follows "Memories of Ice" in the main series.

### Complete Main Series Order
1. Gardens of the Moon
2. Deadhouse Gates
3. Memories of Ice
4. House of Chains
5. Midnight Tides
6. The Bonehunters
7. Reaper's Gale
8. Toll the Hounds
9. Dust of Dreams
10. The Crippled God

### Additional Recommendations for Extensive Worldbuilding and Fantasy Themes
- **The Wheel of Time** by Robert Jordan: 14-book epic fantasy series with intricate worldbuilding.
- **First Law Series** by Joe Abercrombie: Original trilogy followed by several standalone novels, all set in the same universe.
- **Realm of the Elderlings** by Robin Hobb: Expansive series consisting of several interconnected trilogies and quartets.
- **Lightbringer Series** by Brent Weeks: Five-book series with unique magic systems and complex political intrigue.
- **Stormlight Archive** by Brandon Sanderson: Ongoing epic fantasy series with four books published so far, featuring intricate magic systems.
