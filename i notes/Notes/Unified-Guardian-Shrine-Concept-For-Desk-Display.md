# Unified Guardian Shrine Concept for Desk Display

## Summary
This note outlines a detailed design concept for a unified miniature desk shrine combining an oni/dragon guardian, a torii-style frame, and a suspended element. The design is optimized for low-poly style printing in basic filament.

## Key Points
- **Core Concept**: A compact desk shrine with three integrated parts: a low-poly oni mask or dragon guardian, a mini torii-inspired frame, and a suspended airship or dragon.
- **Geometry and Layout**: The piece features a stable base, a tall torii frame, and the guardian centered in side view.
- **Print-Friendly Low-Poly Style**: Large flat facets for easy printing; separate components for modularity.

## Details
The design combines elements of traditional Japanese culture with modern 3D printing techniques to create a functional art piece suitable for desk display. The shrine includes:
- A low-poly oni mask or dragon guardian as the main feature.
- A mini torii-inspired frame that serves as both a stand and a decorative element.
- A suspended airship or dragon, hanging from the top beam of the torii.

### Core Concept
1. **Base**: Rectangular or slightly trapezoidal plate with chamfered low-poly edges.
2. **Torii Frame**:
   - Two vertical posts at the back corners.
   - Top crossbeam flared at the ends, echoing traditional torii while staying stylized.
3. **Guardian**:
   - Either a low-poly oni mask or a bust that rises from the base with subtle dragon motifs.
4. **Suspended Element**: A small dragon coiled in a ring or an airship silhouette.

### Geometry and Layout
- The piece is designed as a shallow "L" shape: stable base, tall torii frame, and the guardian centered.
- Base dimensions: 120–160 mm wide, 60–80 mm deep.
- Front lip for holding business cards or badges.

### Suspended Element
- Subject: A tiny low-poly dragon coiled in a ring or a simple airship silhouette.
- Suspension method: Two or three thin columns from the top beam to the suspended element.

### Print-Friendly Low-Poly Style
- Use large flat facets and avoid tiny spikes.
- Design as single solid pieces that print flat on the bed with 0.2 mm layers and ~10–15% infill.
- Orient the guardian so the front face is vertical or slightly tilted, minimizing supports.

### Modularity and Remix Potential
- Separate components in CAD: base, torii frame, guardian insert, suspended insert.
- Standardize business card slot dimensions for compatibility with various cards or nameplates.
- Provide a simple "blank" insert shape where others can swap the oni/dragon for other guardians (fox, robot, etc.).
