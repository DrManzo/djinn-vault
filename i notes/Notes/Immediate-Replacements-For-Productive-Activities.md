## Details
Javier has recently uninstalled all forms of social media, except YouTube. To replace the time previously spent on social media, he can engage in high-value activities that align with his interests and long-term objectives.

### Law & Legal Scholarship
1. **Case Briefing Practice**
   - Focus on landmark Supreme Court cases using the IRAC method.
2. **Legal Writing Drills**
   - Write 500-word legal memos on hypothetical scenarios to sharpen analytical precision.
3. **Bar Exam Question Sets**
   - Familiarize yourself with MBE-style multiple choice questions for law school preparation.
4. **Read Law Review Articles**
   - Choose one per week from reputable journals like Harvard Law Review, Yale Law Journal, or Stanford Law Review.

### Investing & Financial Acumen
1. **Portfolio Journaling**
   - Track your investment thesis for each position with weekly reviews.
2. **Financial Statement Analysis**
   - Deep-dive into 10-Ks and 10-Qs of three companies per week to calculate key financial ratios.
3. **Investment Book Study**
   - Work through classics like *The Intelligent Investor* (Graham), *Security Analysis*, and *Poor Charlie's Almanack*.
4. **Market Research Reports**
   - Read credible analyst reports from professional sources.

### Psychology & Self-Mastery
1. **Shadow Work Journaling**
   - Daily 15-minute sessions using prompts from Jungian psychology to document patterns, triggers, and integration progress.
2. **Behavioral Psychology Studies**
   - Read primary research from journals like *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* or *Cognitive Science*.
3. **Meditation/Mindfulness Practices**
   - Engage in regular meditation sessions to enhance mental clarity and focus.
