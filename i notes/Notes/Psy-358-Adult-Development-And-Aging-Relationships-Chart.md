# PSY-358 Adult Development and Aging: Relationships Chart

## Summary
The chart evaluates the potential advantages, disadvantages, and techniques/resources for various adult relationships on emotional well-being. It references scholarly sources to provide unbiased and current information.

## Key Points
- **Marriage (heterosexual or homosexual)**
  - Advantages: Emotional support, reduced anxiety/depression, increased survival likelihood.
  - Disadvantages: Compromises, emotional frustration, mental health issues if problems arise.
  - Techniques/Resources: Active listening, shared activities, couples therapy.

- **Cohabitation (unmarried)**
  - Advantages: Increased intimacy, greater autonomy, shared responsibilities.
  - Disadvantages: Relationship uncertainty, societal stigma, unclear future plans.
  - Techniques/Resources: Open communication, support network, proactive counseling.

- **Remaining Single**
  - Advantages: Personal freedom, focus on self-discovery.
  - Disadvantages: Loneliness, social isolation, potential financial challenges.

## Details
### Marriage (heterosexual or homosexual)
- **Advantages**:
  - Provides emotional support, reducing anxiety and depression [mcleanhospital](https://www.mcleanhospital.org/essential/relationships).
  - Offers companionship and physical affection, improving immune system function [mcleanhospital](https://www.mcleanhospital.org/essential/relationships).
  - Increases likelihood of survival by 50% regardless of age, sex, or health status [apa](https://www.apa.org/monitor/2018/03/life-saving-relationships).

- **Disadvantages**:
  - Requires compromises and sacrifices, potentially leading to loss of personal freedom [paired](https://www.paired.com/articles/pros-and-cons-of-marriage).
  - Can lead to emotional frustration and potential disagreements due to heightened vulnerability [paired](https://www.paired.com/articles/pros-and-cons-of-marriage).
  - May contribute to mental health issues such as depression and anxiety if marital problems arise [paired](https://www.paired.com/articles/pros-and-cons-of-marriage).

- **Techniques/Resources**:
  - Practice active listening and empathy to foster understanding and connection [riazcounseling](https://riazcounseling.com/key-strategies-for-emotional-intelligence-in-marriage/).
  - Engage in shared activities and try new experiences together to maintain intimacy [helpguide](https://www.helpguide.org/relationships/social-connection/relationship-help).
  - Seek professional help through couples therapy when facing persistent issues [riazcounseling](https://riazcounseling.com/key-strategies-for-emotional-intelligence-in-marriage/).

### Cohabitation (unmarried)
- **Advantages**:
  - Offers increased intimacy and emotional connection [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).
  - Provides greater sense of autonomy and freedom compared to traditional marriage [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).
  - Allows for shared responsibilities and support systems [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).

- **Disadvantages**:
  - May lead to relationship uncertainty and lack of commitment [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).
  - Can face societal stigma and potential family disapproval [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).
  - Might contribute to stress and anxiety due to unclear future plans [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).

- **Techniques/Resources**:
  - Foster open communication about expectations and future plans [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).
  - Build a support network of friends and family [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).
  - Consider couples counseling to address relationship concerns proactively [legalresearchandanalysis](https://legalresearchandanalysis.com/cohabitationlive-in-relationships/).

### Remaining Single
- **Advantages**:
  - Provides greater personal freedom and autonomy [paired](https://www.paired.com/articles/pros-and-cons-of-marriage).
  - Allows for focus on personal growth and self-discovery.
  
- **Disadvantages**:
  - May lead to feelings of loneliness and social isolation [mcleanhospital](https://www.mcleanhospital.org/essential/relationships).
  - Lacks the built-in emotional support system of a partnership [mcleanhospital](https://www.mcleanhospital.org/essential/relationships).
  - Might face financial challenges without a partner.
