# The Math Behind Djinn Cash Suite

## Summary
The Djinn Cash Suite, a comprehensive personal finance document, relies on several mathematical models to guide financial planning and management. This note outlines the key mathematical concepts used in budgeting, compound interest, debt payoff strategies, investment returns, and options pricing.

## Key Points
- **50/30/20 Rule**: Allocate 50% of net income for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings/debt.
- **Zero-based Budgeting**: Every dollar is allocated so that Net Income minus all assigned categories equals zero.
- **Compound Interest Formula**: \( A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt} \) where \( A \) is the future value, \( P \) is the principal amount, \( r \) is the annual interest rate, \( n \) is the number of times compounded per year, and \( t \) is time in years.
- **Avalanche vs. Snowball Debt Payoff**: Both follow the same amortization formula but differ in ordering debts by highest to lowest interest rates or smallest to largest balance.
- **FICO Credit Score Model**: A weighted sum of five factors: payment history (35%), credit utilization (30%), length of credit history (15%), credit mix (10%), and new credit (10%).
- **Dollar-cost Averaging (DCA)**: Spreads investments to reduce price-timing risk, resulting in an average cost per share.
- **Risk/Reward Ratio**: Requires a minimum 1:2 ratio for speculative investments.

## Details
The Djinn Cash Suite provides a structured approach to personal finance management. Each section is driven by real mathematical models that help users make informed financial decisions. The core concepts include:

### Budgeting Math
- **50/30/20 Rule**: Simplifies budget allocation.
- **Zero-based Budgeting**: Ensures every dollar has a specific purpose, aligning with the zero-sum equation.

### Compound Interest (Wealth-Building)
- **Formula**: \( A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt} \).
- **Key Insight**: Time is the most powerful lever in building wealth; interruptions via debt or speculation pose significant risks.

### Debt Payoff Math
- **Avalanche Method**: Orders debts by highest interest rate first.
- **Snowball Method**: Orders debts by smallest balance first for psychological wins.

### Credit Score Math
- **FICO Model**: Weighted sum of five factors: payment history, credit utilization, length of credit history, credit mix, and new credit.
- **Utilization Ratio**: Keep under 10% to maximize score benefit.

### Investment Returns Math
- **Dollar-cost Averaging (DCA)**: Reduces price-timing risk by spreading investments over time.
- **Risk/Reward Ratio**: Requires a minimum 1:2 ratio for speculative investments, ensuring a balanced approach.
