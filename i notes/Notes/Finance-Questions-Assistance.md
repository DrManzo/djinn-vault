# Finance Questions Assistance

## Summary
Providing guidance on balance sheets and income statements for a Phase 1 finance concept.

## Key Points
- **Balance Sheets**: A snapshot of what a business owns and owes at a single point in time.
- **Income Statements**: Performance over a period, showing revenue, cost of sales, operating expenses, interest, and taxes.

## Details
### Balance Sheets (option 1)
- Core equation: Assets = Liabilities + Equity
- **Assets**: Resources that provide future benefit (cash, inventory, equipment).
- **Liabilities**: Obligations the business must pay in the future (loans, accounts payable).
- **Equity**: The owners’ residual interest after liabilities are subtracted from assets.

### Income Statements (option 2)
- Basic structure:
  - Revenue (sales)
  - Minus cost of sales → gross profit
  - Minus operating expenses → operating income
  - Minus interest and taxes → net income ("bottom line").
