# THC Infused D&D Campaign

## Summary
This campaign adapts traditional Dungeons & Dragons mechanics by replacing hit points with THC gummies, creating a unique and immersive experience centered around cannabis culture.

## Key Points
- **Gummy HP System**: Each gummy represents 5 or 10 hit points.
- **Consumption Rules**: Players consume gummies when taking damage.
- **Buff/Debuff Mechanics**: THC affects player abilities in various ways.
- **Thematic Elements**: Incorporate cannabis-related themes and lore.

## Details
The campaign revolves around a world where the use of THC is both common and integral to society. Characters can gain or lose gummies based on their actions, and these gummies affect not only their hit points but also their abilities and behaviors.

### Gummy HP System
- **Hit Points**: Each gummy represents 5 or 10 hit points.
- **Consumption**: Players must consume a gummy when taking damage. 
- **Maximum HP**: Maximum HP is determined by the character's class, race, and background.

### Buff/Debuff Mechanics
- **Positive Effects**:
  - **Enhanced Perception**: Characters gain better awareness of their surroundings.
  - **Increased Charisma**: Improved social interactions and persuasion skills.
  - **Enhanced Strength**: Temporary boosts in physical abilities.
  
- **Negative Effects**:
  - **Impaired Focus**: Reduced ability to concentrate on complex tasks.
  - **Slowed Movement**: Decreased speed and agility.
  - **Drowsiness**: Characters may fall asleep more easily.

### Thematic Elements
- **Cannabis Culture**: The world is rich with cannabis-related lore, including historical figures, cultural festivals, and medicinal uses.
- **Legal and Social Dynamics**: Explore the legal status of THC in different regions and how it affects society.
- **Quests and Challenges**: Players must navigate a world where THC plays a significant role in daily life.

### Example Quest
**The Cult of Strahd's Secret**
- **Objective**: Uncover the hidden stash of THC-infused gummies used by the cult to maintain their power.
- **Stages**:
  - **Stage 1**: Gather intelligence on the cult’s activities and locate a secret meeting point.
  - **Stage 2**: Confront the cult members and steal some gummies, ensuring they don’t notice your presence.
  - **Stage 3**: Return to the party and use the stolen gummies to gain an advantage in their next encounter with Strahd.
