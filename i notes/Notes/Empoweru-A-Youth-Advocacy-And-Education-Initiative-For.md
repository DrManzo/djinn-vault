# EmpowerU: A Youth Advocacy and Education Initiative for LGBTQIA+

## Summary
The purpose of this advocacy plan is to create awareness, challenge prejudicial attitudes, and support the mental health and social well-being of LGBTQIA+ youth through a series of community-based outreach and educational seminars.

## Key Points
- **Program Name:** EmpowerU: A Youth Advocacy and Education Initiative for LGBTQIA+
- **Target Group:** LGBTQIA+ youth ages 13-24, their families, educators, and allies
- **Main Goal:** To foster empowerment, resilience, and advocacy by dismantling stereotypes and preventing discrimination through education

## Details
The EmpowerU initiative will consist of monthly interactive seminars and online webinars held at community centers, high schools, and digital platforms. Services offered include:
- Peer mentorship
- Mental health workshops
- Resource navigation
- Ally training
- Public storytelling events in partnership with local LGBTQIA+ organizations

### Plan Description
The main goal is to empower LGBTQIA+ youth by addressing the significant stereotyping, prejudice, and discrimination they face, which impacts their health, safety, and sense of belonging. The program aims to:
1. **Educate:** Provide comprehensive education on the experiences and challenges faced by LGBTQIA+ youth.
2. **Mentorship:** Offer peer mentorship to build supportive relationships within the community.
3. **Workshops:** Conduct mental health workshops to address the unique psychological needs of LGBTQIA+ individuals.
4. **Resource Navigation:** Help participants access essential resources for support and advocacy.
5. **Ally Training:** Train allies, educators, and families on how to be effective supporters.
6. **Storytelling Events:** Facilitate public storytelling events to share personal experiences and promote understanding.

### Stereotypes, Prejudice, and Discrimination
- **Stereotypes:** These are oversimplified generalizations about a group of people that can lead to misunderstandings and discrimination.
- **Prejudice:** Unfavorable feelings or opinions towards members of a particular group based on stereotypes.
- **Discrimination:** Actions taken against individuals because they belong to a certain group, often perpetuated by societal norms.

### Reinforcement in Society
Stereotypes, prejudices, and discriminatory behaviors are reinforced through:
- Media representations that often portray LGBTQIA+ individuals negatively.
- Lack of inclusive policies in schools and workplaces.
- Societal stigma and discrimination from family members or community members.

### Addressing Societal Issues
The EmpowerU initiative aims to address these issues by:
1. **Educating the Public:** Through seminars, webinars, and storytelling events, raising awareness about the challenges faced by LGBTQIA+ youth.
2. **Building Alliances:** Partnering with local organizations to create a supportive network for LGBTQIA+ individuals.
3. **Promoting Inclusion:** Advocating for inclusive policies in schools and workplaces.

### Causes of Prejudicial Attitudes
- **Societal Norms:** Societal norms often perpetuate prejudiced attitudes through media, education, and cultural practices.
- **Individual Biases:** Personal biases can lead to discriminatory behaviors, even among those who claim to be allies.
- **Cultural Factors:** Cultural values and beliefs can influence how individuals perceive and treat members of marginalized groups.

### GCU’s Role
GCU's Statement on Christianity, Race, and Ethnicity actively fights against prejudice, stereotypes, and discrimination by promoting inclusivity and respect. This aligns with the goals of EmpowerU to create a more equitable environment for LGBTQIA+ youth.
