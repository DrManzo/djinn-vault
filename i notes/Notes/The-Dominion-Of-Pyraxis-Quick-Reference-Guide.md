# The Dominion of Pyraxis - Quick Reference Guide

## Summary
This document outlines the essential details and structure of the fictional empire, The Dominion of Pyraxis. It covers key aspects such as government, seasons, months, noble houses, and military hierarchy.

## Key Points
- **Empire Name:** The Dominion of Pyraxis
- **World Name:** Coravian
- **Capital:** Sec-tra
- **Current Year:** 1335 PR (Pyraxis Reckoning)
- **Government Type:** Hybrid: Roman Senate + Parliamentary Cabinet + Wizarding Ministry

## Details
### Prologue & Setting
- **Event:** The Burning of Sec-tra
- **Date:** Year 1335 PR, Fifteenth Year of Emperor Arctus, Pyraxis Season, Fire Month
- **Location:** Gallows Hills (vantage point), Sec-tra (burning city below)
- **POV:** Two mysterious figures watching destruction + younger third figure arriving
- **Trigger:** Orchestrated destruction by conspirators to manipulate Emperor
- **Conspiracy Theme:** "Either a seat at the table... or they want the throne itself"

### The Convocatum (Senate)
- **9 Noble Houses**
  - Solentine: Political/Admin
  - Kavaren: Military/Martial
  - Drakenoth: Magical/Arcane
  - Mercurial: Economic/Trade
  - Valerion: Border/Strategic
  - Thessar: Knowledge/Scholarship
  - Ironwell: Industrial/Resource
  - Morghaven: Shadowed/Ambiguous
  - Kalvennor: Storm/Rebellion

- **Senate Mechanics**
  - Simple majority (5/9 houses) passes legislation
  - Supermajority (7/9 houses) can override Emperor's veto
  - Emperor can veto any Senate decision
  - Meets formally once per lunar cycle (28 days)
  - Emergency sessions callable by Emperor or 5+ house leaders

### Imperial Cabinet Structure
- **Emperor Arctus**
- **Prime Minister**
- **Minister of Internal Affairs**
- **Minister of Defense** (reports to Prime Minister, High Marshal reports here)
- **Minister of Magic**
- **Minister of Treasury**
- **Minister of Foreign Relations**
- **Minister of Public Works**
- **Minister of Public Welfare**

- **Cabinet Limits:**
  - Can propose legislation but Senate must approve
  - Cannot declare war (Emperor + Senate only)
  - Cannot levy new taxes (Senate + Treasury only)
  - Can be removed by Senate no-confidence vote
