# Ethical Dilemma for Student Intern

## Summary
The scenario presents a complex ethical dilemma where a student intern, Frank, must balance professional obligations with personal concerns regarding his roommate's behavior. The key questions revolve around confidentiality agreements, personal feelings, and the impact of multiple interns.

## Key Points
- **Ethical Responsibility**: Maintain confidentiality as per signed agreement.
- **Fault in Breach**: Frank would be at fault for unauthorized access or disclosure.
- **Personal Feelings**: Emotions like empathy and fear influence judgment but must be balanced with professional ethics.
- **Collaboration Impact**: Multiple interns could increase accountability but introduce peer pressure risks.
- **Moral and Ethical Complications**: Conflicting factors create a nuanced dilemma involving personal values, religious upbringing, and safety concerns.

## Details
The scenario involves Frank, an intern at a mental health agency who signed a confidentiality agreement. He is asked to file patient charts but notices one for his roommate, who has been acting strangely. Frank is conflicted about whether looking at the file could explain his roommate's behavior while also violating the confidentiality agreement.

### Ethical Responsibility of the Student Intern
- **Maintain Confidentiality**: Access client information only when necessary and under supervision.
- **Avoid Unauthorized Disclosure**: Do not share sensitive details, even to address personal concerns.
- **Uphold Professional Integrity**: Adhere to workplace policies and legal standards.

### Fault in Confidentiality Breaches
- **Frank's Responsibility**: If he accesses or shares the file without authorization, he would be at fault.
- **Agency/Supervisor Liability**: The agency/supervisor could share liability if they failed to provide adequate safeguards or supervision.

### Impact of Personal Feelings
- **Emotions Influence Judgment**: Frank’s empathy and fear must be balanced against his ethical responsibilities.
- **Objective Decision-Making**: Prioritize confidentiality agreements over personal curiosity or anxiety.

### Collaboration with Multiple Interns
- **Accountability**: Increased through mutual oversight, reducing individual temptation to breach confidentiality.
- **Group Dynamics**: Potential risks (peer pressure) or benefits (collective adherence to ethics).

### Moral and Ethical Complications
- **Morals/Values**: Frank’s desire to help his roommate clashes with his duty to respect privacy.
- **Religious Upbringing**: Teachings about compassion might conflict with contractual obligations.
- **Safety Concerns**: Fear of harm could justify accessing the file but risks legal and professional consequences.
