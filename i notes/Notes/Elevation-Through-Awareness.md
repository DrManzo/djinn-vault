# Elevation Through Awareness

## Summary
The note discusses a deep meditation technique for recognizing the core awareness beneath altered states of consciousness, emphasizing self-awareness and shadow work.

## Key Points
- Recognize the difference between the experience (sensations, thoughts, emotions) and the experiencer.
- The witnessing awareness is the unchanging core of one's being.
- Use this practice to connect with your true self during altered states or intense experiences.

## Details
The note provides a guided meditation for recognizing pure awareness. It suggests pausing periodically to ask oneself: "Who is aware of this experience?" This technique aims to help individuals understand that their true essence remains constant despite changes in consciousness, fostering deeper self-awareness and growth.
