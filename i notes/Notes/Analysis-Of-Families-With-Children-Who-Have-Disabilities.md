# Analysis of Families with Children Who Have Disabilities or Special Needs

## Summary
This assignment aims to help students understand the experiences of families with children who have disabilities or special needs by analyzing a parent interview, applying Piaget’s theory of cognitive development, and discussing community and faith-based resources.

## Key Points
- Analyze middle and late childhood developmental factors.
- Apply Piaget’s theory of cognitive development.
- Discuss processes of development including cognitive, biological, psychological, and social factors.
- Evaluate local community and faith-based support systems.

## Details
The purpose of this assignment is to help students understand the experiences of families with children who have disabilities or special needs by analyzing a parent interview. Students will explore developmental factors, apply Piaget’s theory of cognitive development, and discuss the community and faith-based resources available to support these families, enhancing their ability to apply psychological theories to real-world situations.

For this assignment, you will find and watch an interview of a parent with a child who has a disability or special needs. As an example, watch "Advice for Parents Educating Children with Special Needs," featuring Kelsey General, a mother of two boys diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder (ASD).

Next, write a 1,200-1,500-word paper including the following:

### Analysis of Disability
- **Middle and Late Childhood Overview**: Middle and late childhood, typically defined as ages 6 to 11, is marked by significant growth in physical, cognitive, and social domains. Children at this stage gain mastery over motor skills, develop more advanced reasoning abilities, and experience increased influence from peers and school environments.
- **Type and Background of the Disability**: The selected interview features Kelsey General, a mother of two boys diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder (ASD).
- **Statistics**:
  - Approximately 1 in 6 children and adolescents aged 3–17 years in the U.S. have a developmental disability.
  - Autism affects about 2.94% of this population.
  - The prevalence of all developmental disabilities combined is around 16.65%.

### Analysis of the Interview
- **Interviewer’s Background**: The interviewer asked for the background of the interviewee and their child, including demographics (e.g., age, ethnicity).
- **Additional Questions**: Additional questions you would have asked include:
  - What challenges did your child face in school?
  - How has your experience with parenting a child with special needs changed over time?

### Piaget’s Theory of Cognitive Development
- **Stage of Development**: The child falls under the preoperational stage, characterized by egocentrism and symbolic thinking.
- **Examples**: Specific examples include the child's difficulty in understanding abstract concepts.

### Processes of Development
- **Cognitive Factors**: Consider cognitive development milestones and how they impact the child’s learning process.
- **Biological Factors**: Evaluate the biological factors influencing the child’s development, such as genetic predispositions.
- **Psychological Factors**: Assess the psychological impact on the child and family, including emotional well-being and coping strategies.
- **Social Factors**: Examine social interactions and support systems within the community.

### Resources
- **Local Community Resources**: Describe local resources available to support the family.
- **Faith-Based Support System**: Discuss the kind of faith-based support system they have or how their worldview has been impacted by their current situation.
