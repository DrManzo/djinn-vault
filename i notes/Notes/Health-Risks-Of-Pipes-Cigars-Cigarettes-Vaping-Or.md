# Health Risks of Pipes, Cigars, Cigarettes, Vaping/or E-Cigarettes

## Summary
This note summarizes the health risks associated with pipes, cigars, cigarettes, and vaping/e-cigarettes based on two scholarly sources. It highlights the significant impact these products can have on an individual's health throughout their lifespan.

## Key Points
- Cigar smoking is linked to coronary heart disease, COPD, and increased mortality risk.
- E-cigarettes emit potentially toxic substances that can cause lung damage and increase respiratory issues in youth.

## Details
Cigar smoking poses substantial health risks that can affect individuals over their lifetime. According to a study published in BMC Public Health, cigar use is associated with several serious health outcomes, including coronary heart disease and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) (Bhatnagar et al., 2023). The study notes that cigar smoking confers similar cancer-related risks when compared to cigarette smoking. Furthermore, the depth of inhalation plays a crucial role in the severity of these risks. For individuals who reported moderate to deep inhalation, the mortality risk for current cigar smokers was 5.9 times higher compared to never-smokers, and the risk of COPD increased to 4.5 times higher for current cigar smokers (Bhatnagar et al., 2023).

E-cigarettes and vaping also present significant health concerns. The American Lung Association reports that e-cigarettes contain and emit numerous potentially toxic substances (American Lung Association, 2024). The inhalation of these harmful chemicals can lead to irreversible lung damage and lung disease. Specifically, e-cigarettes have been associated with an increased risk of cough, wheezing, and asthma exacerbations in youth. The primary ingredients found in e-cigarettes, propylene glycol and vegetable glycerin, have been shown to be toxic to cells. Moreover, e-cigarettes produce dangerous chemicals such as acetaldehyde, acrolein, and formaldehyde, which can cause lung disease and cardiovascular disease (American Lung Association, 2024).

Both cigar smoking and e-cigarette use can have long-lasting effects on an individual's health throughout their lifespan. The risks associated with these products include an increased likelihood of developing chronic diseases such as COPD, coronary heart disease, and various forms of cancer. It is important to note that the full extent of the long-term health consequences of e-cigarettes is still being studied, as they are a relatively new product. However, the evidence thus far suggests that they pose significant risks to lung health and overall well-being.
