# Technical Description of Puffco Proxy Quad Uptake Recycler

## Summary
This note provides a technical description for the Puffco Proxy Quad Uptake Recycler, detailing its design and functionality suitable for documentation or listing purposes.

## Key Points
- **Component Overview**: The device is a 3D-printed glassware attachment designed to house a Puffco Proxy modular vaporizer core.
- **Functional Design**: It features four parallel uptake tubes for enhanced cooling and filtration.
- **Fluid Path**: Vapor moves through the diffusion section into the lower water reservoir, then up all four uptake tubes into an upper chamber.

## Details
The Puffco Proxy Quad Uptake Recycler is a 3D-printed glassware attachment designed to enhance the functionality of the Puffco Proxy modular vaporizer. The device serves as a multi-chamber recycler with four parallel uptake tubes, each designed to pull water and vapor from the lower reservoir up into an upper recycling chamber during inhalation.

### Component Overview
- **Design**: The body incorporates four independent uptake channels that facilitate the movement of water and vapor between chambers.
- **Integration**: It is modeled to accept the Puffco Proxy core as a removable module, aligning the Proxy’s vapor outlet with the primary diffusion pathway leading into the water chamber. This design ensures that the attachment serves purely as an upgrade for fluid dynamics and ergonomics.

### Functional Design
- **Water Path**: On inhalation, vapor exits the Proxy chamber, enters the main inlet, passes through the submerged diffusion section into the lower reservoir, and is then drawn up all four uptake tubes into the top chamber. Gravity returns the water from the top chamber to the base, completing the recycle loop.
- **Draw Dynamics**: Multiple parallel uptake tubes reduce per-tube resistance, allowing for a relatively open draw while maintaining high turbulence in the diffusion area for effective cooling.

### Material and Print Considerations
- **Intended Materials**: Community notes recommend printing in heat- and impact-resistant thermoplastics such as PETG or ABS to better tolerate warm vapor, cleaning cycles, and mechanical stress.
- **Print Orientation**: Critical seals and thin-wall sections around the can and joint should be oriented to minimize layer-line stress. Users commonly increase wall count and infill for improved mechanical robustness.

### Operational Use
- **Compatibility**: The recycler is designed for concentrates only via the Puffco Proxy’s integrated ceramic 3D chamber and electronics.
- **Use Case**: In typical operation, users fill the recycler to the recommended level, insert the Proxy core, select the desired heat preset, and inhale lightly to maintain continuous recycling without drawing water into the mouthpiece.
