# Ranking Bungee Jumping Safety Assessment

## Summary
This note provides a structured ranking of information sources for assessing the safety of bungee jumping, emphasizing fatality rates and comparative risks.

## Key Points
- **Rank 1 (Most Important):** Bungee jumping fatalities average 1 in 500,000 jumps.
- **Rank 2:** The death ratio of playing tennis deaths compared to bungee jumping deaths is 5 to 1.
- **Rank 3:** There were at least 18 bungee jumping fatalities between 1986 and 2002—a little more than 1 per year.
- **Rank 4:** In some people, eyesight is impaired for months after bungee jumping.
- **Rank 5 (Least Important):** A single case of a woman's eyesight being impaired 7 months after bungee jumping.

## Details
The ranking is based on the quality and relevance of statistical information. Fatality rates with denominators are considered most useful, followed by comparative statistics, population-level absolute numbers, vague population statements, and single case anecdotes. The exercise highlights that intuitive risk assessment often relies on these types of comparisons.
