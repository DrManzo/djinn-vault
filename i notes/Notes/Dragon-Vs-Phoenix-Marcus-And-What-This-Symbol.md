### Dragon
- **Symbol**: Imperial power, authority, auspiciousness.
- **Psychological Interpretation**:
  - Represents wisdom, wealth, legacy, bloodline, continuity of self.
  - If the dragon dies, it feels like annihilation or total abandonment of who you have been.

### Phoenix
- **Symbol**: Renewal, rebirth, legitimacy through surviving destruction.
- **Psychological Interpretation**:
  - Symbolizes promise of tomorrow and radical renewal.
  - Even if the dragon kills the phoenix, "the Phoenix will come back."
  - The phoenix can rebuild from the past but may still follow the same script.

### Inner Civil War
- **Conflict**: Dragon vs. Phoenix represents an inner struggle between preserving identity (dragon) and radical transformation (phoenix).
- **Integrative Approach**:
  - Keep dragon’s wisdom and continuity while allowing phoenix’s capacity for renewal to burn off what is dead.
  - Push toward qualitative transformation, not just survival.
