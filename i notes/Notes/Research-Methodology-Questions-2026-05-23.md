# Research Methodology Questions

## Summary
A series of multiple-choice questions related to research methods, replication studies, and cultural considerations in psychological research.

## Key Points
- Replication study can change the independent variable.
- Internal validity is the highest priority for theory testing.
- A study examining motor responses of newborns would be least influenced by cultural differences.
- Conducting a mirror analysis addresses the file drawer problem.

## Details
1. **Replication Study and Variable Change**
   - Question: Which of the following can an replication study change?
     - A) The dependent variable
     - B) The procedures or participants

2. **Internal Validity in Theory Testing**
   - Question: Why do studies conducted for theory testing mode deemphasize ecological validity?
     - A) Internal validity is the highest priority for theory testing.

3. **Cultural Influence on Research**
   - Question: Which of the following cities would be least likely to be influenced by cultural differences?
     - D) A study examining motor responses of newborns

4. **Mirror Analysis and File Drawer Problem**
   - Question: Cindy decides to conduct a mirror analysis for her research project. Her advisor recommends contacting several researchers in the field for articles that were not published or found no effects. This will address which issue?
     - A) The file drawer problem

5. **Conceptual Replication Study**
   - Question: Melanie conducts an initial study on caffeine and cognitive performance, then measures verbal analogies instead of math problems to test the same hypothesis. This is known as:
     - C) Conceptual replication
