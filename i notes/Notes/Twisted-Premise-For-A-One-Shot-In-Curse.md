# Twisted Premise for a One-shot in Curse of Strahd

## Summary
A one-shot campaign where players, once-mighty gods, are cast down and trapped in the Death House, facing psychological horror and existential dread.

## Key Points
- Players start as dethroned gods in the Death House.
- Their former domains are now corrupted and unreliable.
- Mortal forms and human frailties add depth to their struggle.
- Betrayal by high priests haunts them.
- The Death House itself is a metaphor for their fall from grace.

## Details
The premise of starting players as dethroned gods in the Death House offers a unique and terrifying experience. Here’s how you can flesh out this scenario:

### Divine Domains in Decay

Each player's former godly domain could be reflected in a corrupted form within the house, symbolizing their fall from grace:
- **Player 1 (Former God of Light):** The room that once shone with divine light is now filled with flickering candles and shadows.
- **Player 2 (Former Goddess of Life):** The lush gardens that were once vibrant are now overgrown and twisted, with plants that seem to move on their own.

### Mortal Coils

The shock of being forced into mortal forms adds a layer of human vulnerability:
- Players must navigate the challenges of human life, such as hunger, thirst, and physical limitations.
- They may struggle with emotions they once only observed in others, leading to internal conflicts.

### Betrayal's Bitter Taste

Echoes of their betrayal by high priests torment them:
- Cultists in the house worship twisted versions of these former followers, creating a sense of betrayal and fear.
- Players might hear whispers or see fleeting visions of their treacherous high priests, causing paranoia and distrust among themselves.

### The Death House: A Divine Prison

The Death House itself becomes a metaphor for their fall from grace:
- **Unholy Architecture:** Each room could represent a twisted version of their divine realms.
  - **Room of Light:** Flickering candles and shadows instead of radiant light.
  - **Garden of Life:** Overgrown, twisted plants that seem to move on their own.

- **Cultists of the Fallen:** The cultists in the house worship twisted versions of these former followers, creating a sense of betrayal and fear.
  - Players might hear whispers or see fleeting visions of their treacherous high priests, causing paranoia and distrust among themselves.
