## Summary
The current business model of Typhons Forge LLC, including its subsidiaries like The Forge (online smoke shop), Terp Tribe (TT), and Asgard Acres (AA), is not inherently too complex to be perceived negatively by banks. - **Business Model Structure:** The current structure with multiple divisions/subsidiaries is manageable.
- **Bank Evaluation Factors:**
  - Business Plan Viability
  - Financial Projections and Cash Flow
  - Collateral and Assets
  - Industry Experience of Founders
  - Credit History
