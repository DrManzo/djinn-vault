# How Long Can I Leave Filament in a Sea Reality Space PX4L

## Summary
The filament can be stored for an extended period, but it is recommended to use it within six months to ensure optimal performance.

## Key Points
- Optimal storage time is 6 months.
- Exposure to air and moisture can degrade the filament quality over time.
- Regularly checking and using the filament helps maintain its integrity.

## Details
Filament, especially PLA (Polylactic Acid), should be stored in a dry environment to prevent degradation. While the exact shelf life of filament like PX4L can vary based on storage conditions, it is generally recommended to use it within six months for best results. Exposure to air and moisture can cause the filament to absorb humidity, leading to issues such as warping or inconsistent printing quality. Regularly checking and using the filament helps maintain its integrity and ensures that you get the best performance from your 3D prints.
