## Details
Javier is working on a fantasy book that draws heavily from established universes. The project aims to incorporate elements of the Malazan Book of the Fallen, Mistborn universe, and Harry Potter, while maintaining its own unique narrative. Here are some key points to consider:

### Magic System
- **Influence**: 
  - **Malazan Book of the Fallen**: Focus on political intrigue and complex magic systems.
  - **Mistborn Universe**: Incorporate transformative and elemental magic.
  - **Harry Potter Universe**: Include a class system among both magical users and common people.

- **Mechanics**:
  - The magic system should be detailed but can remain mysterious in the prequel. This allows for gradual revelation of its full extent as the story progresses.

### Empire Structure
- **Lands and Families**:
  - Multiple lands are forming an empire with 8-9 noble families.
  - These families are rising to power through conflict, aligning or opposing the Emperor described in the final scene.

- **Timeline**:
  - The prequel should establish the empire's power structure and the political landscape. It will set up the conflicts that drive the main narrative.

### Class System
- **Social Issues**:
  - A class system is present among both magic users and common people.
  - Magic users can be from various backgrounds, not just nobility or pure blood.

### Prequel's Narrative Purpose
- **Establishment**: The prequel should establish the empire’s power structure and political landscape. It will set up the conflicts that drive the main narrative.
- **Conflict and Power Dynamics**:
