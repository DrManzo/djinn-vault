# Social Psychology by Heinzen & Goodfriend (2nd Edition, 2021)

## Summary
This note provides an overview of the textbook "Social Psychology" by Thomas Heinzen and Wind Goodfriend, published in 2021. It covers key features, chapter structure, core areas of coverage, pedagogical approach, and applications.

## Key Points
- **Book Overview**: Award-winning text presenting social psychology as a science-driven conversation.
- **Key Features**: New pop culture examples, additional diversity coverage, recent controversies, over 100 new citations, streamlined chapter structure.
- **Chapter Structure**: 12 main chapters plus 8 mini application chapters.
- **Core Areas of Coverage**: Individual level processes, social influence, interpersonal relations, applied areas.
- **Pedagogical Approach**: Personal narrative integration, historical contextualization, methods-in-context approach, core question framework, pop culture connections.
- **Research Foundation**: Emphasis on empirical research and scientific methodology. Covers recent controversies in classic studies.

## Details
The textbook "Social Psychology" by Heinzen & Goodfriend (2021) is designed for undergraduate social psychology courses and serves students across various majors by demonstrating the relevance of social psychology to daily life and future careers. The book's structure and content are organized around core questions central to scientific inquiry, making it a comprehensive guide for understanding social behavior.

### Core Areas of Coverage
- **Individual Level Processes**: Self-concept, social identity, social cognition, person perception, attitudes.
- **Social Influence**: Conformity, compliance, social roles, obedience, persuasion techniques.
- **Interpersonal Relations**: Attraction, helping behavior, prosocial actions, aggression, prejudice.

### Pedagogical Approach
- Personal narrative integration to illustrate concepts.
- Historical contextualization of concepts within their development.
- Methods-in-context approach where research methods are integrated throughout the text.
- Core question framework for each chapter.
- Pop culture connections to maintain student engagement.
