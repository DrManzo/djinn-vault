# The Math Behind Djinn Cash Suite

## Summary
The Djinn Cash Suite is a comprehensive personal finance document that utilizes various mathematical models to guide financial planning and management.

## Key Points
- **50/30/20 Rule**: Allocates 50% of net income for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings/debt.
- **Zero-based Budgeting**: Ensures every dollar is allocated so that total expenses equal the net income.
- **Compound Interest**: Central to wealth-building; formula \( A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt} \).
- **Debt Payoff Math**:
  - **Avalanche Method**: Orders debts by highest interest rate first, minimizing total interest paid.
  - **Snowball Method**: Orders debts by lowest balance first for psychological wins.
- **FICO Credit Score Model**: Weighted sum of five factors: payment history (35%), credit utilization (30%), length of credit history (15%), credit mix (10%), and new credit (10%).
- **Utilization Ratio**: Should be kept under 10% for maximum score benefit.
- **Dollar-cost Averaging (DCA)**: Spreads investments to reduce price-timing risk, calculating average cost per share.
- **Risk/Reward Ratio**: Requires a minimum 1:2 ratio for speculative investments.

## Details
The Djinn Cash Suite covers personal finance across 20 sections, each driven by specific mathematical models. The core math includes:

### Budgeting Math
- **50/30/20 Rule**:
  - Needs = 0.50 × Net Income
  - Wants = 0.30 × Net Income
  - Savings/Debt = 0.20 × Net Income

- **Zero-based Budgeting**:
  \[
  \text{Net Income} - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{Category}_i = 0
  \]

### Compound Interest (The Core of Wealth-Building)
\[
A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt}
\]
Where:
- \( A \) = Future Value
- \( P \) = Principal
- \( r \) = Annual interest rate
- \( n \) = Times compounded per year
- \( t \) = Time in years

### Debt Payoff Math
- **Avalanche Method**:
  \[
  M = P \cdot \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}
  \]
  Where:
  - \( M \) = Monthly payment
  - \( P \) = Balance
  - \( r \) = Monthly rate (APR ÷ 12)
  - \( n \) = Number of payments

- **Snowball Method**:
  Orders debts by lowest balance first, providing psychological wins.

### Credit Score Math
- **FICO Model**:
  Weighted sum of five factors: 
  - Payment history (35%)
  - Credit utilization (30%)
  - Length of credit history (15%)
  - Credit mix (10%)
  - New credit (10%)

- **Utilization Ratio**:
  \[
  \text{Utilization} = \frac{\text{Total Revolving Balance}}{\text{Total Revolving Limit}} \times 100
  \]
  Keep this under 10%.

### Investment Returns Math
- **Dollar-cost Averaging (DCA)**:
  Average cost per share is calculated as:
  \[
  C = \frac{\text{Total Dollars Invested}}{\text{Total Shares Acquired}}
  \]

- **Risk/Reward Ratio**:
  Requires a minimum 1:2 ratio for speculative investments.

### Options Greeks (Section 8)

- **Delta**: Sensitivity to underlying asset price changes.
