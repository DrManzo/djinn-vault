# Academic Discussion Post Template

## Summary
This note provides a template for creating academic discussion posts and peer responses that meet specific criteria, including well-developed paragraphs, proper APA citations, and a professional tone.

## Key Points
- Main post requires 3 well-developed paragraphs.
- Includes exactly 3 scholarly sources with one being Cavanaugh, J. C. (2024).
- Peer response should be 2 paragraphs, acknowledging main points and adding new information.
- Maintains a supportive, collegial tone.

## Details
The template ensures that academic discussion posts are structured to meet the following requirements:
1. **Introduction**: Introduce the topic and its significance.
2. **Body Paragraphs**:
   - **Paragraph 1**: Discuss the first main point with supporting evidence and citations.
   - **Paragraph 2**: Discuss the second main point with supporting evidence and citations.
   - **Paragraph 3**: Discuss the third main point, integrating personal observations and concluding thoughts.
3. **Conclusion**: Summarize key points and their implications.
4. **References**: List all sources in APA format.

### Main Post Example

Hello, Class and Professor,

Understanding [specific topic/issue] is indeed a significant aspect of adult development and aging (Cavanaugh, 2024). This response will explore the multifaceted nature of this issue and discuss its implications for policy and practice. The first major point are numerous and interconnected. According to Cavanaugh (2024), [key point from textbook relevant to topic]. These factors often lead to [consequences/outcomes].

The second main point involves [specific aspect/development] in the context of aging populations. Research by [Author, Year] identified [specific finding/statistic], which highlights the importance of [related factor/implication]. This underscores the need for [policy/solution] to address these challenges effectively.

In conclusion, understanding and addressing [topic/issue] is crucial for both theoretical and practical reasons. It not only informs our knowledge but also guides policy-making in ways that can significantly impact the quality of life for older adults.
