## Details

1. **Current Pattern Assessment**: 
   - The "playing the field" approach, where only money is invested and no emotional or physical involvement occurs, creates a controlled environment.
   - This framework eliminates trust to maintain power and distance.
   - When this system gets challenged (as suggested by an argument), it triggers a need for safety and connection.

2. **What Yoshi Represents**:
   - Yoshi serves as a *trust anchor*—a person who knew the user before their current controlled state, representing a time of authentic connection.
   - The feeling of missing her isn’t about her specifically but about the version of oneself that existed in relation to her—a more natural and less controlled self.

3. **Four Questions Analysis**:

   - **Are you missing what you had?**: Partially; missing the feeling of relational safety but not the actual relationship dynamics.
