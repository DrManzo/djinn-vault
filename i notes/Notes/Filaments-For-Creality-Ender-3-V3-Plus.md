# Filaments for Creality Ender 3 V3 Plus

## Summary
This note provides a comprehensive guide on compatible filaments for the Creality Ender 3 V3 Plus, focusing on PLA, PETG, and carbon fiber variants. It also offers recommendations based on specific project needs.

## Key Points
- **PLA**: Ideal for beginners, toys, and rapid prototyping.
- **PETG**: Suitable for functional parts due to its heat resistance, durability, and chemical resistance.
- **Carbon Fiber Filaments**: Enhanced with carbon fiber for increased rigidity and strength.

## Details
The Creality Ender 3 V3 Plus supports a wide range of filament types, all compatible with a 1.75mm diameter. Here are the recommended filaments based on your needs:

### Compatible Filaments

- **PLA**: 
  - Ideal for beginners.
  - Easy to print and colorful.
  - Suitable for toys, storage boxes, and decorative items.

- **PETG**:
  - Semi-rigid material with excellent chemical resistance.
  - Minimal warpage and smooth surface finish.
  - Good for general-purpose prints like bottles.
  - Heat resistant, durable, and chemically resistant.
  - Flexibility without breaking.

- **PLA-CF (PLA Carbon Fiber)**: 
  - Enhanced rigidity and strength.
  - Suitable for professional-level prints.

- **PETG-CF (PETG Carbon Fiber)**:
  - Reinforced with carbon fiber for heavy-duty applications.

- **CR-Carbon**: Creality's carbon fiber filament option for professional-level prints.

### Important Notes

All filaments must be 1.75mm diameter. When using carbon fiber or glow-in-the-dark filaments, a hardened nozzle is recommended due to their abrasive nature.

### Recommendations

For your specific needs:
- **PLA**: Suitable for toys and initial design iterations.
- **PETG**: Ideal for functional e-rig prototypes due to its heat resistance, durability, and chemical resistance.

## Workflow Strategy
Use PLA for rapid prototyping and switch to PETG for final functional parts. Both materials print well on the Ender 3 V3 Plus without requiring an enclosure. PETG requires a bit more practice but is not difficult once settings are dialed in.
