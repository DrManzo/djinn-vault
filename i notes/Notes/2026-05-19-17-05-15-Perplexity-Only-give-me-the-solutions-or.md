PART I: LIVING ARRANGEMENTS

1. EMERGING ADULTHOOD

Emerging or Young Adulthood Living Arrangement: Living with parents

Emotional: Living with parents can lead to potential stressors, such as conflicts over rules and boundaries. Parents may feel taken advantage of, creating a cycle of misunderstanding (Copp et al., 2017).

Social: Privacy may be restricted, limiting romantic relationships and socializing at home. Peers might feel uncomfortable visiting due to parental presence; however, this arrangement could positively influence social interactions by reducing exposure to risky behaviors (Copp et al., 2017).

Physical: Access to quality nutrition is generally better when living with parents. Young adults may benefit from parental guidance on health habits. However, stricter rules might reduce the risk of substance experimentation (Copp et al., 2017).

Financial: Young adults can save money by staying at home, paying reduced or no rent and utilities. If they struggle financially, it has less impact on their credit compared to living independently (Copp et al., 2017).

Emerging or Young Adulthood Living Arrangement: Living with roommates

Emotional: Conflicts may arise due to different living patterns, potentially leading to stress and anxiety; however, it can also provide companionship and shared experiences (Arnett, 2004).

Social: Living with roommates offers opportunities for social interaction and building friendships. It can expand social networks and improve social skills (Arnett, 2004).

Physical: The physical environment may vary depending on roommates' habits; there might be increased exposure to diverse lifestyles, both positive and negative (Arnett, 2004).

Financial: Sharing living expenses can significantly reduce individual costs, allowing for more financial independence. However, conflicts over bill payments may occur (Arnett, 2004).

1. MIDDLE AGE

Middle Adulthood Living Arrangement: Coresidence with adult children

Emotional: Coresidence can provide emotional support and companionship but may also lead to stress due to shared responsibilities and potential conflicts (KFF Health News, 2022).

Social: Opportunities for social interaction are present. However, the quality and quantity of social connections may vary, and some individuals might feel isolated from their previous social networks (KFF Health News, 2022).

Physical: The physical environment can be shared or independent; professional medical care is available but may limit physical activity and independence (KFF Health News, 2022).

Financial: Costs are typically high due to the need for comprehensive care services. Long-term care insurance or government programs may help offset expenses (KFF Health News, 2022).

1. LATE ADULTHOOD

Late Adulthood Living Arrangement: Nursing home

Emotional: Nursing homes can provide a sense of security and safety but may also lead to feelings of loss, loneliness, or depression due to reduced independence and separation from family (KFF Health News, 2022).

Social: Opportunities for social interaction with peers and staff are available. However, the quality and quantity of social connections may vary, and some residents might feel isolated from their previous social networks (KFF Health News, 2022).

Physical: Professional medical care and assistance with daily activities are readily available but may limit physical activity and independence (KFF Health News, 2022).

Financial: Costs are typically high due to the need for comprehensive care services. Long-term care insurance or government programs may help offset expenses (KFF Health News, 2022).

Late Adulthood Living Arrangement: Independent living

Emotional: Independent living can provide a sense of autonomy and freedom but may also lead to feelings of loneliness if social support is lacking (KFF Health News, 2022).

Social: Opportunities for social interaction are present. However, the quality and quantity of social connections may vary, and some individuals might feel isolated from their previous social networks (KFF Health News, 2022).

Physical: The physical environment can be tailored to individual needs; professional medical care is available but may limit physical activity and independence (KFF Health News, 2022).

Financial: Costs are typically moderate due to the need for some level of care services. Long-term care insurance or government programs may help offset expenses (KFF Health News, 2022).
