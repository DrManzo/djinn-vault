# Time Management & Accountability System for ADHD

## Summary
Javier identifies the need for a hybrid approach combining physical accountability with digital tools to manage time and tasks effectively, especially given his ADHD-related executive function challenges.

## Key Points
- **Time blindness** in ADHD brains affects planning, task initiation, and time perception.
- **Executive function challenges** lead to difficulties organizing thoughts, prioritizing tasks, and completing them on time.
- The Rabbit R1 AI has limitations such as a 7-day reminder restriction and no time-blocking features.
- A physical notebook serves as a constant tactile reminder of obligations.

## Details
Javier's struggle with time management and accountability is rooted in executive function challenges common among ADHD individuals. These challenges include:
- **Time blindness**: Difficulty sensing how much time has passed and estimating task duration, stemming from dopamine dysregulation and working memory deficits.
- **Executive function challenges**: Issues organizing thoughts, prioritizing tasks, initiating work, and completing follow-through.

The Rabbit R1 AI offers basic reminder functionality but falls short in comprehensive time management:
- **Current capabilities**:
  - Voice-activated reminders
  - Smart timers and alarms with natural language ("remind me in 15 minutes")
  - Advanced memory system that learns preferences over time
  - AI voice recording with automatic transcription

- **Critical limitations**:
  - **7-day window restriction**: Reminders set beyond seven days are documented but may not trigger notifications.
  - **Device must be powered on**: Missed reminders appear on a "reminder card" when restarted, but there's no lock screen notification if the device was off.
  - **No time-blocking features**: Cannot visualize or schedule blocks of time.
  - **Limited task management**: No integration with calendars, no persistent notifications for ongoing tasks.
  - **No productivity scaffolding**: Cannot break down large projects or provide executive function support.

A physical notebook serves as a constant reminder and can be used to:
- Write down new ideas and tasks immediately.
- Set blocks of time for specific activities.
- Receive notifications and reminders about duties, tasks, and promises made.
