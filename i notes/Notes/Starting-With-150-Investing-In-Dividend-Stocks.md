# Starting with $150: Investing in Dividend Stocks

## Summary
This note outlines a step-by-step approach to investing $150 in dividend-paying stocks, focusing on safety and long-term growth.

## Key Points
- Open and fund your Chase brokerage account.
- Choose safe, reliable dividend stocks like Dominion Energy (D), Enterprise Products Partners (EPD), Pfizer (PFE), or JPMorgan Chase (JPM).
- Set up a Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP) to automatically reinvest dividends.
- Monitor dividend payment dates and enable DRIP in your account settings.

## Details
To start investing $150 in dividend stocks, follow these steps:

### 1. Open and Fund Your Chase Brokerage Account
- **Action**: Open a brokerage account with Chase and deposit the initial amount of $150.

### 2. Choose Safe, Reliable Dividend Stocks
- **Dominion Energy (D)**: Utility stock with a forward dividend yield of about 4.9%, stable but limited growth.
- **Enterprise Products Partners (EPD)**: Offers a distribution yield around 6.5% and has increased its payout for 26 consecutive years, taxed differently than regular dividends.
- **Pfizer (PFE)**: A large pharmaceutical company with a history of paying dividends.
- **JPMorgan Chase (JPM)**: A blue-chip bank stock with a current yield of about 2.4% and strong dividend growth.

### 3. Set Up Dividend Reinvestment (DRIP)
- **Action**: Enable DRIP in your Chase account settings to automatically reinvest dividends into more shares, compounding returns over time.
- **Resources**:
  - [Chase Account Settings](https://www.chase.com/)
  - [DRIP Plan Setup](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/smartest-dividend-stocks-buy-150-right-now-0)

### 4. Monitor Dividend Payment Dates
- Check the ex-dividend and payment dates for your chosen stocks.
- Example: If you buy Dominion Energy or JPMorgan Chase before their next ex-dividend date, you’ll receive a dividend on the upcoming payment date.

### 5. Stay Diversified and Conservative
- **Action**: With $150, you may only be able to buy one or two stocks or ETFs.
- **Tips**:
  - Prioritize dividend safety by sticking to companies with a long history of steady or growing dividends.
  - Reinvest dividends using DRIP to maximize compounding and long-term growth.
  - Diversify as your portfolio grows to reduce risk.

## Example: Getting Your First Dividend Soon
- **Action**: Suppose you buy shares of Dominion Energy (D) today. If the ex-dividend date is in the next few weeks, you’ll receive a dividend payment on the upcoming payment date—often within a month or two.
- **DRIP**: By enabling DRIP, your dividend will be used to buy additional shares automatically.

## Key Tips for Safety and Growth
- Prioritize dividend safety by sticking to companies with a long history of steady or growing dividends.
- Reinvest dividends using DRIP to maximize compounding and long-term growth.
- Diversify as your investment grows to reduce risk.
- Avoid high-risk, high-yield stocks with yields above 8–10%.
