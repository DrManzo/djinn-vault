# Excel Assignment for Statistics Class

## Summary
The assignment involves analyzing data on the hours of television watched by elementary children. The goal is to perform various statistical analyses and create visual representations in Excel.

## Key Points
- **Class:** Statistics
- **Data Provided:** 30 observations of hours of television watched by elementary children.
- **Level of Measurement:** Ratio (meaningful zero, equal intervals).

## Details
1. **Frequency Distribution:**
   - **Steps to Create Frequency Distribution:**
     1. List unique values in Column C starting from C2.
     2. Label the adjacent column as "Frequency."
     3. Enter the formula `=COUNTIF($A$2:$A$31,C2)` in D2 and drag it down.
