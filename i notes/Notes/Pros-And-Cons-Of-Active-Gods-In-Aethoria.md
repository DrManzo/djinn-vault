# Pros and Cons of Active Gods in Aethoria

## Summary
Exploring the incorporation of active gods into the world of Aethoria, similar to the Malazan series, involves weighing pros, cons, and limitations.

## Key Points
- **Rich Worldbuilding**: Adds depth and complexity.
- **Dynamic Storytelling**: More unpredictable plot developments.
- **Character Development**: Opportunities for complex growth.
- **Thematic Depth**: Enhances themes of mortality, power, and consequence.
- **Narrative Techniques**: Versatile storytelling methods.

## Cons
- **Narrative Complexity**: Risk of overwhelming the narrative.
- **Diminished Protagonist Agency**: Mortal characters might be overshadowed.
- **Balancing Issues**: Maintaining story balance can be challenging.
- **Consistency Challenges**: Managing divine interventions without inconsistencies.
- **Overshadowing Mortal Characters**: Focus might shift away from core narratives.

## Limitations and Considerations
- **Power Restrictions**: Implement limitations on gods' powers.
- **Divine Non-Interference Pact**: Establish agreements limiting direct intervention.
- **Mortal Catalysts**: Use mortal agents or champions for divine actions.
- **Essence Fluctuations**: Link divine abilities to Essence state.
- **Factional Conflicts**: Create conflicts between god factions.

## Integration with Existing Worldbuilding
- **Tie Gods to Factions**: Align deities with existing political structures.
- **Divine Artifacts**: Introduce powerful Essence-infused artifacts.
- **Godly Domains**: Link gods' spheres of influence to key societal aspects.
- **Divine Manifestations**: Use venues like the Whispering Theatre for subtle interventions.
- **Essence and Divinity**: Explore relationships between gods and Essence manipulation.
