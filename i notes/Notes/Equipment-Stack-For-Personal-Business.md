# Equipment Stack for Personal Business

## Summary
The note discusses the setup of a personal business, focusing on equipment selection and infrastructure needs. It outlines a detailed plan including desktop tower, laptop, video/audio gear, and connectivity options.

## Key Points
- Desktop Tower ($2,858)
- Laptop ($3,200)
- Video/Audio Gear ($3,485)
- Connectivity ($1,130)

## Details
The equipment stack is designed to support both the business operations and personal use. The desktop tower serves as the primary compute engine with a Ryzen 7 processor and RTX 4070 Ti Super GPU, capable of running mid-tier AI models like Mistral locally. It features 64GB RAM and dual SSDs for efficient model loading and data handling.

The laptop is configured to be a mobile workstation with an i9 processor and RTX 5090 GPU, acting as a backup compute node. This setup allows for distributed inference between the desktop and laptop, ensuring consistent performance whether working from home or on-the-go.

For video and audio recording, a Sony A6700 camera is used for high-quality 4K footage at 120fps, complemented by the Rode Wireless GO II microphone for clean audio. Additionally, a Shure SM7B microphone with an interface provides backup options. Lighting equipment ensures professional appearance during video recordings.

Connectivity includes Cat6A wired Ethernet between the desktop and laptop to minimize latency, ensuring smooth model serving. Wi-Fi is also included for flexibility in network connections.

The total cost of the equipment stack is $10,673, with a 10% contingency buffer added, bringing the total to $11,740.
