# Build a 6' x 6' L-Shaped Desk with Metal Hanging Pedestals and Using Elements Parts

## Summary
This note provides instructions for building an L-shaped desk measuring 6' x 6', using metal hanging pedestals and elements parts.

## Key Points
- **Worksurfaces**: Two rectangular surfaces, each measuring 72" x 30".
- **Corner Connector**: A corner unit or angle bracket to join the two rectangular surfaces.
- **Support Structure**: Metal hanging pedestals with drawers for storage and structural support.
- **Legs or Frame**: Metal legs or frame supports for stability on the open ends of the desk.
- **Modesty Panel** (Optional): For added privacy.

## Details
To build a 6' x 6' L-shaped desk using Elements parts and metal hanging pedestals, follow these steps:

1. **Attach the Two Rectangular Worksurfaces**: Use a corner connector to join the two rectangular surfaces seamlessly.
2. **Secure Hanging Pedestals**: Place one pedestal beneath each worksurface at appropriate positions for storage and support.
3. **Install Metal Legs or Frame Supports**: Add metal legs or frame supports on the open ends of the desk for stability.
4. **Optional Accessories**: Consider adding a modesty panel to the front of the desk if privacy is needed.
