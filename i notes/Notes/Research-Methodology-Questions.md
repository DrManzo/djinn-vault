# Research Methodology Questions

## Summary
A series of questions related to research methodology, focusing on statistical design and control in psychological studies.

## Key Points
- **Within-subjects Design**: Increases between-group differences by reducing within-group variance.
- **Sample Size**: Larger sample sizes generally increase precision and power.
- **Matched Group Design**: Used when double-blinded is not possible.
- **Placebo Effect**: Observed as a significant effect in the study.

## Details
1. **Within-subjects Design**: This design reduces within-group variance, making between-group differences appear larger, thus increasing the risk of false positives.
2. **Sample Size and Variability**:
   - A small sample size with greater variability does not produce the most narrow and precise confidence interval.
   - Increasing the sample size increases precision and power in a study.
3. **Matched Group Design**: Used as an alternative to double-blinded studies when necessary.
4. **Placebo Effect**: Found as "an effect" in the context of the study.
