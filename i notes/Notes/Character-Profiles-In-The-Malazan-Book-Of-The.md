# Character Profiles in "The Malazan Book of the Fallen"

## Summary
This note provides concise profiles for key characters from "The Malazan Book of the Fallen," focusing on their identities, powers, and roles within the narrative.

## Key Points
- **Iskaral Pust**: High Priest of Shadow with sorcery and healing abilities.
- **Shadowthrone (Ammanas / Kellanved)**: God of High House Shadow with strategic and divine powers.
- **Cotillion (The Rope / Dancer)**: Patron of Assassins, co-ruler of Shadow with possession and assassination skills.
- **Quick Ben**: Bridgeburners' premier mage with extensive warren access.

## Details
### Iskaral Pust – Magus of High House Shadow
- **Identity & Role**: Old Dal Honese man, formally declared Magus of High House Shadow. Known for his chaotic yet sharp pattern recognition and foreknowledge.
- **Powers**:
  - Adept in Meanas (illusion/veils), Thyr (light), Rashan (darkness/assassination).
  - Healer and surgeon capable of advanced sorcery and alchemy.
  - Can call on Shadowthrone's direct intervention.
- **Cognitive Profile**: Outwardly chaotic, logorrheic, but functions as a mask for high competence. Surrounded by bhok’arala who treat him like a god.

### Shadowthrone (Ammanas / Kellanved) – God of High House Shadow
- **Identity & Origin**: Former Emperor of the Malazan Empire; ascended with Dancer to become Ammanas.
- **Domain & Capabilities**:
  - Controls High House Shadow and its Hounds, manipulates the Warren of Shadow.
  - Master plotter with long-term strategic vision.
  - Deep knowledge of Azath structure and global magical architecture.
- **Cognitive Profile**: Hyper-strategic, playful, and vindictive. Balances chaos with foresight.

### Cotillion (The Rope / Dancer) – Patron of Assassins
- **Identity & Origin**: Former legendary assassin who ascended with Kellanved to become Cotillion.
- **Domain & Capabilities**:
  - Master of possession and bodily hijack, controls or empowers assassin cults.
  - Highly mobile divine presence; interacts directly with mortals like Mappo and Icarium.
- **Cognitive Profile**: Precise, surgical thinker. Develops a more humane stance over time.

### Quick Ben (Ben Adaephon Delat) – Bridgeburners' Premier Mage
- **Identity & Role**: Squad mage of the 9th Squad, known for his extensive warren access and technical brilliance.
- **Powers**:
  - Access to multiple warrens, giving him extraordinary versatility.
  - Uses finesse and traps rather than brute force.
