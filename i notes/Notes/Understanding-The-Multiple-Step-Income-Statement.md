# Understanding the Multiple-Step Income Statement

## Summary
This post explains the multiple-step income statement, a detailed financial report used by merchandising companies to break down revenues and expenses. It covers key sections such as net sales, cost of goods sold (COGS), operating expenses, and other revenues/expenses.

## Key Points
- **Net Sales**: Total sales minus returns, allowances, and discounts.
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**: Subtract COGS from net sales to find gross profit.
- **Operating Expenses**: Includes selling and administrative costs. Subtract these from gross profit to get income from operations.
- **Other Revenues/Expenses**: Any additional revenues or expenses not related to core business activities.
- **Net Income**: Final profit figure after adding/subtracting other revenues/expenses.

## Details
A multiple-step income statement is a financial document used by merchandising companies to provide a detailed view of their financial performance. It starts with net sales, which are calculated by subtracting returns, allowances, and discounts from total sales revenue. The next step involves calculating the cost of goods sold (COGS), which represents the direct costs attributable to the production of the goods sold by the company. By subtracting COGS from net sales, we arrive at gross profit.

The statement then lists operating expenses, which are divided into selling and administrative costs. Subtracting these expenses from gross profit gives us income from operations, reflecting how profitable the core business activities are. Any other revenues or expenses that do not relate to everyday operations are added or subtracted to find net income, the final profit figure.

For example, if a shop has $500,000 in net sales, $300,000 in COGS, and $150,000 in operating expenses, then:
- Gross Profit = Net Sales - COGS
  \[ \$200,000 = \$500,000 - \$300,000 \]
- Income from Operations = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses
  \[ \$50,000 = \$200,000 - \$150,000 \]

This step-by-step layout makes it easier to understand how a business earns money and helps managers and investors make informed decisions.
