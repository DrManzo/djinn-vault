## Details
The assignment requires creating an infographic that outlines the basic functions of the brain and nervous system. Key points to include are:

### Cellular Level Highlights
- **Neuron Structure**: Soma, dendrites, axon, myelin sheath, nodes of Ranvier, and axon terminals.
- **Neurons vs. Glial Cells**:
  - **Astrocytes**: Support the extracellular environment and provide nutrients (most abundant).
  - **Oligodendrocytes**: Form myelin sheaths in the CNS; one can myelinate multiple axons.
  - **Microglia**: Brain's immune cells that remove debris and protect against invaders.

- **Action Potential**:
  - A rapid electrical signal progressing through seven stages: resting potential, depolarization, peak, repolarization, hyperpolarization, and return to rest. Takes approximately 1 millisecond.

- **Synaptic Transmission**:
  - A 9-step chemical communication process involving synthesis, action potential arrival, vesicle release, neurotransmitter binding, receptor activation, ion channel opening, signal transmission, reuptake, and degradation.

### Brain Lobes
- **Frontal Lobe**: Involved in reasoning, planning, behavior, emotions.
- **Parietal Lobe**: Processes spatial information, sensory input, and language comprehension.
- **Temporal Lobe**: Responsible for memory, hearing, and speech processing.
- **Occipital Lobe**: Handles visual processing.

### Nervous System Divisions
- **Central Nervous System (CNS)**: Brain and spinal cord.
- **Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)**: Cranial nerves, spinal nerves, ganglia.
