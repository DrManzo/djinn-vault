# Dangers of Steroid Use and Exercise Throughout Lifespan

## Summary
This note provides a detailed analysis of the health risks associated with anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS) use, particularly when combined with exercise. It highlights the immediate and long-term dangers for both adults and adolescents.

## Key Points
- Increased mortality risk by threefold.
- Higher frequency of hospital admissions.
- Cardiovascular issues, liver damage, psychological disturbances.
- Stunted growth in adolescents.
- Elevated risk of health problems later in life.
- Perceived benefits outweighed by serious side effects.

## Details
Anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS) use poses significant health risks throughout an individual's lifespan, especially when combined with exercise. Users face a threefold increase in mortality risk compared to non-users and are more likely to be hospitalized due to various complications (Horwitz et al., 2018). The dangers extend beyond immediate health concerns, affecting multiple bodily systems and potentially leading to long-term complications.

For instance, AAS use can result in cardiovascular issues such as hypertension and heart disease, liver damage from hepatotoxicity, and psychological disturbances like mood swings and depression. Additionally, the impact on reproductive health is particularly concerning; users may experience erectile dysfunction and infertility (Horwitz et al., 2018). These side effects are not limited to adults; adolescents who engage in AAS use may face stunted growth and an elevated risk of health problems later in life.

When combined with intense exercise regimens, the strain on the body is amplified. This can lead to more severe cardiovascular complications and an increased likelihood of injury due to overexertion (Mayo Clinic, n.d.). The perceived benefits of enhanced performance and muscle growth are far outweighed by the potential for serious, life-altering side effects. For example, even seemingly minor side effects like acne and male pattern baldness can have significant psychological impacts, affecting an individual's self-esteem and mental well-being throughout their life.
