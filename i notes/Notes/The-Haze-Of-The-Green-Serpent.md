# The Haze of the Green Serpent

## Summary
This note outlines a campaign story for an adult-oriented D&D system based on 5e, using THC products as ability points and hit points. It includes a narrative hook, world-building details, and unique class and race mechanics.

## Key Points
- **Campaign Name:** The Haze of the Green Serpent
- **Setting:** Verdania, a realm where THC is the source of all magic.
- **Main Conflict:** Blight corrupting the land, draining THC from crops and communities.
- **Objective:** Restore the Sacred Herb to save Verdania.

## Details

### Campaign Overview
Welcome to **Verdania**, a world where the mystical Green Serpent—an ancient entity of nature and consciousness—has blessed the land with the Sacred Herb. Five hundred years ago, the Serpent's breath brought forth the first Haze—a transformative fog that altered reality. Those who learned to channel its essence became the first adventurers: warriors who could rage beyond mortal limits, spellcasters who bent reality through smoke and vapor, rogues who vanished into aromatic clouds, and healers who distilled life itself.

But now, the **Blight** spreads—a corrupting force that drains THC from the land, leaving withered crops, powerless mages, and dying communities. The Green Serpent has fallen silent. Without the Sacred Herb, civilization crumbles.

You are Level 5 Initiates of the **Order of the Verdant Flame**, trained specifically to operate at peak capacity. Your mission: venture into the blighted territories, discover the source of the corruption, restore the sacred grow operations, and ultimately awaken the Green Serpent before Verdania falls into permanent darkness.

### World Building: The Five Kingdoms

1. **Floralhaven** (Elf Territory)
   - **Cultivation:** Premium flowers and exotic strains.
   - **Architecture:** Treehouses with hanging gardens.
   - **Export:** Fine buds in all Verdania.
   - **Current Status:** 60% blighted; royal gardens dying.

2. **The Forge Peaks** (Dwarf/Barbarian Territory)
   - **Cultivation:** Edibles and concentrates.
   - **Architecture:** Mountain fortresses with underground grow rooms.
   - **Export:** Potent brownies, gummies, and wax.
   - **Current Status:** 40% blighted; forges cooling as power wanes.

3. **The Crystal Spires** (High Elf/Wizard Territory)
   - **Cultivation:** Wax, shatter, and crystalline concentrates.
   - **Architecture:** Glass towers with alchemical laboratories.
   - **Export:** Pure THC crystals and magical wax.
   - **Current Status:** 70% blighted; spells failing catastrophically.

4. **The Wandering Isles** (Halfling/Bard Territory)
   - **Cultivation:** Tinctures, drinks, and liquid concentrates.
   - **Architecture:** Riverboat communities and floating taverns.
   - **Export:** THC-infused wines, ales, and elixirs.
   - **Current Status:** 50% blighted; the music is dying.

5. **The Shadowgrove** (Dark Elf/Rogue Territory)
   - **Cultivation:** Hybrid strains and experimental crossbreeds.
   - **Architecture:** Hidden underground networks.
   - **Export:** Specialized strains for specific effects.
   - **Current Status:** 30% blighted but spreading rapidly.

### The Campaign Arc Structure

#### Act I: The Withering (Levels 5-8)
*"Something is very wrong in Verdania."*

**Hook:** Your party gathers at **The Last Harvest Festival** in Floralhaven, where the Grand Cultivator announces the worst crop failure in five centuries. The sacred herbs are withering, and the land is dying.

#### Act II: The Search (Levels 9-12)
*"Find the source of the Blight."*

**Hook:** Your party travels to various kingdoms, uncovering clues about the Blight's origin. Each kingdom faces unique challenges and threats.

#### Act III: The Awakening (Levels 13-16)
*"Awaken the Green Serpent."*

**Hook:** After gathering all necessary information, your party confronts the source of the Blight and awakens the Green Serpent to restore balance to Verdania.
