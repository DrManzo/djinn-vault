# Assignment Guide for Empirical Research and Validity Analysis

## Summary
This note provides a structured guide to complete an assignment that involves summarizing an empirical article, identifying its association claim, and interrogating the study's four validities (construct, external, statistical, internal) using APA formatting.

## Key Points
- **Assignment Purpose**: Summarize an empirical article and apply the three claims, four validities framework.
- **Article Selection Criteria**: Peer-reviewed, association claim, correlational design.
- **Template Use**: Must use provided "Article Interrogation Template" for full credit.
- **APA Formatting**: 12pt Times New Roman, 1-inch margins, title page.

## Details
The assignment involves a structured approach to summarizing and analyzing an empirical research article. Key steps include:

### Step 1: Selecting the Empirical Article
- **Criteria**:
  - Peer-reviewed study.
  - Association claim (e.g., "Social media use is linked to anxiety in adolescents").
  - Correlational design.

### Step 2: Summarizing the Article
- **Association Claim Identification**:
  - Identify and quote the two variables involved.
  
- **Study Overview**:
  - Participants: Sample size, demographics, recruitment method.
  - Methods: How variables were measured (e.g., surveys, observational data).
  - Results: Strength/direction of association.

### Step 3: Interrogating the Four Validities
- **Construct Validity**:
  - Evaluate operationalization of variables.
  - Discuss potential biases and measurement tools used (e.g., validated scales like GAD-7).

- **External Validity**:
  - Assess generalizability.
  - Note sample limitations in demographics or cultural context.

- **Statistical Validity**:
  - Analyze statistical rigor: Significance, effect size, Type I/II errors.

- **Internal Validity**:
  - Address potential confounds (e.g., socioeconomic status).

### Step 4: APA Formatting and LopesWrite Submission
- **APA Style**: Use 12pt Times New Roman, 1-inch margins, title page.
- **Citation**: In-text citations and reference list in APA format.
