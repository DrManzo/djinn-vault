# Starting a Tax Service and Notary Business in California

## Summary
This note provides the necessary steps to start legitimate tax preparation and notary public businesses in California, leveraging bilingual skills for competitive advantages.

## Key Points
- **Tax Preparation Business**: Requires 60-hour qualifying education course, PTIN, $5,000 surety bond, LiveScan fingerprinting, and CTEC registration.
- **Notary Public Business**: Needs 6-hour education course, passing exam, LiveScan fingerprinting, and application to the Secretary of State.

## Details
To legally prepare taxes for compensation in California, you must become a California Registered Tax Preparer (CRTP) through CTEC. Here are the steps:

### Required Credentials

1. **60-Hour Qualifying Education Course**: 45 hours on federal tax law and 15 hours on California state tax law from a CTEC-approved provider, completed within the past 18 months.
2. **IRS Preparer Tax Identification Number (PTIN)**: Obtainable through the IRS website for $30.75.
3. **$5,000 Surety Bond**: Purchase from an insurance/surety agent to protect clients against fraud or negligence.
4. **LiveScan Fingerprinting and Background Check**: Submit electronic fingerprints at approved locations; approval takes 2-8 weeks.
5. **CTEC Registration Fees**: $100 application fee plus $33 registration fee (plus processing fees).

### Recommended Course Providers
- Universal Accounting School Tax Preparation Program
- National Association of Tax Professionals (NATP) Course
- National Tax Training School (NTTS)
- H&R Block Income Tax Course
- IRS Tax Training

### Annual Renewal Requirements
- Complete 20 hours of continuing professional education (CPE) from a CTEC-approved provider by October 31.
- Maintain the $5,000 surety bond.
- Renew registration and pay fees before the October 31 deadline.

### Advanced Credential Option
Consider pursuing **Enrolled Agent (EA)** status—the highest credential the IRS awards. EAs must pass a three-part comprehensive IRS exam covering individual and business tax returns, complete 72 hours of continuing education every three years, and gain unlimited representation rights before the IRS.
---

For becoming a California notary public:
1. **Initial Credentials**:
   - Be 18 years or older and a legal California resident.
   - Complete a 6-hour state-approved notary education course (3 hours for renewals).
   - Pass a written examination with 70% or higher (21 of 30 scored questions correct).
   - Complete LiveScan fingerprinting for background check.

2. **Timeline and Process**:
   - **Week 1**: Complete the 6-hour education course and receive your certificate.
   - **Weeks 2-3**: Schedule and pass the written exam at an approved testing location.
   - **Weeks 3-5**: Complete LiveScan fingerprinting (2-3 week processing).
   - **Weeks 5-8**: Submit application to the Secretary of State with required fees (approximately $40 exam fee plus application fees).

Your notary commission is valid for 4 years from the date of issuance.

### Bilingual Advantage
Your bilingual skills provide significant competitive advantages in California's diverse market:
- **Tax Services**: You can serve non-English speaking clients who are often underserved, expanding your client base.
- **Notary Services**: While you can notarize documents in languages other than English, being able to communicate directly with signers without translators is crucial.
