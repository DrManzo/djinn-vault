# Correct Order of Accounts in a Trial Balance Worksheet

## Summary
The correct order for accounts in a trial balance worksheet is as follows:

D.) 
- Balance sheet accounts (assets from most liquid to least liquid)
- Liabilities (short term followed by long term)
- Owner's equity/owner's drawings/retained earnings
- Income statement accounts (revenue, expenses)
- Sum of debits and sum of credits

## Key Points
- The worksheet starts with balance sheet accounts.
- Followed by liabilities.
- Then owner's equity/owner's drawings/retained earnings.
- Next are income statement accounts.
- Ends with the sum of debits and credits.

## Details
The standard format for a trial balance ensures accuracy in financial records. By organizing accounts in this order, it helps in identifying any discrepancies or errors before finalizing financial statements.

# Case Study: Trial Balance Worksheet

## Summary
A trial balance worksheet includes three main sections: 
- **Trial Balance**
- **Adjustments** 
- **Adjusted Trial Balance**

Each section lists accounts with their respective debit and credit entries.

## Key Points
- **Trial Balance Section**: Lists all accounts with debits and credits.
- **Adjustments Section**: Records adjustments needed for the trial balance to be accurate.
- **Adjusted Trial Balance Section**: Shows the final balances after applying adjustments.

## Details
The characteristics of a trial balance worksheet are crucial for ensuring financial accuracy. By systematically organizing accounts, it helps in identifying any discrepancies or errors before finalizing financial statements.
