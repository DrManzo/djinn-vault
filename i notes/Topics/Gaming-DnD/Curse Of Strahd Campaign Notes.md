# Curse Of Strahd Campaign Notes

## Summary
Notes on running Curse of Strahd and related gothic horror D&D content, including a Death House oneshot concept where players begin as dethroned gods forced into mortal forms. Includes THC-themed campaign mechanics adapted for dispensary venue play at The Artist Tree.

## Key Points
- Death House serves as an effective standalone oneshot for 1st-level characters, blending horror and mystery in a single session
- Oneshot premise: players begin as dethroned gods trapped in the Death House, cast down by their own high priests or followers
- Each player's former divine domain is reflected in corrupted form within the house, with diminished and unreliable powers
- Players experience the shock of mortal frailty, haunted by phantoms of their treacherous followers
- The house architecture becomes a metaphor for their fall — each room represents a twisted version of their former divine realms
- Cultists within the house worship corrupted versions of the players' former divine selves, forcing confrontation with their darker nature
- Core themes: power and corruption, faith and betrayal, identity without godhood, and the terror of mortality
- Oneshot structure: awakening/disorientation, exploration revealing the fall, confrontation with cultists and corrupted essences, climax with moral choice, revelation of Barovia
- Top 5 one-shot adaptable campaigns: Death House, Secrets of Skyhorn Lighthouse, Grammy's Country Apple Pie, A Wild Sheep Chase, Scarab of Death
- Top 5 long-term campaigns: Curse of Strahd (levels 3-10), Storm King's Thunder, Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage (to level 20), Tomb of Annihilation, Princes of the Apocalypse (levels 1-15)
- THC-themed adaptation replaces traditional HP with gummy-based hit points, with class-specific THC consumption mechanics
- The Artist Tree dispensary venue offers Studio Lounge (100 capacity) and Loft (50 capacity) spaces with projection screens and customizable layouts for hosting sessions
- Dank Master (DM) mechanics include Cosmic Tokens for narrative influence, Munchies Meter for party-wide tracking, and DM THC level that scales with player success
- THC dependency ranking by class: Vapor Mage (extreme), Psychonaut (very high), Budtender (high), Blunt Barbarian (high), Smoke Shaman (high), Alchemist (moderate-high), Strain Seeker (moderate), Munchies Master (moderate), Canna-Knight (low-moderate), Cultivator (low)

## Details
The Death House oneshot concept creates a powerful entry point into Curse of Strahd. Players who emerge from the house as fallen gods find themselves in Barovia — a land where another dark god reigns supreme, with Strahd as its dark lord. This creates thematic resonance between their personal fall and the broader campaign narrative.

The THC-themed campaign system ("Dank Nights") integrates cannabis culture into core D&D mechanics. Gummy-based HP varies by class tolerance (1 gummy = 4-8 HP depending on class). Overconsumption triggers disadvantages on Wisdom and Intelligence checks but grants advantage on Charisma-based social interactions. Cosmic Tokens earned through critical rolls allow players to influence the Dank Master's decisions through Reality Warp, Hazy Revelation, Narrative Twist, Cosmic Insight, and Strain Synergy effects.

The Artist Tree partnership provides a real-world venue that aligns thematically with the campaign. Their art-gallery aesthetic and rotating local artist displays can inspire in-game quests and atmospheric elements.
