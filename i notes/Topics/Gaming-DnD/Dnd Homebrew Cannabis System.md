# Dnd Homebrew Cannabis System

## Summary
A fully functional D&D 5E homebrew system replacing traditional HP and ability resources with THC product consumption mechanics. Includes five classes with product-specific consumption rules, five playable races with THC-related buffs and debuffs, and optional Double Take and Penalty Pass mechanics for adult groups.

## Key Points
- System is built on standard D&D 5E rules with THC consumption as flavor and resource mechanic for HP and abilities
- Base character level is 5; all standard 5E rules remain intact with THC overlay
- Five classes with specific THC product affinities: Barbarian (edibles/gummies 5mg), Bard (drinkables/shots 10mg), Druid (flowers/hits 5-10mg), Fighter (gummies 5mg sustained), Cleric (mixed products, flexible)
- Wizard and Warlock use wax concentrates (dabs) for precision and potency
- Five playable races with THC bonuses: Human Naturalist (+2 HP, flexible), Elf Refined (+1 HP, wax specialty), Dwarf Hearty (+3 HP, heavy tolerance), Halfling Quick (+1 HP, quick recharge), Dragonborn Potent (+5 HP, universal products)
- Elves have increased affinity for flower-based products; Barbarians use edibles to go berserk; Bards use drinkables for social enhancement
- Double Take option: consume extra THC for advantage on failed checks
- Penalty Pass option: skip consumption and give enemies advantage instead
- Token/candy alternative system allows identical mechanics without actual THC consumption
- Edibles dosed in 5mg increments; drinkables as shot classes; inhalables measured as 5-10 second hits
- Safety protocols: adults only (21+), consent required, designated sober monitor, medical awareness, stop-anytime protocol
- Three deliverable documents: Complete Rules System, Character Templates (one-page quick-fill sheets), and Master How-To Guide
- Character sheets include ability scores, THC pool, combat stats, class features, damage conversion tables, and product-specific consumption mechanics
- Session structure: 15 min safety/rules, 90 min character creation, then gameplay in 3-4 hour sessions
- Starting THC pool calculated by race and class combination
- All mechanics are generic and easy to distribute — players fill in their name and are ready to play

## Details
The system is designed for sit-down-and-play accessibility. Each class has a one-page character sheet with pre-filled ability scores, THC pool calculations, combat stats, and class features. Players select a race from the Races Reference file to determine HP bonuses, Double Take and Penalty Pass mechanics, and starting THC pool.

The damage-to-consumption conversion chart translates in-game damage into THC product consumption. For example, a Barbarian taking 15 damage would consume three 5mg gummies. The system supports both actual THC consumption and token/candy substitutes with identical mechanical effects.

Safety is emphasized throughout: 21+ verification, explicit consent, medical condition awareness, and a designated sober monitor who can call stop at any time. The token/candy alternative ensures anyone can participate regardless of their comfort with actual consumption.
