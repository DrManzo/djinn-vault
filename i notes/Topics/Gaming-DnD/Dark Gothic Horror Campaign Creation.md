# Dark Gothic Horror Campaign Creation

## Summary
Research into mature, roleplay-heavy D&D campaigns as alternatives to Curse of Strahd, including published modules and a custom homebrew campaign pitch. Focuses on political intrigue, social encounters, and moral ambiguity with minimal required combat.

## Key Points
- Published RP-heavy alternatives to Curse of Strahd: Waterdeep: Dragon Heist (urban heist and politics), The Wild Beyond the Witchlight (solvable through bargaining and trickery), Journeys Through the Radiant Citadel (culture and politics anthology), Courts of the Shadow Fey by Kobold Press (courtly intrigue)
- Custom campaign pitch: "The Masks of Virellion" — a city-state ruled by anonymous oligarchs wearing enchanted masks that hide identity and broadcast illusions
- The Masks of Virellion is designed for levels 3-8 with mature themes of political corruption, blackmail, and addiction to magical mask personas
- Three major factions: Council of Masks (anonymous oligarchs with secret identities), Bleak Lanterns (underground smugglers and secret-sellers), House of Reflections (mask creators offering addictive illusion rituals)
- Act I: The Vanishing Mask — investigation of disappearances linked to Council events, masked party social scenes, identity theft twist
- Act II: Faces in the Gutter — slum exploration, trust-building with gangs, negotiating with Bleak Lanterns, moral dilemma about exposing refugee routes
- Act III: The Shattered Council — discovery of identity-overwriting ritual, public council session confrontation, multiple possible endings
- For level 8 parties with 6 players: treat PCs as major players from start, use Renown/Reputation tracks, design short story-critical combats or soft conflicts
- Level 8 scaling: councilors with legendary-style social power, Bleak Lanterns as full spy networks, mask magic capable of rewriting memories and faking divination
- Recommended encounter types: Grand Masquerade (social rounds shifting faction reputations), Council Crisis Session (lobbying and vote tracking), Moral Trial (defend or condemn NPCs with cross-examination)
- Session structure: opening social situation, 1-2 short conflicts (only one combat), climax is always a choice or negotiation not a boss fight
- Front-loaded secrets at Session 0 drive conversation: each PC gets a theme-relevant secret (e.g., former Bleak Lantern, disowned councilor child, mask addiction)
- Combat should serve drama: fights include emotional ties, ideological arguments mid-battle, and consequences beyond victory or defeat
- Courts of the Shadow Fey identified as the closest published module matching RP-heavy political intrigue requirements (5e compatible, Kobold Press)

## Details
The Masks of Virellion campaign is designed to run like a prestige television series, with each session structured as an episode. The opening social situation establishes stakes, brief conflicts create tension, and the climax is always a meaningful choice rather than a combat encounter.

The front-loaded secret system is particularly effective for generating organic roleplay. By giving each player a personal connection to the campaign's themes at Session 0, the DM ensures that every player has invested stakes in the narrative. Secrets like "you are already addicted to the House of Reflections' illusions" create immediate internal conflict and roleplay opportunities.

For large parties (6 players at level 8), the action economy makes combat trivial. The recommendation is to either make encounters short and dangerous with elite enemies and environmental hazards, or replace combat entirely with skill challenges, chases, and social conflicts. The Reputation/Renown system provides mechanical rewards for social success, giving players tangible progression even without combat.
