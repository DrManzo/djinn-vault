# TASK-039 — Part 10 — Full Scene Transcript (Phone / Voice)

> **Note type:** Practical Script Reference
> **Date:** 2026-06-23
> **Purpose:** A complete, realistic phone scene from opening to aftercare. Written as it actually sounds, with stage directions. Pull this before a scene. Read it out loud alone first if it helps.
> **Format:** [Stage direction] *"Spoken words"*

---

## Before the Call

> One slow breath. Drop your jaw slightly. Let your chest settle.
> You're not performing. You're just shifting into the part of you that already knows how to hold authority.
> You've done this your whole life. Just not in this room.

---

## OPENING

*[Normal conversational tone for the first minute or two. Just talking. Let them relax.]*

*[Then — one beat of silence. Deliberate. You decided to stop. Not running out of things to say. Decided.]*

*[Register drops. Lower. Slower. Like you have nowhere else to be.]*

**You:** *"Alright."*

*[3 second pause. Say nothing.]*

**You:** *"We’re starting now. Put everything else down and just listen to my voice."*

*[4–5 second pause. Let it land completely.]*

*[They respond — okay, yes, whatever they say.]*

**You:** *"Good."*

*[Just that word. Pause after it. Let it sit.]*

---

## EARLY SCENE — Building Focus

**You:** *"Close your eyes."*

*[Wait for them to confirm or go quiet.]*

**You:** *"Good boy."*

*[Low. Weighted. 2 second pause after.]*

**You:** *"Tell me what you feel right now. Just your body. Nothing else."*

*[They describe something — warmth, heart beating, whatever it is. Listen fully. Don’t fill the gap.]*

**You:** *"Yeah."*

*[Pause 3 seconds.]*

**You:** *"Stay there."*

*[That’s it. Don’t add to it. Those two words just told them: I heard you, I approve, don’t move. That’s all three things they need right now.]*

---

## DEEPENING — Narrowing Their World

*[A few minutes in. Responses are coming a little slower. That’s good. That means it’s working.]*

**You:** *"Good. Now just breathe. Follow my pace."*

*[Breathe audibly and slowly yourself. They’ll match it without thinking. Biological mirroring — it’s automatic.]*

*[After 3–4 slow breaths together:]*

**You:** *"There you are."*

*[5 second pause.]*

**You:** *"You don’t have to hold anything right now. That’s mine to carry. All you do is stay with me."*

*[Pause. Let it fully land.]*

**You:** *"Good pet."*

*[Quiet. Almost to yourself. Like you’re just stating something true.]*

---

## MID SCENE — Ownership Register

*[They’re going deeper. Answers are shorter. Voice is softer. This is the moment to move into ownership language.]*

**You:** *"You don’t get to think right now. That’s not your job."*

*[Flat. Certain. Slightly bored — like it’s obvious.]*

*[3 second pause.]*

**You:** *"Your only job is to feel what I tell you to feel."*

*[Pause.]*

**You:** *"Can you do that?"*

*[They answer — yes, mm, whatever it is.]*

**You:** *"Good boy."*

*[Low. Immediate. Like a reward landing exactly on time.]*

---

## PEAK — Maximum Ownership

*[They’re as deep as they’re going. Monosyllabic responses or silence. Voice barely above a whisper if they’re speaking at all. This is it.]*

*[Your voice is at its lowest and slowest here. Almost a murmur. No effort in it — like gravity.]*

**You:** *"You’re mine."*

*[5 second pause. Do not fill it.]*

**You:** *"Every part of you."*

*[Another pause. Let that sit in the silence.]*

**You:** *"Mine to keep."*

*[Don’t say anything else for a full 6–8 seconds. The silence after those three phrases is doing more work than any additional sentence could.]*

*[If breeding language is confirmed okay:]*
**You:** *"All of you. I’m keeping all of you."*

*[Then silence again.]*

---

## IF THEY PUSH BACK

*[Mid-scene they say something resistant. Try to take control. Get sarcastic. Whatever it is.]*

*[Do not react immediately. Do not defend. Do not explain. Count 3 seconds internally.]*

**You:** *"Mm."*

*[2 second pause.]*

**You:** *"Try that again."*

*[Calm. Almost amused. Like it didn’t move you even slightly. Because in this register — it didn’t.]*

*[If they continue pushing:]*

**You:** *"Give me your color."*

*[That’s a check-in. It tells them you’re paying attention AND you’re still in control of the frame.]*

---

## CLOSING — Bringing Them Back

*[The scene is done. You decide when. Begin slowing your pace further. Warmth starts entering your voice — slowly, not all at once.]*

**You:** *"Alright."*

*[Pause.]*

**You:** *"Come back to me now."*

*[Give them time. 10–15 seconds of quiet. They’re surfacing. Don’t rush it.]*

**You:** *"We’re done. You did so well."*

*[Warmer now. Authority edge softening.]*

*[Pause.]*

**You:** *"How are you feeling right now? And I mean actually — not the quick answer."*

*[Listen. Really listen. Don’t prepare a response while they’re talking.]*

---

## AFTERCARE — Just You Now

*[No register to maintain. No frame to hold. Just you, talking to someone you give a damn about.]*

**You:** *"I’m right here. Take your time."*

*[If they seem scattered or emotional:]*

**You:** *"Get some water if you need it. I’m not going anywhere."*

*[After they’ve settled, name something real and specific you noticed:]*

**You:** *"You went really quiet around [whenever it happened]. That was — that was good. I noticed that."*

*[Before ending the call:]*

**You:** *"I’ll check in with you later tonight. You good?"*

*[Wait for a real answer. Then let them go.]*

## One Thing To Do Before Your First Scene

Read this out loud alone. Not in your head — out loud. Your voice needs to hear itself say these words before the pressure of a real scene. The awkwardness you feel reading it now will drop significantly the second time through. By the third time it starts to feel like yours.

The words are not magic. Your voice and your presence are. The words are just the vehicle.
