## THE SCENE — Phone / Voice, Full Intensity

---

### OPENING — The Cut

*[Mid normal conversation. You decide. One beat of silence — deliberate, not awkward.]*

*[Register drops. Lower jaw. Slower. Like you own the next ten minutes and both of you know it.]*

**You:** *"Hey."*

*[3 second pause. Don't fill it.]*

**You:** *"Stop whatever you're doing. Right now."*

*[pause]*

**You:** *"Just my voice. Nothing else exists right now."*

*[They go quiet or say okay or mm — doesn't matter what it is.]*

**You:** *"Good."*

*[One word. Let it land. 3 seconds.]*

### EARLY — Taking Control

**You:** *"Close your eyes."*

*[wait]*

*[pause — let them answer]*

**You:** *"Good boy."*

*[pause — warmer underneath the certainty, just slightly]*

**You:** *"Good pet."*

*[Responses are getting slower. Good. Keep going.]*

**You:** *"Feel the weight of my hand on the back of your neck right now."*

*[anchor — just slightly warmer. contrast between the two tones is the whole mechanism.]*

*[anchor — possessive, not harsh]*

*[pause]*

**You:** *"Good boy."*

*[immediate, low, certain — reward landing exactly on time]*

### IF THEY GO VERY QUIET AND YOU'RE NOT SURE

*[Calm. No urgency. Just checking.]*

*[Listen. Really listen. Don't prepare a response while they're talking.]*
