# Story Big Brain Class Assignment

## Summary
A class assignment for a Visual Basic Studio programming class integrates the Dominion of Pyraxis world-building with a creative narrative about "the Big Brain" invasion. The assignment demonstrates functions and procedures through a battle scene between Emperor Arctus and High Steward Brax against the Big Brain, using magical logic as a metaphor for programming concepts.

## Key Points
- The assignment prompt involves a "Big Brain" — an immense intellect emerging within the narrative realm, requiring alliance with a peer's avatar to confront it
- The story serves as a potential in-world prologue or companion short story tied to the Emperor's infamous battle at the center of the Dominion
- Three narrative options were developed: Emperor's duel with Big Brain, Big Brain's perspective, and Emperor allied with a shadowed peer
- The chosen approach pairs Emperor Arctus with High Steward Brax Solentine fighting together against the Big Brain during the prologue timeline
- Magic is scripted as defensive sigils and wards across the city like a living codebase, with each function representing a line of logic sustaining Dominion stability
- Arctus and Brax sync their incantations in perfect sequence: loops of defense, recursive patterns of energy feeding protective systems
- Each spell must call the next precisely or the Dominion's core will unravel
- The Big Brain represents malicious recursion or corrupted code, attacking the structures that keep reality intact
- The battle becomes ideological programming as much as physical combat — Brax insists on containment while Arctus pushes for purification
- Victory comes when Arctus overrides Brax's nested barrier with a final destructive procedure, stabilizing the Dominion but seeding mistrust
- The VB code implementation uses Sub procedures (actions that do not return values) and Functions (which return String values) to display story text
- The assignment reflection explains that functions take input, perform work, and return results, while procedures are series of steps that run without returning values
- The story integration frames the first fracture between Arctus and Brax, providing political seed for later distrust in the trilogy

## Details
The assignment successfully bridges creative writing and programming education by mapping magical concepts to coding fundamentals. Defensive wards become function calls, recursive spell patterns become loops, and the Big Brain's reality-warping becomes corrupted code. The partnership between Arctus and Brax mirrors the relationship between a main function and a support procedure — both necessary, both contributing different capabilities, but with inherent tension in their approaches. The narrative choice to have Arctus override Brax's barrier provides both a satisfying resolution to the assignment and a seed for the trilogy's political conflict. The VB implementation demonstrates the practical distinction between Sub procedures and Functions while telling a compelling story within the Dominion's lore.
