# Story Concept And Prologue Development

## Summary
The story concept draws from Malazan, Mistborn, First Law, and Harry Potter influences to create a fantasy world with Roman-inspired government, class-based magic systems, and political intrigue. The Dominion of Pyraxis on the world of Coravian features nine powerful noble families, an Emperor who is "first among equals," and a prologue centered on the orchestrated burning of Sec-tra.

## Key Points
- The Dominion of Pyraxis is the empire name, with Coravian as the world name and Sec-tra as the capital city
- The prologue "The Burning of Sec-tra" depicts orchestrated destruction by conspirators to manipulate the Emperor
- Emperor is characterized as "last amongst equals, first to battle, first sacrifice for the empire" who maneuvers around court while keeping family and marriage in line
- Nine noble houses are already established, mostly aligned with the Emperor but with some opposition
- Magic system uses wands, some verbal magic, high fantasy magic, and human abilities influenced by breeding
- Government structure draws from Roman Senate and HP Wizarding World with class system but no pure-blood requirements
- Timeline framework: Age of Fracture (0-4,200 years ago), Age of Unification (4,200-1,200 years ago), The Troubling (1,200-300 years ago), Modern Era (300 years ago to present)
- Dating system uses Pyraxis Reckoning (PR) as primary calendar, House Reckoning (HR) for individual noble houses, and seasonal/lunar tracking for common people
- Four seasons: Pyraxis (Spring), Solentine (Summer), Valdross (Autumn), Mortain (Winter), each with three months
- Magic should be shown through action rather than explanation, with 2-3 sensory tells for major magic types used consistently
- Prologue structure: opening hook with Sec-tra burning, historical context, character interaction revealing they caused the destruction, complication with youth arriving through spatial magic, closing hook about wider implications
- The Emperor's role is as a necessary figure whose will represents the people's will, maneuvering through court politics
- Noble house naming convention follows House + surname + regional title structure with distinct power bases

## Details
The world-building synthesis combines Malazan's epic scope and cost-based magic, Mistborn's rule-based systems, First Law's cynical realism and dark humor, and HP's institutional complexity without its rigid class structures. The Emperor's characterization as "first among equals" creates a unique power dynamic — he leads by necessity and sacrifice rather than absolute authority. The nine noble houses provide a built-in political structure with competing interests. The dating system with multiple calendars creates natural tension, as different factions literally disagree on what year it is. The prologue's burning of Sec-tra establishes the central mystery: who orchestrated it, why, and what does it mean for the Emperor's legitimacy? The timeline framework provides historical depth without overwhelming the narrative, with the prologue set at Year 1 of the Pyraxis Reckoning.
