# Aethoria Geography And Climate

## Summary
Aethoria is a Victorian-inspired fantasy realm with four primary regions, each with distinct geographical and climatic features that shape culture, politics, and daily life. The Grand Metropolis of Ironhaven serves as the central hub, surrounded by the Essence-rich Misty Moorlands, the magically powerful Sundered Isles archipelago, and the industrialized, smog-choked Rustlands.

## Key Points
- Ironhaven is a sprawling Victorian city divided into distinct districts: Upper Terraces (wealthy), Smokestack Quarter (industrial), and the shadowy Undercity, surrounded by a massive wall for protection and Essence control
- The Misty Moorlands are wild, Essence-rich lands surrounding Ironhaven, home to strange creatures and unpredictable magical phenomena that supply rare materials for Essence catalysts
- The Sundered Isles are an archipelago known for powerful Adepts and ancient magical traditions, with diverse landscapes including beaches, forests, and possible volcanic regions
- The Rustlands are a vast industrialized northern region constantly shrouded in smog, dominated by Rust Barons who push technological advancement over traditional magical practices
- Climate in Ironhaven is temperate with industrial smog and magical influences affecting weather patterns
- The Misty Moorlands feature a misty, temperate climate with frequent fog and unique magical storms driven by high Essence concentration
- The Sundered Isles may have tropical to temperate microclimates created by isolation and magical energies
- The Rustlands have a harsh, polluted climate with limited natural vegetation and reliance on steam-powered technology
- The Undercity's shadowy, damp environment creates unique ecosystems and magical phenomena worth exploring
- Flora and fauna in the Moorlands are influenced by magical energies, affecting the broader ecosystem
- Climate interacts with magical practices differently in each region — mist affects Essence manipulation, smog impacts steam technology
- Environmental and social impacts of Rustlands industrialization include political and economic implications of the Rust Barons' technological push

## Details
Each region's unique characteristics influence plot, character development, and narrative. The wall around Ironhaven controls both physical access and the flow of Essence, making it a political tool. The Sundered Isles' geographical isolation reinforces their cultural and magical independence from mainland Aethoria. The Rustlands' environmental degradation creates a visual and thematic contrast with the Moorlands' wild, Essence-rich beauty. The Undercity beneath Ironhaven represents the hidden, repressed aspects of the society — a place where the rules of the surface world do not fully apply.
