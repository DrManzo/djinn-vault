# Fantasy Worldbuilding Hub

## Summary
Central index for all world-building notes related to the dark fantasy realm of Aethoria and the Dominion of Pyraxis. This hub organizes notes by topic area, providing quick navigation to geography, politics, society, economy, magic, religion, military, education, transportation, international relations, and methodology notes.

## Key Points
- Aethoria is a Victorian-inspired dark fantasy realm with four primary regions: Ironhaven, Misty Moorlands, Sundered Isles, and Rustlands
- Political power is layered: nominal Crown authority, Assembly of Lords, Guild of Artisans, and the true power — the Shadow Council led by Master Thorne
- Society is stratified across upper, middle, working, and lower classes with a shadowy underworld operating beneath visible structures
- Economy is guild-controlled with banking houses managing finance and international trade
- Magic system centers on Essence Manipulation using personal catalyst objects, with distance-based limitations and a cosmic origin story
- Religion features active gods inspired by Malazan, with divine power tied to worship and Essence availability
- Military combines traditional command with specialized magical and technological units
- Education and healthcare blend magical and technological approaches with class-based access disparities
- Transportation and communication networks are stratified by class, from Essence-powered airships to steam-powered underground trains
- International relations center on tension with the Sundered Isles, providing opportunities for diplomatic intrigue and conflict
- World-building follows a six-phase methodology designed for sustainable, incremental development
- Dark fantasy audiobook recommendations provide reference material from established authors

## Details
The world of Aethoria and the Dominion of Pyraxis represents a synthesis of multiple dark fantasy influences: Malazan's epic scope and active gods, Mistborn's rule-based magic, First Law's cynical realism, and institutional complexity inspired by Roman governance. The central tension between magic (Essence) and technology (steam power) drives political, economic, and social conflict. The Shadow Council's covert control creates a world where nothing is as it appears. Characters like Corvus Shadowblade and Master Thorne navigate this landscape of layered deception, where personal ambition intersects with cosmic forces. The Essence Drought introduces a ticking clock — as steam technology expands, magical power diminishes, forcing adaptation or decline.
