[[Pantheon]] [[Legendary_Figures]] [[Religious_Practices]]


•	Origin: Gearspring was born from the intricate mechanisms of time itself, embodying order, justice, and precision. He is often depicted as a figure made of clockwork gears and cogs.

•	Role: As an arbiter of law and order, Gearspring enforces justice across Aethoria. He teaches that balance must be maintained in all things.

•	Impact: His doctrines guide legal systems and societal structures. Temples feature intricate clockwork mechanisms that symbolize his influence on time and justice.
