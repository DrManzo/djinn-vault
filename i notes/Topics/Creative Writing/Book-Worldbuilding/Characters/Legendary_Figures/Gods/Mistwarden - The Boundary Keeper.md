[[Pantheon]] [[Legendary_Figures]] [[Religious_Practices]]

•	Origin: Mistwarden arose from the mists that separate realms, embodying protection and vigilance. She is often depicted as a watchful figure cloaked in fog.

•	Role: As a defender of borders, Mistwarden ensures safety against external threats. She teaches her followers to remain vigilant against dangers beyond their realm.

•	Impact: Her influence is crucial in maintaining peace along Aethoria’s edges. Temples are often located at border crossings or high vantage points.
