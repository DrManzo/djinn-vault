[[Pantheon]] [[Religious_Practices]] [[Legendary_Figures]]

•	Origin: Oblivion emerged from the depths of loss and forgetfulness, embodying neutrality toward what is lost or forgotten. He is often depicted as a shadowy figure with an air of mystery.

•	Role: As custodian of all things lost, Oblivion neither protects nor attacks; he simply exists as a guardian of forgotten memories and relics.

•	Impact: His realm poses moral dilemmas for those seeking to reclaim what they have lost; he demands a toll for any item or memory retrieved.
