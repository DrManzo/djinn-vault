[[Pantheon]] [[Religious_Practices]] [[Legendary_Figures]]

•	Origin: Rustbane was born from ancient relics and forgotten traditions, embodying preservation and heritage. He is often depicted as an aged figure surrounded by scrolls and artifacts.

•	Role: As a protector of tradition, Rustbane safeguards ancient knowledge against decay and oblivion. He teaches his followers to honor their past.

•	Impact: His influence opposes unchecked progress, advocating for cultural heritage preservation. Temples often serve as museums for ancient artifacts.
