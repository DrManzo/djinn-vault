[[Pantheon]] [[Religious_Practices]] [[Legendary_Figures]]

•	Origin: Shadowmist arose from the darkness that envelops secrets and hidden truths. She is often depicted as a cloaked figure whose face is obscured by shadows.

•	Role: As the guardian of secrets and espionage, Shadowmist oversees those who operate in the shadows. She teaches her followers to navigate deception and intrigue.

•	Impact: Her influence shapes political landscapes through covert operations and clandestine dealings. Temples dedicated to her are hidden away, known only to those who seek her guidance.
