[[Pantheon]] [[Religious_Practices]]

•	Origin: Born from the molten core of the earth, Ironheart embodies strength, craftsmanship, and innovation. He is often depicted as a sturdy figure wielding a hammer that sparks with divine energy.	

•	Role: Ironheart merged magic with technology, inspiring artisans and inventors to create wondrous devices and structures. He emphasizes the importance of ingenuity and progress.

•	Impact: His teachings have led to significant advancements in industry and engineering. Temples near factories symbolize his connection to both magic and machinery.
