•	Beliefs: A fervent believer in the doctrines of Gearspring, Rustbane, and Mistwarden, Valen Gearhart is a staunch advocate for order, tradition, and protection. He interprets the gods’ teachings as mandates to purge any perceived threats to societal stability.

•	Methods: Valen employs rigorous investigations, public trials, and harsh punishments to enforce divine law. His actions are characterized by an unwavering commitment to what he perceives as divine justice, often leading to extreme measures.

•	Impact: His zealotry creates a climate of fear and oppression, stifling innovation and dissent. Valen’s actions lead to societal divisions, as those who oppose his methods are branded heretics and traitors.

•	Integration: Valen’s presence in the story can serve as a catalyst for conflict between traditionalists and progressives, forcing characters to navigate the moral complexities of justice and order.
