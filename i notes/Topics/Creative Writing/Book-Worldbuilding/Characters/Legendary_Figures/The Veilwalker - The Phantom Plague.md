[[Legendary_Figures]]


•	Concept: The Veilwalker is not a single person but a mantle assumed by members of the Thieves’ and Assassins’ Guilds during times of great need. This role is shrouded in secrecy, with only the guild leaders knowing its true nature.

•	Methods: The Veilwalker employs strategic sabotage, deception, and assassination to destabilize oppressive regimes or corrupt institutions. Each bearer of the mantle acts independently but follows a legacy of rebellion against tyranny.

•	Impact: The Veilwalker becomes a symbol of resistance and hope for the oppressed, while simultaneously instilling fear in those in power. The mystery surrounding their identity adds to their legend.

•	Integration: The Veilwalker can be used to explore themes of identity and legacy, as well as the tension between personal sacrifice and collective action.
