•	Impact: His actions destabilize regions, forcing unlikely alliances between factions to combat his threats. Morghast’s presence serves as a reminder of the dangers of unchecked ambition and the consequences of wielding forbidden power.
	
•	Integration: Sable Morghast can drive plotlines involving political intrigue and magical conflict, challenging characters to confront their own fears and limitations.
