## Basic Info
- **Full Name:** Master Thorne (first name unknown)
- **Age:** Sixties
- **Occupation:** Former royal assassin, current court advisor, secret mastermind
- **Essence Ability:** Adept-level Essence manipulation

## Physical Description
- **Appearance:** Outwardly appears as a kindly, somewhat eccentric scholar
- **Distinctive Features:** Capable of sudden, terrifying physical transformations when angered
- **Typical Attire:** Well-tailored suites, aristocratic style

## Personality
- **Traits:** 
  - Machiavellian
  - Manipulative
  - Cunning
  - Patient and instructive as a mentor
  - Capable of sudden bursts of anger and cruelty
- **Motivations:** 
  - Long-term plans for reshaping Aethoria's power structure
  - Hidden personal agenda possibly tied to past trauma
- **Fears/Weaknesses:** 
  - Vulnerability related to a lost love (Evelyn)
  - Potential for his carefully constructed plans to unravel

## Background
- **Origin:** Former royal assassin turned court advisor
- **Key Life Events:** 
  - Loss of someone named Evelyn (possibly romantic interest)
  - Transition from assassin to advisor and secret manipulator

## Relationships
- **Allies:** Members of the Shadow Council
- **Enemies:** Numerous, but kept in the dark about his true nature
- **Corvus Shadowblade:** Apprentice and unwitting pawn in his schemes

## Role in Story
- **Function:** 
  - Main antagonist
  - Mentor figure to Corvus
  - Mastermind behind major political events
- **Character Arc:** 
  - Gradual reveal of his true nature and plans
  - Potential for a tragic fall as his schemes come to light

## Abilities
- **Essence Manipulation:** Highly skilled, able to influence minds subtly
- **Combat Skills:** Formidable, with a terrifying capacity for violence
- **Political Acumen:** Expert manipulator of people and events
- **Shapeshifting:** Can transform into a monstrous form when enraged
