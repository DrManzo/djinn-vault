1. [[Aetheros - The Essence Born]]
	•	Worship: Revered by mages and those seeking magical knowledge, Aetheros’s temples are centers of learning and research.
	•	Influence: Advocates for responsible use of magic, shaping magical policies in Aethoria.
2.	[[Ironheart - The Forgemaster]]
	•	Worship: Worshipped by artisans and inventors, with temples near factories symbolizing the fusion of technology and magic.
	•	Influence: Drives technological advancements and industrial progress.
3.	[[Shadowmist - The Veiled One]]
	•	Worship: Revered by spies and those operating in shadows, with hidden temples.
	•	Influence: Involved in espionage and clandestine operations, influencing politics through covert means.
4.	[[Gearspring - The Clockwork Judge]]
	•	Worship: Worshipped by those valuing order and justice, with temples featuring intricate clockwork mechanisms.
	•	Influence: Advocates for law and order, involved in legal and judicial affairs.
5.	[[Embercrown -  The Radiant Sovereign]]
	•	Worship: Revered by rulers seeking power, with grand temples reflecting leadership majesty.
	•	Influence: Involved in politics and governance, emphasizing strong leadership.
6.	[[Rustbane - The Preserver]]
	•	Worship: Worshipped by those valuing tradition and preservation.
	•	Influence: Opposes unchecked progress, advocating for cultural heritage preservation.
7.	[[Mistwarden - The Boundary Keeper]]
	•	Worship: Revered by those guarding borders, with temples near Aethoria’s edges.
	•	Influence: Involved in defense and border control.
8.	[[Oblivion - The Keeper of Shadows]]
	•	Domain: Guards all things lost and forgotten, existing as a neutral entity.
	•	Role: Demands a toll for reclaiming lost items or memories, leading to moral dilemmas.
