Harvey's mind reeled as he delved deeper into the cosmic genesis, a tale that would haunt his dreams for years to come:

In the vast emptiness before time, Essence, a being of incomprehensible power, writhed in the infinite Void. Its existence was an agony of potential, a mighty yet utterly alone creature. In its isolation, it felt the crushing weight of nothingness surrounding it, a profound emptiness that threatened to swallow even Essence's immeasurable strength.

Driven by an instinct as old as its unfathomable existence, Essence began tearing itself apart. Each fragment of its being became a spark of creation, a desperate attempt to fill the Void that pressed in from all sides. The cosmos shuddered as Essence poured itself into this act of creation, its pain giving birth to the first whispers of reality.

From this torment, light emerged – brilliant, blinding, beautiful. It burst forth in radiance, each pulse a testament to Essence's sacrifice. The newborn light danced and swirled, painting the canvas of existence with its ethereal glow. It seemed the darkness had been banished for a moment, pushed back to the farthest reaches of reality.

Yet even as Essence marveled at its creation, a seed of dread took root. Though glorious, the light cast shadows where there had been only a void before. And in those shadows, something stirred – a presence as ancient and terrible as Essence itself.

Oblivion crouched at the edge of existence, a monstrous figure of absolute negation. Its form, if it could be called such, was a writhing mass of un-creation, a promise of endings yet to come. It watched the light with patient, hungry eyes, biding its time.

For an age, Essence basked in the glow of its creation while the Void sat in silent observation. But as eons passed, imperceptible at first, the light began to dim. Its brilliance faded by degrees so minute that only the ever-watchful Void could perceive the change. The cosmic dance of light and shadow took on a new, ominous rhythm.

Harvey's skin prickled as he recalled his grandfather's gravelly voice recounting the next part of the tale, each word etched into his memory with terrifying clarity:

"As the last ray of that first light guttered like a dying star's final gasp," the old man intoned, his eyes gleaming with otherworldly knowledge. The Void stirred from its vigil, hunger and cruelty incarnate. It slithered towards the fading remnants of creation, offering a bargain that dripped with malice—a single spark of creation in exchange for the promise of ultimate destruction."
The room had grown cold then, as if the air recoiled from the cosmic treachery being described. Years later, Harvy could still feel that chill, a bone-deep reminder of the universe's dark origins.

"That spark, born of cosmic deceit," his grandfather continued, voice dropping to a whisper that somehow filled the entire space, "merged with the dying light in an unholy union. Our world sprang from this tainted seed. But never forget – the Void's appetite is insatiable, and its patience lasts longer than time itself. The end will come again; when it does, it will be merciless and absolute."
As Harvy reflected on this cosmic genesis, he couldn't shake the feeling that he was part of something far greater and more terrifying than he could comprehend. The weight of this knowledge pressed down on him, a constant reminder that darkness always waited to reclaim what it had lost in the grand tapestry of existence.

This creation myth gave the universe's beginning a chilling immediacy.
