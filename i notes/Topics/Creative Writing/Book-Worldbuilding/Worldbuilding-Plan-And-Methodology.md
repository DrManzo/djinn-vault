# Worldbuilding Plan And Methodology

## Summary
A streamlined world-building plan for Aethoria combines the best elements of multiple approaches into a six-phase methodology: Core Concepts Development, Relationship Mapping, Character Integration, Historical Timeline, Economic and Military Structure, and World Bible Compilation. The plan emphasizes daily dedicated work, digital organization, and flexible iteration.

## Key Points
- Core Concepts Development focuses on setting, political structure, magic and technology, cultural elements, and societal structure with a benchmark of one-page summaries for each
- Relationship Mapping explores connections between political entities, magic and technology's impact on society, cultural interactions, and trade relations with a mind map benchmark
- Character Integration develops character profiles tied to the world including backgrounds, motivations, political and social structure placement, and relationships with magic and technology
- Historical Timeline creates a concise timeline of major events including the origin of Essence Manipulation, rise of the Shadow Council, key conflicts, and technological advancements
- Economic and Military Structure defines the guild system, major industries, banking houses, trade routes, military hierarchy, and special units with organizational chart benchmarks
- World Bible Compilation organizes all world-building information into a single, easily-referenced document
- Dedicate 30 minutes daily to world-building, focusing on one aspect at a time
- Use digital tools like Notion or OneNote to organize the World Bible for easy updating and cross-referencing
- Create simple checklists for each benchmark to track progress
- Make notes on areas needing further development while writing the story
- Regularly review the World Bible to ensure consistency in writing
- Use commute or downtime to brainstorm ideas, recording voice notes if possible
- Set weekly goals to complete one benchmark, adjusting as needed based on schedule
- The plan focuses on core concepts and key relationships to create a rich, cohesive world without getting overwhelmed by details
- Stay flexible and allow the world to evolve as the story develops

## Details
The methodology is designed for sustainable, incremental world-building that fits into a busy schedule. Rather than attempting to build everything at once, the plan breaks world-building into manageable phases with clear benchmarks. The Core Concepts phase establishes the foundation — what Aethoria is, who rules it, how magic works, and how society is structured. Relationship Mapping then connects these elements, revealing how politics affects magic, how economics shapes class, and how geography influences culture. Character Integration ensures that the world serves the story by tying characters directly into the world's structures. The Historical Timeline provides depth and context, explaining how Aethoria became what it is. Economic and Military Structure adds the practical systems that make the world function. Finally, the World Bible compiles everything into a reference document that grows and evolves with the story. The emphasis on daily 30-minute sessions and weekly benchmark goals makes the process sustainable without overwhelming the writer.
