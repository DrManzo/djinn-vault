# Aethoria Economic System

## Summary
Aethoria's economy is structured around a powerful guild system, influential banking houses, and major industries centered on Essence-infused technology and steam-powered machinery. The Guild of Artisans, Merchants Guild, and Essence Workers Guild control different economic sectors, while the Royal Bank of Ironhaven and the Sundered Isles Trading Company manage finance and international trade.

## Key Points
- The Artisans Guild includes sub-guilds for specific crafts such as blacksmithing, carpentry, and glassmaking, with rigorous apprenticeship programs progressing from apprentice to journeyman to master craftsman
- The Merchants Guild establishes trade routes with other regions including the Sundered Isles and Rustlands, sets market regulations to prevent monopolies, and negotiates trade agreements
- The Essence Workers Guild is divided into sub-guilds for different types of Essence manipulation including healing, enchanting, and elemental control, requiring licensing at different expertise levels
- The Royal Bank of Ironhaven functions as a central bank regulating currency, maintains economic stability, offers loans for business ventures, and provides secure storage for Essence-infused artifacts
- The Sundered Isles Trading Company facilitates international trade, specializes in exotic materials such as rare metals and magical plants, and offers maritime security services against piracy
- Essence-infused technology industry includes research centers developing new technologies, manufacturing facilities producing enchanted weapons and magical tools, and regulations ensuring safe use
- Steam-powered machinery industry encourages innovation in efficient steam engines and advanced clockwork mechanisms, with applications across manufacturing, transportation, and agriculture
- Exotic materials trade sources from the Sundered Isles and Misty Moorlands, with trade agreements and regulations ensuring ethical and sustainable practices
- Guild politics create conflicts and alliances that shape the economic landscape and provide plot opportunities
- Economic tensions between traditional Essence users and proponents of steam technology reflect broader societal changes
- Character involvement in the economic system — merchants navigating trade agreements, Essence workers facing ethical dilemmas — adds depth to world-building

## Details
The economic system of Aethoria is deeply intertwined with its magic system and political structure. The Guild of Artisans controls not just production but also the flow of knowledge — apprenticeship programs ensure that skills are passed down within controlled channels. The Essence Workers Guild's licensing system creates a bureaucracy around magic itself, turning a natural ability into a regulated profession. The Royal Bank of Ironhaven's role as central bank gives it enormous political leverage, as it can influence which industries grow and which decline. The Sundered Isles Trading Company's monopoly on exotic materials creates dependency relationships that can be exploited for political purposes. The tension between Essence-based industries and steam-powered industries mirrors the broader conflict between tradition and progress in Aethorian society.
