# Aethoria Societal Structure

## Summary
Aethoria features a multi-tiered class system with pronounced divisions between upper, middle, working, and lower classes, plus a growing shadowy underworld. Daily life, entertainment, customs, and access to resources vary dramatically across classes, with institutional classism perpetuating inequality and limiting social mobility.

## Key Points
- Upper Class enjoys wealth, power, and influence with high-end education, exclusive social clubs, political influence, and luxurious lifestyles
- Middle Class struggles to maintain a decent lifestyle, focusing on education and white-collar jobs while facing challenges achieving financial stability
- Working Class endures long hours in blue-collar jobs, limited education access, financial insecurity, and risk of unemployment with limited social mobility
- Lower Class faces harsh poverty, lack of education, low-paying jobs, reliance on welfare systems, and difficulty accessing basic necessities like healthcare and housing
- A shadowy underworld operates beneath the visible class structure, providing alternative economies and power networks
- Class affects every aspect of daily life including education, job opportunities, and social interactions
- Entertainment preferences differ by class: upper class attends exclusive events while working class prefers affordable activities like sports and local parks
- Unique customs vary by class: upper class maintains formal dining etiquette while working class gathers for community events and informal traditions
- Institutional classism perpetuates inequality by limiting access to quality healthcare, education, and legal services for lower classes
- Economic policies disproportionately affect different classes, such as tax laws benefiting the wealthy and labor laws impacting workers
- Characters from different classes illustrate class distinctions and their impact on individuals through daily struggles and aspirations
- Class conflicts serve as plot points, such as healthcare access struggles or relationship barriers across social lines
- Physical environments reflect class divisions: luxurious neighborhoods versus cramped living conditions
- Themes of inequality, social mobility, and class consciousness emerge through character perceptions of their place in society

## Details
The class system in Aethoria is not merely economic but cultural and institutional. Each class has distinct customs, entertainment, and social expectations that reinforce boundaries. The growing middle class represents a destabilizing force — educated enough to question the system but not powerful enough to change it. The shadowy underworld provides an alternative power structure for those excluded from legitimate society, creating opportunities for characters who operate outside the law. Class intersects with magical ability: the upper classes tend to have stronger magical abilities, while the lower classes may possess raw talent but lack access to training. This creates a self-reinforcing cycle where magical power and social power concentrate in the same hands.
